# The 2025 Peninsula Prize — Week 18 Laureate Ceremony

*Delivered from the Stockholm Concert Hall on the closing night of the season. Your Majesties, Your Royal Highnesses, Ladies and Gentlemen — please rise.*

---

It is my solemn honor, in accordance with the will of the League and the statutes of the Peninsula Academy, to convene the final ceremony of the 2025 season. Eighteen weeks of laborious research have brought us to this stage of laurel and gold. A reminder to our distinguished audience: these are the playoff weeks, and so the win-loss records you will hear read into the record are **frozen** at their Week 12 values — no diploma awarded tonight can amend a laureate's ledger. Tonight we honor points, and points alone. The banquet at City Hall follows. Let us begin.

## The Prize in Literature — awarded to Chris

The Academy awards the 2025 Prize in Literature, and with it the season's highest honor as the league's top cumulative laureate, to **Chris**, "for a body of work totaling **2239** cumulative points, and for a finale composed in the tightest of margins." Chris defeated **Sean, 79.5 to 78** — a suspense novel resolved in its final sentence by a margin of **1.5**. The prose was carried by **Alec Pierce (WR, 25)**, with supporting chapters from **TreVeyon Henderson (RB, 19)** and **Colston Loveland (TE, 14)**. This is what we in the committee call the Iron Grip: two straight victories (streak W2), a 10-2 record sealed, and the number-one podium claimed. Chris ascends the steps, bows to the King, and receives the medal. The citation notes it was very nearly a tragedy — but literature, like Chris, survives its closest calls.

## The Prize in Peace — awarded to Sean, in a losing cause

There is no medal for the vanquished, and yet the Academy cannot let this evening pass without recognizing the single most luminous individual achievement of the week. The Peace Prize — that most bittersweet of honors, so often given to those who fought hardest and won least — goes in spirit to **Rhamondre Stevenson (RB, 33)**, the week's top individual laureate, whose brilliance for Sean could not prevent the 79.5-78 defeat. Alongside him stood **Parker Washington (WR, 14)** and **Devin Singletary (RB, 10)**. A magnificent oration, delivered to a hall that ultimately voted the other way. History, we assure Sean, will be kinder than the scoreboard was.

## The Prize in Physics — awarded to Cal

The Academy awards the 2025 Prize in Physics to **Cal**, "for the discovery of raw force, and for producing the week's highest measured energy output: **108.5 points**." In a dismantling of **Pete, 108.5 to 85.67** — a margin of **22.83** — Cal demonstrated a trifecta of fundamental particles: **Trevor Lawrence (QB, 24)**, **Tyrone Tracy Jr. (RB, 20)**, and **Caleb Williams (QB, 18)**, with **Drake London (WR, 14)** completing the equation. This is the Closing Kick made manifest. On a week when Cal scored a mere **+0.14** above his own season average — proof this is simply who Cal *is* — the result vaulted him from fourth into **third place, 1950.5** cumulative. One asterisk in the peer review: **Keon Coleman (WR, 2)** underperformed, a cold spot in an otherwise brilliant field — but even a masterwork tolerates one flaw.

Pete does not leave empty-handed. **Matthew Stafford (QB, 29)** delivered the week's second-best individual result, joined by **Joe Burrow (QB, 21)** and **Ray Davis (RB, 20)** — three laureates over 20, and still the loss. Pete rises one spot to **fifth**, small consolation on an L3 skid.

## The Prize in Chemistry — awarded to Bruce

The Academy awards the 2025 Prize in Chemistry to **Bruce**, "for the reproducibility of his results — three consecutive victories, WLWWW — and for completing a season-long experiment upon **Aaron**." Bruce won **93 to 66.5**, a margin of **26.5**, and in doing so swept the entire Bruce-versus-Aaron series (Weeks 7, 15, and 18), a third straight double-digit synthesis. The reagents: **Riley Leonard (QB, 27)**, the week's third-highest individual score, catalyzed by twin fourteens from **Ja'Marr Chase (WR, 14)** and **Michael Wilson (WR, 14)**. Aaron, our 2-10 laureate of the Lost Season, closes on an L2 skid and anchors the table at **1331.5**. The committee extends its warmest sympathies and a very fine dinner.

## The Prize in Physiology — awarded to Dan

The Academy awards the 2025 Prize in Physiology or Medicine to **Dan**, "for the most decisive intervention of the week." Dan administered the largest margin on the docket — **80 to 43** over **Mike**, a gap of **37** — a diagnosis delivered without mercy. The treatment: **Zay Flowers (WR, 25)**, a defensive dose from the **Browns DST (16)**, and **Aaron Rodgers (QB, 14)**. Mike, meanwhile, submitted the week's lowest specimen (week rank 12 of 12) — his Feast-or-Famine condition entering its terminal, well-documented phase. **Jahmyr Gibbs (RB, 14)** and **Baker Mayfield (QB, 11)** were the only signs of life; **Kyle Monangai (RB, 2)** flatlined, one of our three official busts of the evening. Dan snaps a long famine of his own — W1, and his first win in five (LLLLW).

## The Prize in Economic Sciences — awarded to Eli and Mitch

The Academy this year splits the Prize in Economic Sciences between our two masters of the narrow margin, for their contributions to the theory of winning with as little as possible.

**Eli** is honored for defeating **Joe, 55.17 to 53**, a margin of **2.17**. The portfolio: **Tyler Shough (QB, 22)** — the week's seventh-best individual return — with dividends from **Kenneth Gainwell (RB, 11)** and **Amon-Ra St. Brown (WR, 9)**. And here the committee must note the great cautionary tale of 2025: **Joe**, our second-place laureate at **2065.5**, closed with a **53-point** dud — a stunning **-61.75** beneath his own average. The Late Fade, entered permanently into the record. What if Joe had merely been average? The finale, and perhaps the whole conversation about first place, reads differently. Instead: a distant, dignified silver.

**Mitch** shares the prize for edging **Matt, 56.67 to 50.5**, a margin of **6.17**, avenging two prior defeats to claim the last word in the series. **Lamar Jackson (QB, 21)** and **Puka Nacua (WR, 14)** funded the account. Matt, whose **Darius Slayton (WR, 2)** joins our bust registry, scored **-42.14** below his average and slips one rung to **sixth**.

## The Banquet — Final Standings, read into the record

And so the season's ledger closes, sealed with wax and gold: **Chris (10-2, 2239)** atop the hall, **Joe (10-2, 2065.5)** second, **Cal (7-5, 1950.5)** third, **Bruce (6-6, 1753)** fourth, **Pete (7-5, 1692.67)** risen to fifth, **Matt (8-4, 1667.5)** fallen to sixth. Below them the long table stretches — Sean, Dan, Mike, Mitch, Eli — down to **Aaron (2-10, 1331.5)** at the foot, where a fine meal awaits regardless. The league averaged **70.79** points on the night. The medals are struck. The season is complete.
