# The PFL Class of 2025 Reunion — Week 18, The Finale

*Sign the guest book. Grab a name tag. Yes, the little sticky kind. Punch bowl's by the trophy case, DJ's playing something from a season that already feels like a lifetime ago. Come in, come in — this is the last one, the finale, the reunion where the yearbook finally goes to print. Records are frozen now, folks, locked at their Week 12 selves like a photo on the mantel, so nobody's win-loss line changes tonight. But oh, do we have some catching up to do.*

---

**Chris! Get over here, valedictorian!** Ten-and-two, riding a two-game win streak, and — would you look at that — a league-high **2239** cumulative points that nobody's touching. You closed it out in the tightest handshake of the night: **79.5 to Sean's 78**, a margin of 1.5, the kind of squeaker that makes you loosen your tie. Alec Pierce showed up in his Sunday best with **25**, TreVeyon Henderson chipped **19**, Colston Loveland **14** — and it all held by a point and a half. Remember Week 5, when you took Sean 98-91? You just closed the season series with the exact same result, only closer, only meaner. Iron grip stays iron. Nobody pried the #1 sticker off your chest.

**And Sean — 3-and-9 Sean — you almost crashed the party.** Rhamondre Stevenson walked in with **33 points**, the single biggest individual number of the entire reunion, the guy everybody's whispering about by the coat check. Parker Washington added **14**, Devin Singletary **10**, Justin Jefferson **9**. And it all went in the "losing effort" scrapbook. Thirty-three points, best in the building, and you're still driving home wondering about a point and a half. That's the reunion story you'll be telling for years.

**Cal!** Look who came dressed to the nines. **108.5** — the week's best score, walking in first place on the door, **+0.14 over your own average** on the one night it mattered most. That's the *Closing Kick* they've been talking about all offseason: Trevor Lawrence **24**, Tyrone Tracy Jr. **20**, Caleb Williams **18**, Drake London **14**, Chris Rodriguez Jr. **11** — five guys in double digits, a whole clique of them. The finale had the last word.

On most nights 85.67 wins you a slow dance. Tonight it walked into Cal's buzzsaw and left on an L3 skid. You slid up to **5th (▲1)** in the cumulative yearbook anyway, so hold your head up. (Keon Coleman, Cal's own **2-point** wallflower, is a reminder that even the best-dressed table has one guy who won't leave the punch bowl.)

**Matt and Mitch, still bickering by the gym doors.** Matt swept the regular-season series — Week 8 (82-80.5) and Week 15 (108-77) — but the reunion gave Mitch the last word: **56.67 to 50.5**, margin 6.17. **Bruce!** Three straight wins, WLWWW, the *Late Surge* nobody wants to guard at the punch bowl. You put **93** on the board and sent Aaron home **66.5** — a 26.5-point beatdown — completing a clean sweep of the season series (Week 7, Week 15, and now Week 18, three straight double-digit wins over the same poor soul).
