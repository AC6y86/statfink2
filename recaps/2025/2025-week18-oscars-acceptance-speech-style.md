# The 2025 PFL Awards — Week 18: "And the Trophy Goes To…"

*Live from the Peninsula Football League, the orchestra warming up, the envelopes sealed. Ladies and gentlemen, this is the finale — the closing night of the season, where the last statuettes are handed out and the last speeches run long. Remember, the records are frozen, folks — they've been carved in marble since Week 12, so nobody's win-loss changes tonight. What's on the line is legacy, cumulative points, and the right to hold the gold above your head. Roll the teleprompter.*

## Best Picture in a Nail-Biter: **Chris 79.5, Sean 78**

*(A hush. The envelope opens.)* Chris steps to the podium clutching the statuette by a margin of **1.5**, and honestly, he looks like a man who wasn't sure his name was inside. "I'd like to thank the Academy," he says, "and I'd like to thank **Alec Pierce**, who caught **25** points like he'd rehearsed it for months." **TreVeyon Henderson** chipped in **19**, **Colston Loveland** added **14** — enough, just barely, against a co-star who nearly stole the whole show. Because Sean's **Rhamondre Stevenson** put up **33** — the single greatest individual performance of the entire evening — and it went home empty-handed. That's Hollywood. Chris rides a **W2** streak and his league-high **2239** cumulative points to the number-one podium. The orchestra tries to play him off. He keeps talking. He's earned it.

## Best Comeback in a Supporting Role: **Cal 108.5, Pete 85.67**

If there's a Lifetime Achievement montage tonight, cue it here — Cal delivered the **week's best score, 108.5**, and won going away by **22.83**. "This one's for the ensemble cast," Cal beams, and what an ensemble: **Trevor Lawrence 24**, **Tyrone Tracy Jr. 20**, **Caleb Williams 18**, **Drake London 14**, **Chris Rodriguez Jr. 11** — three separate players over 10 points, a genuine closing kick. Pete gave a game speech — **Matthew Stafford** was electric at **29**, **Joe Burrow** at **21**, **Ray Davis** at **20** — but three headliners couldn't outdraw Cal's whole studio. Pete exits on an **L3** skid, still gracious, still **85.67**.

Bruce takes the stage for the third straight ceremony — **W3**, the story arc of the back half — and completes the season sweep of Aaron by **26.5**. "I dreamed about this as a kid," Bruce says, and the dream had a leading man: **Riley Leonard** and his **27**-point performance, flanked by **Ja'Marr Chase 14**, **Michael Wilson 14**, and even the **Texans DST** stepping up for **8**. ## Best Blowout — The Landslide Award: **Dan 80, Mike 43**

Every ceremony has the runaway winner, and tonight it's Dan, taking the **biggest margin of the night, 37**. "I want to thank **Zay Flowers**," Dan says, holding up a **25**-point statuette, "and I want to thank the **Browns DST**, because a defense that drops **16** on you deserves its own category." **Aaron Rodgers** added **14**, **Jaydon Blue 11** — a deep, decorated cast. Poor Mike gave the shortest speech of the evening: **43** points, dead last in the week rankings (12th of 12), a **Jahmyr Gibbs 14** here, a **Baker Mayfield 11** there, and **Kyle Monangai** stranded on **2**. Feast or famine, the reviews said all year. Tonight the kitchen was closed.

## Best Original Screenplay, Photo-Finish: **Eli 55.17, Joe 53**

Now *this* is drama — the envelope trembling, the margin a razor-thin **2.17**. Eli, an underdog nobody predicted, wins Best Upset. "I'm not going to cry," Eli lies, "I'd like to thank **Tyler Shough**" — **22** points from a name half the room can't spell — "and **Kenneth Gainwell**, **11**, and **Wil Lutz**, **8.5**." Across the aisle sits Joe, the year's co-lead, the 10-2 man, delivering a **53** that was **-61.75 below his own average** — a stunning, script-flipping dud for a runner-up. **Jacoby Brissett** managed **16**, **Cade Otton 14**, and then the credits rolled early. Joe's late fade is now a documentary in three parts, and he keeps a distant second at **2065.5** cumulative despite the loss. The Academy consoles him. The gap to Chris is real.

## The Career-Achievement Spotlight: **Rhamondre Stevenson, 33**

We pause the ceremony for the performance of the night. Sean's **Rhamondre Stevenson** poured out **33** points — the highest individual total anyone posted, more than **Matthew Stafford's 29**, more than **Riley Leonard's 27**. Sometimes the best performance isn't in the winning picture — **Parker Washington** added **14**, **Devin Singletary 10**, and it still fell one field goal short. Roll the tears.
