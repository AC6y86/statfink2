# The State of the League: A Week 18 Address to the Peninsula Football League

*Delivered to a joint session of all twelve owners, in the final hour of the 2025 season.*

---

Madam Speaker. Mr. Vice President. Members of this chamber. My fellow owners of the Peninsula Football League.

I come before you tonight, at the close of Week 18, the last night of our long season, to report on the condition of our union. And I can tell you, without fear of contradiction from either aisle: **the state of our league is settled, the records are frozen, and the gavel has come down.**

Let me be clear about the ground rules of this address. We are deep in the playoff weeks now. The won-lost ledgers you carried into Week 12 are sealed in the marble — no game tonight moved a single record. What moved tonight was *points*, and pride, and position on the final scroll. So let us proceed to the business of the people.

## To the Majority Leader

I want to begin by recognizing the gentleman who holds this chamber's highest seat. Chris — you came in on a two-game winning streak, and tonight you extended it to a third, but oh, did you make us sweat for it. **Chris 79.5, Sean 78.** A margin of one and one-half points. The narrowest victory of the evening.

Let the record show it was **Alec Pierce and his 25 points** who carried the flag when it mattered, with TreVeyon Henderson adding 19 and Colston Loveland chipping in 14. A word of caution to my friend across the aisle: 79.5 was *44.89 below your own season average.* You governed tonight not with a mandate but with a prayer, and the prayer was answered. You finish first on the cumulative ledger — **2239 points, the highest in the land.** The union recognizes its leader.

## To the loyal opposition

And to Sean — sir, you lost, but you sent the single greatest patriot of the night to the floor. **Rhamondre Stevenson: 33 points.** The top individual performance in all twelve districts, and he delivered it in a *losing* cause. Parker Washington added 14, Devin Singletary 10. History will note the tragedy: 78 points, more than enough to win most weeks, and it fell one and a half short. That, colleagues, is the cruelty of the arena.

## The second seat, and the peaceful transfer of near-power

Joe. My distinguished second-ranked colleague. I must be honest with this body, because the state of the union demands candor. You entered tonight riding a proud 10-2 banner, and tonight you posted **53 points — 61.75 below your own average**, the steepest collapse anyone suffered. Jacoby Brissett gave you 16, Cade Otton 14, and then the well ran dry.

And so you fell — **Eli 55.17, Joe 53** — by two and seventeen hundredths. Eli's Tyler Shough answered the call with 22, Kenneth Gainwell added 11. You hold your second seat on the final standings, sir, 2065.5 points, but the late fade is real, and this chamber has noticed. A 10-2 record cannot fully hide a finish like this one.

## The closing kick — a rising star addresses the gallery

Now I ask this body to turn its eyes to the gentleman in the third seat, because if there was a State of the Union guest tonight seated beside the First Lady, it was **Cal.**

**Cal 108.5, Pete 85.67.** A 22.83-point statement — the highest score in the entire union this week, and the *only* owner tonight to finish above his own average, by a modest but proud +0.14. Trevor Lawrence, 24. Tyrone Tracy Jr., 20. Caleb Williams, 18. Drake London, 14. A whole caucus of double-digit contributors. With this closing kick you vaulted from fourth into **third place, 1950.5 cumulative.** Ladies and gentlemen, please rise and recognize the finest single night of the week.

To Pete, I say this: 85.67 is no disgrace — the third-best score of the night — and Matthew Stafford's 29, Joe Burrow's 21, and Ray Davis's 20 formed an honorable delegation. But you ran into a buzz saw, and your streak now reads L3. Some nights the other side simply governs better.

## The state of the surge

Bruce — three straight victories now, and tonight the sweep was completed. **Bruce 93, Aaron 66.5**, a 26.5-point margin, and with it Bruce closes the book on Aaron for a third consecutive double-digit beating this season. Riley Leonard was your engine at **27 points**, with Ja'Marr Chase and Michael Wilson each adding 14 and the Texans DST kicking in 8. This is a caucus with momentum, WLWWW over five weeks. The surge is real, and it is bipartisan-approved.

To Aaron, anchor of our standings at 2-10 and 1331.5 points — I do not forget the districts that struggled this year. 66.5 tonight, Jaxson Dart's 16 and Tank Bigsby's 14 notwithstanding, was not enough. The union grieves with your constituents and looks to a brighter offseason.

## The largest margin, and a note on volatility

The widest gulf of the night belonged to Dan. **Dan 80, Mike 43** — a 37-point landslide, the blowout of the week. **Zay Flowers, 25 points**, and here is the line that will echo through the offseason: the **Browns DST scored 16** — a defense outscoring nearly every skill player on Mike's roster. Aaron Rodgers added 14, Jaydon Blue 11.

And Mike — the feast-or-famine pattern this chamber knows all too well returned once more. **43 points, dead last, week rank 12 of 12**, 40.83 below your average. Jahmyr Gibbs's 14 stood alone. It is a familiar tale from a familiar district.

## The middle chamber

I would be derelict not to address the tightest ideological battle of the floor. **Mitch 56.67, Matt 50.5** — Mitch by 6.17. Matt had won both prior meetings this season, but Mitch got the last word, and Lamar Jackson's 21 and Puka Nacua's 14 got him there. Matt, at 50.5 — 42.14 below your average — this was a rough close to an 8-4 season; Bryce Young's 19 and C.J. Stroud's 13 could not lift you. You slip one spot to sixth on the final ledger.

## A word on the state of our busts

No address is complete without an honest accounting of the underperformers. Let the record reflect three skill players who reported for duty and produced barely a whisper: **Keon Coleman, 2 points for Cal** (a rare blemish on the night's best team), **Kyle Monangai, 2 for Mike**, and **Darius Slayton, 2 for Matt.** We thank them for their service. We ask for more.

## The state of our health — the Injury Report

Before I yield the floor, the chamber physician's report.

> CAVEAT: Injury designations reflect CURRENT status at generation time, not necessarily during Week 18 games.

- **Pete:** Brandon Aiyuk (WR, SF) — Out. Per a June 24 report, in a video posted to Instagram, Aiyuk (knee) said that if the 49ers released him, he'd sign with the Commanders "tomorrow," per ESPN.com.

## In closing

So there it stands, my fellow owners. The 2025 season is gaveled shut. Chris governs from the summit at 2239. Joe holds the second seat at 2065.5. Cal, on the strength of a 108.5-point closing kick, claims the third. The records are frozen, the votes are counted, and the peaceful transfer of the offseason begins tonight.

The state of our league is strong. God bless the Peninsula Football League, and God bless the twelve who play it. Thank you, good night, and see you in the spring.
