# Fear and Loathing in Week 2: A Savage Journey to the Heart of the Peninsula Football League

We were somewhere around the waiver wire on the edge of Sunday when the box scores began to take hold. I remember saying something like "I feel a bit lightheaded, maybe you should score the Chargers defense —" and suddenly there was a terrible roar all around us and the sky was full of what looked like quarterbacks, all of them throwing for twenty-plus, swooping and screeching and diving around the car, which was doing about 100 through the standings with the top down and no goddamn brakes at all.

Twelve men. Six games. A league average of **96.54 points** — which is the statistical equivalent of a lukewarm beer at a funeral — and four of the six matchups decided by single digits. Nobody escaped clean. Let me explain.

---

## MILE 1: AARON AND THE BUFFALO MADMAN

There is a special kind of terror reserved for the man who must line up across from **Josh Allen** in September. Allen went for **40 points** — the top performance in the entire league, a second consecutive week of pure unregulated chemical violence after his 38 in the opener — and Aaron rode him straight through Bruce's living room wall. **Aaron 146, Bruce 118.** Margin: 28.

And understand: Bruce did *nothing wrong*. Bruce put up **118**, the second-best score of the week, **+13.5 over his own average**, with **Davante Adams at 28** and **Tyler Shough at 22** and **Deshaun Watson at 16** and eight different men contributing something. In any other week, in any other universe, Bruce wins going away. Instead he caught the buzzsaw. Aaron added **Amon-Ra St. Brown at 25** and **Cam Ward at 22**, a quarterback stack that has no business working and worked anyway. Aaron's **146** was **+6 vs his own average**, which tells you the horrifying part: this is just *what he does now*. He climbed **▲2 to rank 1** with **280 cumulative points**, the only man in the league past 280. Bruce, for his trouble, rocketed **▲5 to fifth on 209** and fell to **1-1**, streak L1. He bought the ticket. He took the ride. The ride was terrible.

## MILE 2: THE NIGHTMARE ON THE LOW END — DAN 76.5, MIKE 73.67

Here is where it got genuinely ugly. Two men crawling across the desert floor toward a water tower that turned out to be a hallucination. **Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67. Margin: 2.83.**

Dan — the man who dropped a league-high **168 in week 1** — came back this week and posted **76.5**, a collapse of **-45.75 against his own average**, the single most catastrophic negative delta in the league. The 2022 nostalgia act finally started coughing blood: **Travis Kelce 17**, **Stefon Diggs 16**, **Aaron Rodgers 6**. A whole aging stack held together with athletic tape and a prescription pad. Eleventh-best score of the week. And he *won*, because Mike put up **73.67** — dead last, twelfth, **-23.17 vs his own average** — on the back of **DeVonta Smith 17**, **Joe Burrow 16**, and a **New England Patriots Defense at 9.67** that was, no exaggeration, Mike's third-best asset on the afternoon. **Justin Jefferson scored 3.** Let that sit in your bloodstream a while. Dan holds serve at **2-0** but slid **▼1 to second, 244.5 cumulative**. Mike fell **▼4 to ninth**, **0-2**, streak L2, **193.67**. As your attorney, I advise Mike to burn it down to the frame and start over.

## MILE 3: MATT MUGS THE WINLESS — 96.67 OVER MITCH'S 76.5

The only blowout that wasn't Aaron's. **Matt 96.67, Mitch 76.5, margin 20.17.** Matt still owns **zero tight ends**, two full weeks into this thing, a roster built by a man who looked at an entire position group and said *no thank you, I'll take my chances in the desert*. It keeps working. **Bryce Young 24**, **Jalen Hurts 19**, **Denzel Boston 14**, and the **Carolina Panthers Defense for 9.67**. Matt's **96.67** was actually **-16.92 vs his own average** and he still won by twenty, which tells you everything you need to know about Mitch's week: **76.5**, **-3.5 vs average**, tenth-best, a lineup where **Chase McLaughlin's 8.5 kicking points** finished fourth on the team behind **Kyren Williams 14**, **Lamar Jackson 11**, and **Sam LaPorta 11**. Mitch is **0-2**, **▼1 to twelfth, 160 cumulative** — last in the league and sinking. Matt holds at **rank 4, 2-0, 227.17**, unmoved, unbothered, unbloodied, and still without a tight end.

## MILE 4: JOE 95, CHRIS 88 — AND THE BUST OF THE WEEK

Chris got **32 points from Jared Goff**, the third-best performance in the entire league, and lost anyway. That is the PFL in one sentence. **Joe 95, Chris 88, margin 7.**

Joe's **95** was **-21.25 off his own average** and came in only sixth-best on the week — **Patrick Mahomes 27**, **Jonathan Taylor 22**, **James Cook III 17** — which is to say Joe won this thing with the headlights half off, one hand on the wheel. Chris had Goff and **Christian McCaffrey at 16** and then the doors came off the rental car: five tight ends on the roster and the three that showed up went **McBride 8, Warren 8, Fannin 3**. And then there was **Travis Etienne Jr. — 2 points**, the lone officially registered Bust of Week 2, a running back who arrived, drew breath, and contributed roughly the fantasy value of a hotel ashtray. The gap was 7. Etienne gave him 2. Find one warm body anywhere on the Peninsula with a double-digit afternoon in him and Chris is standing over Joe's body instead of the reverse. He didn't find one. Joe climbs to **1-1** and sits **third at 232.5** (**▼1**); Chris drops to **1-1**, **▼1 to seventh, 199.5**.

## MILE 5: THE 2.67 — PETE SURVIVES ELI

The tightest margin of the week, and the cruelest. **Pete 90.67, Eli 88.** Two point six seven.

Pete's entire existence this week was **Jaxon Smith-Njigba: 36 points**, second only to Allen league-wide, plus **Brock Purdy at 27**. After those two? **C.J. Stroud 12**, **Rashee Rice 6**, **Andy Borregales 5**, **Drake London 3**, and a **Minnesota Vikings Defense contributing 1.67** — and in a game decided by 2.67, even that fractional garbage was doing load-bearing work. Best receiver room in the league, no backfield to speak of, a C+ draft grade, and a **2-0** record. He stayed put at **rank 8, 195.17 cumulative**, which is the strangest unbeaten résumé I have ever seen through a windshield.

Eli: **88**, **-1.75 vs his own average**, **Dak Prescott 29**, **Omarion Hampton 14**, **TreVeyon Henderson 14**, **George Kittle 14**. Nothing broken. Nothing stupid. Just 2.67 short, **0-2**, streak L2, **▼1 to tenth on 179.5**. Eli bet on 2028 and 2028 is currently in a hospital gown asking for a lawyer. More on that below.

## MILE 6: CAL 109.5, SEAN 100 — A-GRADES IN THE DITCH

The two best draft classes in the league, per the grades — **Cal A, Sean A-** — met out past the county line and only one crawled back. **Cal 109.5, Sean 100, margin 9.5.** Cal's **+25.75 over his own average** was the biggest positive swing anybody posted: **CeeDee Lamb 28**, **Ja'Marr Chase 22**, **Jake Ferguson 16**. Sean answered with **Matthew Stafford 32** and **Kirk Cousins 24** — that quarterback room "held together with tape and prayer" put up 56 points by itself — plus **Jahmyr Gibbs 14**, **Derrick Henry 11**, **Chuba Hubbard 11**. Sean scored **100 points and lost**. Again. He is **0-2**, streak L2, and here is the knife: he sits **sixth at 209 cumulative**, the exact same total as 1-1 Bruce. Same points. Different universe. Cal climbs to **1-1**, **▲1 to eleventh on 167.5**, still the lowest cumulative total of anyone in this league holding a win.

## THE STANDINGS, AS SEEN THROUGH THE WINDSHIELD

Four unbeaten (**Aaron, Dan, Matt, Pete**), four winless (**Sean, Mike, Eli, Mitch**), four at 1-1 (**Joe, Bruce, Chris, Cal**). A clean 4-4-4 split, like the deck was stacked by a dealer with a grudge. Aaron's **280** towers over Dan's **244.5**; the run from first to last — **Mitch at 160** — is 120 points of open daylight. And the draft grades? **C+ Dan and C+ Pete are 2-0. A- Sean and B+ Eli are 0-2.** The scouts are in a ditch outside Barstow with their notebooks on fire.

There he goes. One of God's own prototypes. Some kind of high-powered mutant never even considered for mass production. Too weird to live, and too rare to die. That's **Josh Allen, 40 points**, week after week, and the rest of us just trying to keep the car on the road.
