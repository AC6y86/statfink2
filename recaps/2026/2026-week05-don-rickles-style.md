# The Merchant of Venom Plays the PFL: Week 5, Live from the Lounge

*(A spotlight hits a small stage. A bald man in a tuxedo walks out, glares at the crowd and points.)*

Sit down, sit down. Look at this crowd. Twelve owners, and not a working brain in the bunch. I've seen smarter lineups at the DMV. Don't look at me like that, I'm here to help you. I love every one of you hockey pucks. Somebody has to.

Week 5 is in the books, and the league averaged 105.25 points. Some of you cleared it. The rest of you were standing around like Frank waiting on a bar tab. Let's go table by table.

## Table One: Chris 147, Aaron 109

**Drake Maye threw up 40 points.** That's the best individual line in the whole league this week. Then **Jared Goff** drops **32** on top of it. Two quarterbacks going off, and Chris sits there like he planned it. He didn't plan it, folks. This is the guy who loaded up on tight ends like they were giving them away at Costco. **Tyler Warren 14, Trey McBride 11, Kyle Pitts Sr. 8.** It's not a roster, it's a tight end museum. And still, God bless him, it worked. **Christian McCaffrey** chipped in **11** and the **49ers Defense** gave him **8**.

He's still **2-3**, though, and seventh in points at **534.5**. Seventh! You won the week by a mile and moved up one spot. That's like winning the lottery and finding out it pays in Canadian Tire money.

**Aaron.** Aaron, my friend. Don't get up, you've had a rough week. You actually got real production: **Jaylen Warren 31, Josh Allen 30.** Two guys in the league's top six! And you still lost by **38**, dummy. Why? **Amon-Ra St. Brown: 3 points.** Three! I've seen more action at a retirement home pinochle game.

And here comes **the bust of the week**: **Dalton Kincaid, TE, 2 points.** Two points. Aaron, you'd have gotten more out of that roster spot with a folding chair in it. At least the chair shows up.

Aaron's **109** was **-8.9 under his average**, he's on an **L2** after starting **WWW**, and he slid **▼1 to third** at **589.5**. Still **3-2**. Still my guy. I'm kidding, I love you. Fix the tight end.
