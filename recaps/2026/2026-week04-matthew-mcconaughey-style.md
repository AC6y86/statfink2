# Alright, Alright, Alright: Greenlights, Week 4

*A PFL 2026 recap, told slow from the driver's seat of a Lincoln with the windows down.*

---

Alright, alright, alright.

You ever notice how a football week is just a long drive at dusk? You got your hand out the window, feeling the air, and some guys are catching greenlights all the way down the boulevard while other guys hit red after red after red and wonder what they did to the universe. That's Week 4 in the Peninsula Football League. Same road for all twelve of us. League average this week was **105.92 points**. Some folks blew right through that. Some folks stalled out at the curb.

My daddy used to say you don't get to choose the lights. You choose how you drive through 'em. So let's ride.

---

## Chapter One: The Greenlight Week (Joe 148.5, Sean 136)

This was the big one, man. The highest-scoring game on the board. Two teams that both got an A-minus on draft day, and they both drove like it.

**Joe put up 148.5**, the best number in the whole league, and that's **+28.5 over his own average**. Look at the ground game. **Brian Robinson Jr. ran for 27**, **Jonathan Taylor added 22**, **James Cook III chipped in 14**, and **Quinshon Judkins had 11**. That's a backfield that just keeps livin'. Then **Romeo Doubs went for 19**, and Joe had two quarterbacks pulling the same wagon, **Patrick Mahomes 16** and **Jordan Love 16**. Nothing flashy up top. Just green, green, green, all the way down Main Street.

Now here's where it gets philosophical. **Sean scored 136.** That's **+14.5 over his own average**, third-best in the league this week. **Chuba Hubbard 25**, **Kirk Cousins 24**, **Alvin Kamara 16**, **Brock Bowers 14**, **Derrick Henry 11**. Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league, and the running backs paid out. And he *still lost*, by **12.5**.

Think about that. 136 would have beaten every other team on the board this week except Joe's 148.5 and Matt's 137. Sean drew the one guy on the one night who was driving faster. That's not a red light, my friend. That's a red light that turned on *while you were already in the intersection.*

And here's the cosmic joke. With that loss, **Sean jumped two spots to first in cumulative points at 486**, while sitting at **1-3**. Most points in the league, three losses. Leading the race in miles driven, stuck in the standings parking lot. Rust Cohle would tell you time is a flat circle. Sean's living it: score big, lose, repeat.

Joe, meanwhile, moved **up two spots to third at 480**, now **2-2** on a **W1** streak, last five reading **LWLW**. He's been flipping a coin all season. This week it landed heads with a thump. *Mmm-hmm. Mmm-hmm.*

---

## Chapter Two: The Week's Brightest Star in a Dark Room

Every week there's one man who plays the best football on the field and goes home with nothing. This week that man was **Drake Maye, 29 points**, the **number one individual performance in the league.** And it went down in a loss.

That's the real Hollywood ending, the one the studio cuts. More on that in a minute.

---

## Chapter Three: The Long Red Light (Matt 137, Cal 73.5)

Some losses hurt. Some just kinda roll over you like weather.

**Matt beat Cal 137 to 73.5.** That's a **63.5-point margin**, the biggest blowout of the week, and nobody else came close. Matt's still got **zero tight ends** on the roster, man. Never fixed it. And it didn't matter one bit, because **Tetairoa McMillan caught fire for 28**, tied for the second-best individual score in the league, and **Nico Collins added 25**. **Bryce Young threw for 22**, **Ollie Gordon II ran for 17**, **Justin Herbert gave 11**, and **Jake Bates kicked in 9**.

That's **137**, second-best this week, **+29.71 over his own average**. Matt climbed **three spots to sixth at 429.17**, and he's **3-1** with a **W1** streak. It's a rebound. Sometimes you gotta lose a step to find your stride.

And Cal. Cal got an **A** on draft day, the top grade in the league. Now he's **twelfth in cumulative points at 334.5**, **down two spots**, **1-3**, on an **L2** streak. **CeeDee Lamb did his part with 20**. After that it went **Carnell Tate 9**, **Tyler Allgeier 8**, **Ashton Jeanty 6**, **Kyler Murray 6**. Total: **73.5**, eleventh in the league, **-10.13 under his own average**.

The draft grade is the poster. The season is the movie. Cal's still waiting for the movie to start.

---

## Chapter Four: Two C-Pluses Who Won't Stop (Dan 113, Aaron 93.5; Pete 95.5, Mike 64)

Here's the thing nobody saw coming in August. The draft grader handed out a couple of **C-pluses**, to Dan and to Pete, and those are the only two teams in this league that haven't lost. Both **4-0**. Both on a **W4** streak. Both with last five reading **WWWW**.

**Dan beat Aaron 113 to 93.5**, a **19.5-point** win. Dan bet on 2022 and 2022 keeps answering the phone. **Kenneth Walker III ran for 28**, **Javonte Williams for 27**, and **Aaron Rodgers threw for 26**. Three of the top seven performances in the league, all on one roster. Add **Michael Penix Jr. 11**, **Deebo Samuel Sr. 8**, **Cairo Santos 7** and **Khalil Shakir 6**, and you get 113. That's actually **-3 under Dan's own average**, and he still won comfortably. That's the thing about the C-plus guys. They don't need their best stuff.

Aaron had a tough night. **Josh Allen gave him 22**, **Malik Nabers 17**, **Cam Ward 13**. But **Amon-Ra St. Brown managed just 6** and **Chase Brown only 3**, and Aaron finished at **93.5**, which is **-26.63 under his own average**. He slid **one spot to second at 480.5**, now **3-1** with an **L1**. Still a heck of a season. Just a yellow light.

Over on the other side of town, **Pete beat Mike 95.5 to 64**, a **31.5-point** margin. And Pete did it with only the **seventh-best score in the league**, **-7.67 under his own average**. Best receiver room in the league, no backfield to speak of, and it showed. **C.J. Stroud 22**, **Zay Flowers 17**, **Brock Purdy 16**, **Rhamondre Stevenson 10**, and two defenses, the **Vikings and the Bears, 5 apiece**.

And here's our **bust of the week**: **Najee Harris, 2 points.** Two. That's barely enough gas to back out of the driveway. Pete won anyway. That's the C-plus magic.

Mike, though. Brother. **Joe Burrow threw for 28**, tied for the second-best individual score in the league, and Mike still scored **64**, the **league low**, **-26.42 under his own average**. After Burrow it went **Jacoby Brissett 10**, **Tucker Kraft 8**, **Matthew Golden 8**, **Cameron Dicker 7**, **D'Andre Swift 3**. Mike dropped **two spots to tenth at 361.67**, **1-3**. Pete dropped one spot to **seventh at 412.67** despite the win, because that's how cumulative points work. You can win the race and still lose a little ground on the odometer.

---

## Chapter Five: First Light (Eli 128, Bruce 92.5)

Now this one I love. This one's about patience.

Eli started **0-3**. Eli bet on 2028, building for the hero who's always ten years away. And this week, 2028 showed up early. **Eli beat Bruce 128 to 92.5**, a **35.5-point** win, and that **128 was +33 over his own average**, the biggest jump of anybody in the league.

**Kyle Monangai ran for 25**. **Jameis Winston threw for 24**, and you know Jameis, man. Ride the wave. **Dak Prescott 17**, **Puka Nacua 17**, **George Kittle 11**, **Will Reichard 10**. Eli climbed **three spots to ninth at 380**. Still **1-3**, but the streak now says **W1**, and the last five read **LLLW**. That last letter is a greenlight, baby.

Bruce had two backs carrying him. **Bijan Robinson went for 25**, **Emanuel Wilson for 22**, and **Tyler Shough and Deshaun Watson put up 14 each**. After that it thinned out: **Aaron Jones Sr. 6**, and **Jeremiyah Love, Colston Loveland and KC Concepcion at 3 each**. Bruce finished at **92.5**, **-17.5 under his own average**, and slid **one spot to fifth at 440**. **2-2**, **L1**.

---

## Chapter Six: The Hollywood Ending They Cut (Mitch 105, Chris 84.5)

Remember Drake Maye's 29? Here's the rest of that story.

**Mitch beat Chris 105 to 84.5**, a **20.5-point** win, and Mitch finally got off the schneid. **Kyren Williams ran for 25**, **Lamar Jackson threw for 16**, **Sam LaPorta caught 14**, **Tee Higgins 12**, **Trevor Lawrence 11**, and the **New York Giants defense chipped in 8**. That's **105**, **+15.13 over his own average**. Mitch stays **eleventh at 359.5**, **1-3**, but with a **W1** and a pulse.

Chris got the **top individual score in the league, Drake Maye 29**, plus **Jared Goff at 20**. That's 49 points from two quarterbacks. Then **Christian McCaffrey 11**, **Harold Fannin Jr. 8**, **Ka'imi Fairbairn 7.5**, **Michael Wilson 6**, **Cam Skattebo 3**. And that's where the road ran out. **84.5** total, **-12.38 under his own average**. A roster with five tight ends and two receivers just doesn't have enough wheels. Chris slipped **one spot to eighth at 387.5**, **1-3**, on an **L3** streak, last five reading **WLLL**.

The D-plus draft grade keeps following him around like a shadow at noon.

---

## Epilogue: Where the Road Goes From Here

No game came down to the wire this week. No margin under 10, and the tightest finish was Joe over Sean by **12.5**. The heartbreak was Sean's 136 losing anyway, and the blowout was Matt by 63.5.

The top of the table is a traffic jam: **Sean 486**, **Aaron 480.5**, **Joe 480**, then **Dan 464**. The two unbeatens, Dan and Pete, sit **fourth and seventh** in points. Sean leads the league and has won once. Time is a flat circle, man. The standings and the scoreboard are telling two different stories, and only one of them gets you to the playoffs.

So here's what I'll leave you with. If you got the greenlight this week, enjoy it, but don't get comfortable. If you got the red, roll the window down. Feel the air. The light always changes.

Just keep livin'.
