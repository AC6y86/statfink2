# PFL Week 3: Postgame Press Conference, Transcript
### "We're On To Week 4."

*[The coach walks to the podium in a gray hoodie with the sleeves cut off. He looks at the stat sheet, then at the room, then back at the stat sheet. Long pause.]*

**OPENING STATEMENT:** Okay. Credit to everybody. Six games, all of them played. Some teams did their jobs and some teams didn't. Sean did his job. Bruce did his job. Matt did not do his job. That's the week. We'll look at the film. Questions.

---

**Q: Coach, let's start with Sean. 141 points, league high, first win of the year over Eli, 141 to 72.5. What did you see?**

We saw a team that ran the football. Jahmyr Gibbs, 33 points. Top performance in the league this week. You don't see that too often. He's a tough guy to tackle, he's got good vision, he finishes runs. Derrick Henry, 22. Two backs doing their jobs. Everybody was saying you can't bet on running backs in a quarterback league. Well, look, I don't know what everybody was saying. I don't read that stuff. But he ran it and it worked.

Then you got the quarterbacks. Matthew Stafford 22, Kirk Cousins 21. Brock Bowers at tight end, 17. Tampa Bay defense got him 5. That's complementary football. Everybody contributed. Sean was 0-2, now he's 1-2, 141 points, that's +24.33 against his own average. Moved up three spots to third in cumulative points, 350. That's one week. One week. It doesn't mean anything if you don't do it again.

**Q: And Eli, on the other side?**

*[Pause.]* Tough day. 72.5. George Kittle, 22, he played hard, Dak Prescott 16. After that, Jaylen Waddle, 2 points. That's a bust. That's a receiver in the scoring lineup giving you 2 points. Omarion Hampton, 3. You can't win like that against anybody, and you certainly can't win like that against a team scoring 141. 68.5-point margin, biggest of the week. Eli's 0-3, streak is L3, fell two spots to twelfth. Dead last in cumulative points at 252. The backfield's been hit hard. Injuries are part of the game. Nobody cares. Next man up.

**Q: The draft recap gave Eli a B+. Does a grade matter at 0-3?**

*[Long stare.]* The grade's not playing. The players are playing.

---

**Q: Bruce put up 138.5 and beat Cal 138.5 to 93.5. Talk about Bruce's quarterbacks.**

Tyler Shough, 29. Bo Nix, 28. And then Bijan Robinson, 28. Three guys at 28-plus. That's execution. That's what we talk about all the time — do your job, and they did their jobs. Garrett Wilson 17, Jeremiyah Love 14. Chris Boswell, 7.5 in the kicking game. Kicking game matters. It always matters. Bruce is 2-1, second-best score of the week, +22.67 against his average, moved up one spot to fourth at 347.5.

**Q: Bruce got a C+ in the draft.**

Yeah, I heard. Look, I've been around a lot of drafts. We'll see what the grades look like in December.

**Q: Cal was the draft's only A. He's 1-2.**

Cal scored 93.5. That's +6.5 against his own average, so he's not playing bad. He moved up one to tenth. Ja'Marr Chase 14, Jordan Addison 14, CeeDee Lamb 11, Washington defense with 8. Those are good players. But Kyler Murray gave him 5 at quarterback. In this league, you get 5 out of a quarterback slot, it's hard. Ashton Jeanty 3, Carnell Tate 3. He ran into a buzzsaw. It is what it is.

---

**Q: Coach, the closest game of the season. Mike 104, Chris 103.5. Half a point.**

Half a point. *[Pause.]* That's situational football. Every play matters. Every kick matters. Harrison Mevis kicked for 9 points for Mike. Chris's kicker, Evan McPherson, 5.5. You want to know where the game was? Start there. People don't want to talk about the kicking game, and then the game is decided by half a point.

Mike's quarterbacks carried him. Jacoby Brissett 27, seventh-best performance in the league. Geno Smith 26, eighth-best. Matthew Golden, 17. Mike's got Baker Mayfield and De'Von Achane on the injury report and he's still finding a way. That's a mentally tough team. 1-2 now, first win, up one spot to eighth.

**Q: And Chris?**

Chris did a lot of good things. Jared Goff 19, Harold Fannin Jr. 19 at tight end, Christian McCaffrey 14, Michael Wilson 14, Denver defense 8. Scored 103.5, that's +2.5 on his average. But Malik Willis, 9 at quarterback. Trey McBride 6, Travis Etienne Jr. 3, Cam Skattebo 3, Tyler Warren 3. Somewhere in there, one of those guys finds a little more and it's a different conversation. That's football. Chris is 1-2, streak's L2, stays seventh. Got a D+ in the draft and loses by the smallest margin of the season. Tough way to go. We're not making excuses for anybody.

**Q: Chris has five tight ends. Should he trade one to Matt?**

I'm not gonna get into other teams' personnel decisions.

---

**Q: Speaking of Matt. 65 points, the league low. Aaron beat him 107 to 65.**

*[Shakes head.]* We didn't play well. Matt didn't play well. That's the worst score in the league. That's -32.39 against his own average. Christian Watson 14, Bryce Young 14, Justin Herbert 11, Keaton Mitchell 11. After that, Saquon Barkley 6, Tony Pollard 3, and Denzel Boston, 2 points. Another receiver in the scoring lineup at 2 points. That's the other bust this week. And there's no tight end on the roster. There's no tight end at all. You can't run your offense without the pieces. Matt's 2-1 only because of what he did the first two weeks, and he fell five spots to ninth. We'll go back to work.

**Q: What about Aaron, who won by 42 while scoring below his own average?**

Aaron's a good football team. 107 is -22 against his average, and he won by 42. That's what good teams do. They win when they don't play their best. Josh Allen 22, Juwan Johnson 19 at tight end, Luther Burden III 11, Cam Ward 11. Two defenses, zero and zero, didn't matter. Aaron's 3-0, streak W3, still number one in cumulative points at 387. He's got to keep doing it. Nobody gives you anything for September.

---

**Q: Dan beat Mitch 106.5 to 94.5, a 12-point game. Dan's 3-0 too.**

Dan had Aaron Rodgers at 24, tenth-best performance of the week. Kenneth Walker III 19, Michael Penix Jr. 16, Javonte Williams 14, Deebo Samuel Sr. 14, Travis Kelce 11. Veteran players. Guys who know how to play. Dan's at 106.5, that's -10.5 on his average, but a win's a win. 3-0, stays second at 351. The draft recap said Dan bet on 2022. Well, it's 2026, and he's undefeated. So.

**Q: And Mitch?**

Mitch played better. 94.5, +9.67 on his average, moved up one spot to eleventh. Trevor Lawrence 21, Lamar Jackson 19, Tee Higgins 14, Terry McLaurin 14, Wan'Dale Robinson 11. But he's 0-3. Streak's L3. He keeps scoring and he keeps losing. Moral victories? *[Pause.]* We don't do moral victories.

---

**Q: Pete beat Joe 122 to 99. Pete's also 3-0.**

Pete's been quiet. He's just winning. Sam Darnold, 32 — second-best performance in the league. Brock Purdy, 29. Jaxon Smith-Njigba, 25. That's the ninth-best performance of the week. Drake London 12. Three weeks, three wins, streak W3, and he's still only sixth in cumulative points at 317.17. Moved up two spots. The record's ahead of the scoring, I understand that. I don't care. The standings count wins. Another C+ draft grade at 3-0, by the way. I'm just saying.

**Q: And Joe dropped to fifth.**

Joe scored 99. -11.5 on his average, down two spots to fifth, 331.5 in cumulative points. James Cook III 20, Jordan Love 19, Patrick Mahomes 16, Brian Robinson Jr. 11. Jonathan Taylor, 3. Ladd McConkey, 3. You get 3 from those two guys and you run into a quarterback room going 32 and 29, you're gonna lose. Joe's 1-2, streak L1. He'll be fine. He's got to coach 'em up.

---

**Q: Big picture. Where does the race stand after three weeks?**

*[Reads from sheet.]* Aaron first, 387. Dan second, 351. Sean third, 350. Bruce fourth, 347.5. That's a tight group behind Aaron. Joe fifth at 331.5, Pete sixth at 317.17. Chris 303, Mike 297.67, Matt 292.17, Cal 261, Mitch 254.5, Eli 252. Three unbeatens — Aaron, Dan, Pete. Two winless — Mitch and Eli. League average this week was 103.92.

It's Week 3. Nobody's winning anything in Week 3. We're on to Week 4.

**Q: Coach, one more —**

We're on to Week 4.

*[Exits.]*
