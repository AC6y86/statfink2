# My Name Is Forrest. This Is What Happened in Week 5.

*(as told on a bus-stop bench, with a box of chocolates and a real nice pair of shoes)*

Hello. My name's Forrest. Forrest Gump. You want a chocolate?

Mama always said fantasy football was like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're gonna get. Some weeks you bite in and it's caramel, and some weeks it's that pink coconut thing nobody wants. Week 5 in the Peninsula Football League had both kinds, and I'm gonna tell you about every single piece, because Mama also said if you're gonna tell a story you oughta tell the whole thing.

The league averaged 105.25 points this week. I don't know much about averages, but I know when a man goes way over it, and when a man goes way under it.

---

## The Night Chris Ran and Ran

**Chris 147, Aaron 109**

Now, I used to run. I ran clear across the country and back, and folks followed me 'cause they figured I knew something. Chris ran like that this week. He put up **147 points**, the most of anybody, and that was **+40.1 over his own average**. That's a whole lot of running.

The biggest part of it was a quarterback named **Drake Maye**, who scored **40 points**, the best of any player in the whole league this week. When I saw that 40, I thought of Bubba, 'cause Bubba could tell you every way there is to cook shrimp, and Drake Maye found about every way there is to score. Then **Jared Goff** came along and added **32**, which was the third-best number anybody had. Two quarterbacks scoring like that is like peas and carrots. They just go together.

Chris has got a lot of tight ends. People in the league talk about it, how he's got five tight ends and only two receivers, and folks figured that was stupid. But stupid is as stupid does, and this week **Tyler Warren** gave him **14**, **Trey McBride** gave him **11**, and **Kyle Pitts Sr.** gave him **8**. **Christian McCaffrey** chipped in **11**, and the **San Francisco 49ers Defense** gave him **8**.

Aaron didn't play bad. **Jaylen Warren** ran for **31** and **Josh Allen** threw for **30**, and **Malik Nabers** had **17**. But then there was **Dalton Kincaid**, a tight end who only got **2 points**, and that was this week's bust. **Amon-Ra St. Brown** only had **3**. Sometimes you reach in that box and you get the one that's just empty foil.

Aaron finished at **109**, which is **-8.9 against his own average**, and he's **3-2** now with two losses in a row. He slid down **one spot to third** in points, at **589.5**. Chris won by **38**, and he's **2-3**, moved **up one to seventh** at **534.5**. That's all I have to say about that.

---

## Pete Just Keeps on Going

**Pete 126, Mitch 70.5**

Pete hasn't lost. Not once. He's **5-0** and he's won **five straight**. It's like when Mama asked me how I could keep running and I just said, "I just felt like running." Pete just feels like winning.

This one was the **biggest blowout of the week**, a margin of **55.5**. Pete scored **126**, which was **+18.27 over his own average** and the second-best score of the week. He's got two quarterbacks doing the work: **C.J. Stroud** had **22** and **Brock Purdy** had **18**. **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** had **17**, and **Jacory Croskey-Merritt** ran for **14**. **Rhamondre Stevenson** and **Samaje Perine** had **11** each. Folks say Pete's got the best receivers and no running backs, but this week the running backs showed up anyway.

Mitch, he tried. **Trevor Lawrence** got **22** and **Sam LaPorta** got **14**, but after that it was **Kyren Williams** with **8**, **Brandin Cooks** with **8**, and **Terry McLaurin** with only **5**. Mitch scored **70.5**, which is **-15.5 against his own average**. He's **1-4**, and he dropped **one spot to twelfth**, last in points at **430**.

I felt bad for Mitch. Sometimes there just aren't enough rocks.

---

## Lieutenant Dan

**Cal 104.5, Dan 57.5**

Now I gotta tell you about Dan. I knew a Lieutenant Dan once. He was real sure about his destiny, and then one day things went bad all at once.

Dan had won **four straight** games. His last five go **W, W, W, W, L**. That L is this week. Dan scored **57.5**, the **lowest score in the whole league**, twelfth out of twelve, and **-46.8 under his own average**. That's a long way down. **Aaron Rodgers** did his part with **21**, and **J.K. Dobbins** and **Michael Penix Jr.** had **11** each, but his kicker **Cairo Santos** got **0.5**, and both his defenses, Seattle and the Chargers, got **0**. Dan fell **four spots, down to eighth**, at **521.5**. He's still **4-1**, though. Lieutenant Dan got his legs back in his own way, and I bet this Dan can too.

Cal won by **47**. **Jayden Daniels** threw for **33 points**, the second-best number of anybody this week. **Braelon Allen** ran for **17** and **MarShawn Lloyd** ran for **16**, and **Ja'Marr Chase** and **Kyler Murray** had **14** each. Cal's **104.5** was **+16.7 over his own average**. He's **2-3**, and he went **up one to eleventh** at **439**. Cal is like the shrimp boat after the hurricane. Everybody else's boat got wrecked, and his is still floating.

---

## Sean Has All the Points but Not the Wins

**Mike 122, Sean 112**

This was the closest one all week, and they still finished **10 points apart**. Not a single game came down to under 10. Mama said some weeks are like that.

Sean is a puzzle to me. He's **first in the whole league in points, with 598**. But he's **1-4**. It's like having the biggest box of chocolates in Alabama and nobody'll sit on the bench and share it with you. **Jahmyr Gibbs** ran for **31**, **Matthew Stafford** threw for **27**, **Derrick Henry** had **19**, and **Brock Bowers** had **17**. Folks say Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league, and Gibbs and Henry did their part. But both his defenses, **Buffalo** and **Tampa Bay**, gave him **0**, and his **112** came in at **-7.6 against his own average**. He's lost **two in a row**.

Mike went right past him. **Joe Burrow** got **26**, **Parker Washington** got **25**, and **Jacoby Brissett** got **24**. Two quarterbacks and a receiver nobody was talking about, all scoring at once. That's a lot like a fella from Greenbow, Alabama, ending up on a ping-pong team. You don't always get to pick how you end up somewhere. **Justin Jefferson** added **14** and **Matthew Golden** added **11**, and the **New Orleans Saints Defense** gave him **8**. Mike's **122** was the third-best score of the week, **+25.27 over his own average**. He's **2-3**, went **up one to ninth** at **483.67**.

---

## Joe Is Gaining on Sean

**Joe 116.5, Bruce 102**

Joe won by **14.5**. **James Cook III** ran for **25**, **Jordan Love** threw for **24**, and **Isaiah Likely**, a tight end, got **22**. **Mike Evans** had **19** and **Jameson Williams** had **16**. That's five fellas pulling the same rope, like a whole shrimp-boat crew.

Bruce had **Davante Adams** with **19**, and **Garrett Wilson** and **Aaron Jones Sr.** with **17** each. But **Bijan Robinson** only got **6**, and his quarterbacks **Bo Nix** and **Deshaun Watson** got **5** apiece. Bruce scored **102**, which is **-6.4 against his own average**, and he's lost **two in a row**. He's **2-3**, but he still went **up one to fourth** at **542**.

Here's the thing I keep thinking about. Joe is **3-2**, he's won **two straight**, he went **up one to second** in points at **596.5**, and Sean's at **598**. That's only **1.5 points** between them. Sean's out front and Joe's right behind him, the way people ran behind me across the country and never quite caught up. But I stopped one day. Folks always stop someday.

---

## Matt Doesn't Make Much Noise

**Matt 111, Eli 85**

Matt doesn't have any tight ends on his team. Not one. Folks said that was a mistake. But Matt is **4-1**, he's won **two straight**, and he went **up one to fifth** at **540.17**. Mama said you have to do the best with what God gave you, and Matt's doing just fine with no tight ends.

**Bucky Irving** ran for **28**, **Christian Watson** caught **25**, and **Ollie Gordon II** got **14**. **Jalen Hurts** and kicker **Jason Myers** got **11** each. Matt scored **111**, **+2.97 over his own average**. Matt reminds me of Jenny's tree. It just stands there quiet, and it's always there.

Eli had **Jameis Winston** with **27**, and **Puka Nacua** and **George Pickens** with **17** each. **Dak Prescott** added **14**. But after that it was **Justice Hill** with **3** and two defenses with **0**. Eli finished at **85**, **-8 against his own average**, lost by **26**, and he's **1-4**, down **one to tenth** at **465**.
