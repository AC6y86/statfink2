# Coat of Many Colors: The PFL Week 1 Setlist

*Liner notes, hand-written in rhinestone glitter pen, from the Peninsula Opry*

Well hey y'all, come on in and set a spell. Season openers are just like first dates — everybody shows up with their hair done, their best boots on, and a whole lot of hope they ain't earned yet. Twelve of you walked into this league in September with a draft grade in your back pocket, and honey, Week 1 went through those grades like a hot comb through a beehive.

The league averaged 111.58 points, which tells you exactly nothing about what actually happened, because six of y'all lived way above that line and six of y'all are sitting in the dressing room wondering where the band went. Let me sing it to you one track at a time.

---

## Track 1 — "Dan 168, Joe 137.5" (the big number)

Somebody told Dan his draft was a C+. Somebody told Joe his draft was an A-. Then Sunday came along, and darlin', the scoreboard don't read report cards.

Dan put up **168** — the highest score anybody managed all week — and he did it with a backfield stacked like a three-tier wedding cake. **Caleb Williams went for 38**, the single biggest number in the whole league. **Kenneth Walker III put up 28**. **David Montgomery went 27.** That's three men in the week's top ten all wearing the same jersey. Add **DJ Moore's 17** and **Javonte Williams' 16**, and Dan didn't so much win a football game as put on a revue.

And here's the part that just tickles me pink: the man everybody said "bet on 2022" got **Stefon Diggs 11**, **Aaron Rodgers 11**, and **Deebo Samuel Sr. 8** — thirty points from the antique cabinet. Storms make trees take deeper roots, sugar, and apparently so do birthdays.

Joe? Bless him, Joe played a beautiful song into a hurricane. **137.5** was the second-highest score in the entire league, and it bought him a loss and an 0-1 record. **Patrick Mahomes 24**, then a perfect little three-part harmony of **Jonathan Taylor 22, Jordan Love 22, and Isaiah Likely 22**. **Ladd McConkey 14. Demarcus Robinson 11.** Eleven men counted and nearly all of 'em sang. In four of the other five games this week, that 137.5 wins walking away. Instead Joe drew the one buzzsaw on the schedule and lost by **30.5**. If you want the rainbow, you gotta put up with the rain — but Joe, honey, you brought an umbrella *and* a raincoat and still got soaked.

## Track 2 — "Jolene, Jolene" (Aaron 134, Sean 109)

Two men brought their very best and only one of 'em got to keep it. **Josh Allen went for 38** — tied with Caleb Williams for the loudest note struck all week — and around him Aaron found a surprise sweetheart in **Jalen Coker at 25**, with **Amon-Ra St. Brown 19**, **Chris Olave 12**, and a matched pair of elevens from **Chase Brown** and **Juwan Johnson**. That's **134** and a 1-0 start for the B+ build.

Sean did not go quietly. **Derrick Henry 33. Jahmyr Gibbs 28. Chuba Hubbard 16.** The man who bet the farm on running backs in a quarterback league got 77 points out of three of 'em, and he *still* came up 25 short, because **Kirk Cousins 15** was the whole quarterback room's contribution and the tape-and-prayer held about as well as advertised.

Now here's my favorite little wrinkle in the whole week. The draft chatter said Aaron was the one who drafted two kickers. Come Sunday it was *Sean* who ran two of 'em out there — **Trey Smack 6.5** and **Wil Lutz 2.5** — nine points of kicking in a game he lost by twenty-five. It costs a lot of money to look this cheap, sugar, and it costs a lot of roster spots to look this deep.

## Track 3 — "Two Doors Down" (Matt 130.5, Mike 120)

Matt drafted nineteen men and not one single tight end, and I have never in my life seen a fella look so comfortable in a house with no roof. **Bryce Young 35** — the third-biggest score in the league — plus **Christian Watson 25**, **Jalen Hurts 21**, **Nico Collins 14**, and **Denzel Boston 11**. **130.5**, no tight end anywhere in sight, 1-0.

Mike put **120** on the board and still went home 0-1, and the ache here is in the arithmetic. **D'Andre Swift ran for 33** — fourth-best performance in the league — **Justin Jefferson 24**, and a curious little twin set of **Joe Burrow 14** and **Baker Mayfield 14**, with **Parker Washington 14** singing right alongside 'em. He lost by **10.5**. His kicker, **Cameron Dicker, scored 1**. I'm not saying one point is the whole story, darlin', but when you lose by ten and a half, every point you left out on the porch comes knocking at midnight.

## Track 4 — "Here You Come Again" (Chris 111.5, Eli 91.5)

They gave Chris a **D+** on draft day for hoarding five tight ends and two receivers like a squirrel with a shape problem. So of course Chris started **three tight ends** and won by twenty. **Dallas Goedert 22**, **Trey McBride 14**, **Tyler Warren 8** — forty-four points out of the position nobody wanted — with **Jared Goff 16**, **Cam Skattebo 14**, **Malik Willis 14**, and **Evan McPherson 9.5** filling in the harmony. Christian McCaffrey, the prize of that room, managed **3**, and Chris won anyway. **111.5.**

Eli, who drafted for 2028, scored **91.5** and got a real pretty glimpse of it: **Jaxson Dart 24** and **Kyle Monangai 17** are exactly the kind of tickets he bought. But **Puka Nacua 3** and **Alec Pierce 3** ain't the future, they're just Sunday, and **Dak Prescott's 16** couldn't cover the gap. Find out who you are and do it on purpose, hon — Eli sure has. It just don't cash till later.

## Track 5 — "Nine to Five" (Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5) — *the only close one*

One game all week finished inside a single score, and it was these two clocking in and grinding it out. Bruce passed on **nine** first-round quarterbacks in the draft and lived with the guilt, and then **Tyler Shough scored 30** and **Deshaun Watson added 11** — forty-one of Bruce's ninety-one points, from the position he swore off. Tumble outta bed and stumble to the kitchen, indeed. **Bijan Robinson 20** did the heavy lifting; **Jeremiyah Love 8** and **Aaron Jones Sr. 8** carried the lunch pail.

Mitch had **Trevor Lawrence 26** and **Lamar Jackson 22** — forty-eight points from two quarterbacks, with Lawrence's number good for tenth-best in the league — plus **Breece Hall 17**, and came up **7.5** short at **83.5**. One more touchdown anywhere on that sheet and Mitch clocks out a winner. Instead he's 0-1 and eleventh, and Bruce, the C+ with the guilty conscience, is 1-0 at **91** — the lowest-scoring winner in the whole league.

## Track 6 — "The Bargain Store" (Pete 104.5, Cal 58)

And now the sad song, because every good album's got one.

Cal got the league's only **A** on draft day. Cal scored **58** points — the lowest total in the Peninsula Football League this week, a full 25.5 beneath the next-lowest — and it came against **Pete's 104.5** for a **46.5**-point margin, the biggest gap of the opening weekend. **Ashton Jeanty went for 25**, which means everybody else Cal dressed combined for 33. **Jayden Daniels 10. CeeDee Lamb 8.** Then **3, 3, 3** from Tuten, Odunze, and Allgeier, and two kickers between 'em managing **6** — **Jake Elliott 3.5** and **Drew Stevens 2.5**. The adult build had itself a real long afternoon.

Pete, meanwhile, was the **C+** with the best receiver room and no backfield, and he made it look downright easy: **Brock Purdy 21**, **Zay Flowers 20**, **C.J. Stroud 19**, **Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17**, and **Jacory Croskey-Merritt 11** out of that backfield nobody believed in. That's an early statement in a great big voice.

---

## Where Everybody's Sitting

Dan's on top at **1-0 with 168** cumulative and a **30.5**-point cushion over Joe's **137.5** — and ain't that a thing, second in points, dead last in luck, **0-1**. Aaron's third at **134**, Matt fourth at **130.5**, Mike fifth at **120** with nothing but an L to show for it. Chris sixth at **111.5**, Sean seventh at **109**, Pete eighth at **104.5** despite winning by the widest margin of the week. Eli ninth at **91.5**, Bruce tenth at **91**, Mitch eleventh at **83.5**, and Cal bringing up the rear at **58**, already 110 points behind Dan after one solitary week.

Six of y'all are riding a one-game winning streak and six are riding a one-game skid. It's Week 1, sugar. Nobody's a legend yet and nobody's finished, and since every one of these was a first meeting, there's not a single grudge on the books. Give it time. We'll write some.

---

## The Infirmary Report

> **CAVEAT: Injury designations reflect CURRENT status at generation time, not necessarily during Week 1 games.**

Now let me set down the guitar and read this part straight, because a broken heart is one thing and a broken ankle is another.

- **Joe** — A.J. Brown (WR, NE), **Injured Reserve**, Sep 13: Brown, officially placed on IR Saturday, is expected to miss about six weeks with a right high-ankle sprain (Ian Rapoport, NFL Network). Losing an A.J. Brown the same weekend you score 137.5 and lose is a country song all by itself.
- **Sean** — Isiah Pacheco (RB, DET), **Injured Reserve**, Sep 14: Pacheco (back) underwent surgery Monday and is expected out a significant amount of time (Nolan Bianchi, Detroit News). Also **Out**: Alvin Kamara (RB, NO); Tua Tagovailoa (QB, ATL); and Brock Bowers (TE, LV), labeled day-to-day Monday by Klint Kubiak (Sam Warren, The Athletic).
- **Cal** — Dylan Sampson (RB, CLE), **Out**, Sep 14: Todd Monken said Monday the team is still evaluating the severity of Sampson's knee injury (Daniel Oyefusi, ESPN.com).
- **Chris** — Josh Jacobs (RB, GB), **Out**, Sep 13: Jacobs (personal) remains on the Commissioner's Exempt List, though the NFL may count that time as served if a suspension is eventually issued (Adam Schefter, ESPN).
- **Eli** — TreVeyon Henderson (RB, NE), **Out**, Sep 14: Mike Vrabel said Henderson (ankle) will practice Monday (Mike Reiss, ESPN.com). Also Zach Charbonnet (RB, SEA), **Out**, Sep 7: Mike Macdonald said Charbonnet (knee) looks "awesome" rehabbing from a torn ACL (Gregg Bell, Tacoma News Tribune).
- **Pete** — Ty Johnson (RB, BUF), **Out**, Sep 14: listed as a limited participant on Monday's estimated practice report.

---

That's the record, y'all. One week down, and already the A students are crying while the C+ crowd dances on the tables. Don't you go getting comfortable, Dan — and don't you go getting discouraged, Cal. The way I see it, you'll never do a whole lot in this life if you're not brave enough to try.

Same time next Sunday. Bring your best coat, whatever colors it comes in.
