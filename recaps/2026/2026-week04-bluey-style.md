# PFL Week 4: "Keepy Uppy" (A Bluey Recap)

*Episode guide, Season 2026, Week 4. Narrated by Bluey Heeler, aged six-and-three-quarters, with help from Bingo, and Dad when he's not lying on the floor pretending to be a rug.*

---

## Cold Open: The Balloon Game

Righto, everybody listen up! This week we're playing **Keepy Uppy**. Remember the rules? You've got a balloon, and you have to keep it up in the air, and if it touches the floor you LOSE. That's the whole game.

Two teams still have their balloon up after four weeks: **Dan and Pete**, both 4-0, both on a W4 streak. And the funny part is that in the draft the grown-ups gave them both a **C+**! Bingo says a C+ is "a bit like when Dad makes pancakes and they're a weird shape but they still taste good." For real life, Bingo, that's exactly what it is.

Meanwhile **Cal** got an **A** in the draft, and Cal's balloon is... well. We'll get to Cal. Somebody hold Dad's tail so he doesn't roll away. Wackadoo, let's go!

---

## Episode 1: "Big Joe's Pancake Stack" (Joe 148.5, Sean 136)

This was the biggest game in the whole house. Both teams were jumping on the couch and Mum said "not on the couch" and nobody listened.

**Joe put up 148.5, the most points of anyone this week**, and that's why he's our centerpiece. Hooray! It was like when Dad makes a giant stack of pancakes, and every pancake is a running back:

- **Brian Robinson Jr.**: 27 points. The biggest pancake on the stack!
- **Jonathan Taylor**: 22
- **Romeo Doubs**: 19 (he's a receiver, he's the syrup)
- **Patrick Mahomes** and **Jordan Love**: 16 each
- **James Cook III**: 14
- **Quinshon Judkins**: 11

That's +28.5 over Joe's own average, and he went from fifth all the way up to **third in cumulative points (480)**. ▲2! His streak is W1 and his last five go LWLW, which is a bit like a game of Musical Statues: freeze, move, freeze, move.

Now here's the part that made Bingo cry a little bit. **Sean scored 136.** That's +14.5 over his own average and the **third-best score of the week**, and he STILL lost. Chuba Hubbard got 25, Kirk Cousins got 24, Alvin Kamara got 16 and Brock Bowers got 14. In the draft the grown-ups said Sean "bet on RBs in a QB league," and the RBs mostly showed up! But Joe's pancake stack was taller.

And the strangest bit: Sean is **first in the whole league in cumulative points, with 486**, up two spots, ▲2. But his record is **1-3**. Dad says that's "like being the best dancer at the party but nobody asks you to dance." Mum told Dad that wasn't helpful. Sean's balloon keeps floating up in points and then sneaking down to the floor anyway.

This game had the smallest gap of the week at **12.5**. That's still not close, though. The digest says no game was decided by under 10. Nobody was nervous for very long.

## Episode 2: "The Unicorse Says Neigh" (Matt 137, Cal 73.5)

Remember Unicorse? The puppet who's really rude and just keeps winning? That's **Matt** this week.

**Matt 137, Cal 73.5. A 63.5-point margin.** That's the **blowout of the week**, the biggest gap of anybody. Bingo wants to know if that's even allowed. It is, Bingo. It's allowed.

Matt's receivers were the stars:
- **Tetairoa McMillan**: 28, tied for the second-best score in the whole league
- **Nico Collins**: 25
- **Bryce Young**: 22
- **Ollie Gordon II**: 17

That's +29.71 over Matt's own average. He jumped **▲3 to sixth (429.17)** and he's **3-1**. And remember, **Matt still has zero tight ends.** ZERO! It's like going to the beach without a bucket and still building the biggest sandcastle. Nobody understands it. Not even Dad, and Dad says he understands everything.

Poor **Cal**. **CeeDee Lamb** did his bit with 20, but after him it was Carnell Tate at 9 and Tyler Allgeier at 8, and then a whole row of 6s and 3s. Kyler Murray got 6. Ashton Jeanty got 6. Cal's two defenses got 0 each. He was -10.13 under his own average, and he's now **twelfth in cumulative points (334.5)**, ▼2, at 1-3 with an L2 streak.

The **A-grade** draft team is at the bottom of the pile. Bluey's rule: a shiny new toy doesn't mean you'll win Keepy Uppy. Sometimes the balloon just goes *bloop*, straight onto the carpet.

## Episode 3: "Grannies Win Again" (Dan 113, Aaron 93.5)

When Bingo and I play Grannies, we shuffle around very slowly and say "back in my day." That's **Dan's** whole team! The grown-ups said Dan "bet on 2022," and the grannies are doing just fine, thank you very much.

**Dan 113, Aaron 93.5. Dan wins by 19.5.**

- **Kenneth Walker III**: 28, the second-best score in the league
- **Javonte Williams**: 27
- **Aaron Rodgers**: 26! A granny doing the floss!

That's three of the top seven scores this week, and all three are Dan's. Michael Penix Jr. added 11. The funny bit is Dan was actually -3 under his own average, and he slipped **▼2 to fourth (464)**. But he's **4-0** with a **W4** streak, and his last five are WWWW. The balloon is still up! The grannies are still dabbing!

**Aaron** was the sad one. **Josh Allen** got 22 and **Malik Nabers** got 17, and Cam Ward added 13. But Amon-Ra St. Brown only got 6 and Chase Brown only got 3, and Aaron was **-26.63 under his own average**. He's still **3-1** and **second in points (480.5)**, ▼1, but his perfect WWW start just went *pop*. Last five: WWWL.

## Episode 5: "Bin Night for Najee" (Pete 95.5, Mike 64)

**Pete 95.5, Mike 64. Pete wins by 31.5.**

Pete scored only the **seventh-best** total of the week (95.5, -7.67 under his own average), and he still won by more than 30. Dad says that's "the dream, mate. Do the bare minimum and still win." Mum gave Dad the look.

Pete's helpers:
- **C.J. Stroud**: 22
- **Zay Flowers**: 17
- **Brock Purdy**: 16
- **Minnesota Vikings Defense** and **Chicago Bears Defense**: 5 each. That's one of the only times a DST scored anything this week!

But here's this week's **BUST**, so everybody put on your sad face. **Najee Harris got 2 points for Pete.** Two! That's like taking the bin out on bin night and finding out it was already empty. The grown-ups said Pete has "the best WR room but no backfield," and Najee kind of proved it. Pete is still **4-0**, W4, last five WWWW, though he slid ▼1 to seventh (412.67). The other C+ pancake team is still standing too!

**Mike**, oh Mike. **Joe Burrow scored 28**, tied for the second-best score in the league, and it didn't matter one bit. Jacoby Brissett added 10. Tucker Kraft and Matthew Golden got 8 each. Then D'Andre Swift got 3 and both defenses got 0. Mike's **64 was the lowest score in the whole league** (-26.42 under his own average), and he dropped **▼2 to tenth (361.67)**. That's 1-3, last five LLWL.

Imagine if Burrow had been on a team that scored like everyone else. The league average this week was **105.92**. Mike was still a long way short of that, even with Burrow.

**Mitch 105, Chris 84.5. Mitch wins by 20.5.**

**Mitch** got off the floor for his **first win**! **Kyren Williams** got 25, **Lamar Jackson** got 16, **Sam LaPorta** got 14 and **Tee Higgins** got 12.
