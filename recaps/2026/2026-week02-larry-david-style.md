# PFL Week 2: Pretty, Pretty, Pretty Bad

Look, I don't want to make a thing out of this. But I'm going to make a thing out of this.

Week 2 of the Peninsula Football League. League average: 96.54 points. Four games decided by single digits. And a level of social misconduct that, frankly, I found unacceptable. So I've made a list. I do that now. I keep a list.

---

## Grievance #1: The Man Who Scored 118 and Got Nothing

Bruce put up 118 points. One hundred eighteen! That's the second-best score in the entire league this week. Davante Adams goes for 28, Tyler Shough — a quarterback I would not recognize in a deli — goes for 22, Deshaun Watson 16, Garrett Wilson 11. Bruce scored 13.5 more than his own average. He did everything you're supposed to do.

He lost. 146 to 118. Because Aaron happened to be sitting across the table with Josh Allen.

Now here's what kills me. Bruce goes up five spots in the standings. ▲5! He *lost* the game and he *climbed*. And Sean and Cal each went up one too, so don't come at me with "nobody moves up when they lose" — three teams rose this week and one of them just got beaten by 28. That's the system! You get punished with a loss and rewarded with a promotion. It's like getting fired and then being handed a parking space.

## Grievance #2: Josh Allen Doesn't Even Look at Anybody

Forty points. Josh Allen, forty points, for Aaron. Top performance in the league, and it's not close — Jaxon Smith-Njigba was second at 36.

And Aaron didn't need it! He had Amon-Ra St. Brown at 25, Cam Ward at 22, Chris Olave 14, Dalton Kincaid 14, Terrance Ferguson 11, Brandon Aubrey 8. That's a *dinner party*. That's a full table before Allen even sits down. 146 points, plus-6 against his own average, first in weekly scoring, 2-0, and now 280 cumulative points and the number one seed after moving ▲2.

Two weeks, a 38 and a 40 from Allen. That's not a hot streak, that's a guy who knows he's not getting stopped and has stopped pretending otherwise. B+ draft grade, by the way. B+! Somebody owes somebody an apology and it's not Aaron.

## Grievance #3: The Zeros

I counted. I sat there and I counted. Defenses in this league put up zero after zero after zero. Houston: zero. Detroit: zero. The Ravens, the Browns, the Bills, the Bucs, the Jags, the Chiefs, the Eagles, the Commanders, the Giants, the Cowboys, the Rams, the Steelers, the Broncos, the 49ers — all zeros.

You show up, you dress, you play sixty minutes of professional football, you contribute *nothing*. And nobody says anything! Meanwhile Pete's Minnesota Vikings Defense scrapes together 1.67 points and that 1.67 turns out to be the whole ballgame, which brings me to —

## Grievance #4: 2.67 Points

Pete 90.67, Eli 88. The closest game of the week.

Pete gets 36 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and 27 from Brock Purdy — 63 points from two guys — and then it's C.J. Stroud at 12, Rashee Rice at 6, Drake London at 3, and the tumbleweeds. Pete is 2-0 and he has not enjoyed a single minute of it. He blew Cal out by 46.5 in week 1 and he has been sweating through his shirt ever since. He scored 6.92 below his own average and *stayed unbeaten*. A C+ draft grade doing all this. It's absurd.

And Eli. Eli started two kickers. Two! Will Reichard 6, Nick Folk 2. Dak Prescott went for 29, Omarion Hampton 14, TreVeyon Henderson 14, George Kittle 14 — that's a real football team — and he lost by 2.67 because he needed one more human being and had to go get a second kicker. If Folk kicks one additional field goal, we're having a completely different conversation. Eli is 0-2, down a spot to tenth, and he's 1.75 under his own average, which means this is just *who he is now*.

## Grievance #5: Dan Won a Game He Should Be Embarrassed By

Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67. Margin: 2.83.

Dan scored 45.75 points below his own average. He was eleventh out of twelve in weekly scoring and he *won*. Travis Kelce 17, Stefon Diggs 16, Kenneth Walker III 12, Daniel Jones 11, Harrison Butker 9.5, Aaron Rodgers 6. That's the 2022 season in a jar on a shelf. He went 168 in week 1 to 76.5 here. That's not a decline, that's a *cliff*. And he's still 2-0 and still second in cumulative points with 244.5.

Mike, meanwhile, was dead last in scoring — 73.67, 23.17 under his average — and his fourth-best scorer was the New England Patriots Defense at 9.67, behind DeVonta Smith's 17, Joe Burrow's 16, and Baker Mayfield's 11. Justin Jefferson gave him 3. *Three.* Mike fell ▼4 to ninth, the biggest drop of the week. That's a man getting out of an elevator on the wrong floor and just walking anyway because he doesn't want to explain himself.

## Grievance #6: Matt Still Doesn't Own a Tight End

Matt 96.67, Mitch 76.5. Two weeks in, Matt has zero tight ends and he's 2-0. Bryce Young 24, Jalen Hurts 19, Denzel Boston 14, Carolina's defense 9.67, Tetairoa McMillan 9. He scored 16.92 under his own average and still won by 20.17.

Meanwhile Chris is sitting over there with five tight ends like a man hoarding umbrellas. Trey McBride 8, Tyler Warren 8, Harold Fannin Jr. 3. Somebody make the trade! Pick up the phone! Nobody picks up a phone anymore.

Mitch: 0-2, last in cumulative points at 160, and his third-leading scorer was Chase McLaughlin, a kicker, with 8.5. Kyren Williams 14, Lamar Jackson 11, Sam LaPorta 11. Lamar Jackson, eleven points. I don't accept that.

## Grievance #7: The Bust, and the Deli Slicer

Joe 95, Chris 88. Seven points. Joe rode Patrick Mahomes 27, Jonathan Taylor 22, and James Cook III 17 — 66 from three guys — and then got 3 from Mike Evans and 3 from Brian Robinson Jr. and hung on 21.25 points below his own average.

Chris got a 32-point masterpiece out of Jared Goff and lost anyway, because of the official Bust of the Week: **Travis Etienne Jr., 2 points.** Two! In a seven-point loss! You're allowed to have a bad day. You're not allowed to have a bad day that costs the group. That's a violation. Chris drops to seventh.

And Cal 109.5, Sean 100. Cal's the A-grade draft that's *still* only 1-1, but he went 25.75 over his own average — CeeDee Lamb 28, Ja'Marr Chase 22, Jake Ferguson 16 — and got it done. Sean? Matthew Stafford 32, the highest-scoring player on either roster and the only Sean player in the league's top ten. Kirk Cousins added 24. A hundred points. Zero wins. A- draft grade, 0-2, and he sits on exactly 209 cumulative points — the *identical* number as Bruce, who's 1-1 and ranked one spot ahead of him. Same points, different lives. That's the league. That's everything.
