# Oh, the Points You'll Score! (A PFL Week 5 Fable, as Told by Dr. Seuss)

*Hang on to your hats, all you Whos of the League,*
*For Week Five brought some thrills and a lot of fatigue!*
*Six games were played out on the Peninsula lawn.*
*Some winners went whooping, some losers just... yawned.*
*The winners skipped home into Whoville to feast,*
*Where the roast beast is stacked up from the west to the east.*
*The losers trudged off to a gloomier space,*
*A slumpy, grey, grumpy spot called The Waiting Place.*

*Waiting for touchdowns, or waivers to clear,*
*Waiting for kickers who kick, now and here.*
*Waiting for Sunday, or a Tuesday, or so...*
*Waiting, just waiting, for points that won't show.*

Let's see who went where. Turn the page, if you please.
And remember the league average: **105.25**. Easy as cheese.

## Fable the First: The Maye-Bird Who Hatched a Forty
### Chris 147, Aaron 109

*There once was a Chris with a roster so odd*
*That the Whos in the stands gave it a sideways nod.*
*Five tight ends! Two receivers! A Zizzer-Zazz build!*
*"It won't work," said the Whos. "It's a team that's been grilled."*

*But then came a Maye-Bird, a Drake with a wing,*
*Who flapped and who flung and who did the whole thing.*
*He sat on his egg and he hatched it with gall:*
*He went FORTY. Yes, **40**. The best of them all.*

There it is, the big fish of the week, the one in the pot: **Drake Maye's 40 points** was the top performance in the whole league. Nobody else got close. Then Chris piled on **Jared Goff's 32**, the third-best score of the week, so Chris had two quarterbacks in the top three. He had a Thing One and a Thing Two, and both of them were out of the box and running wild.

*Tyler Warren chipped **14**, and McCaffrey **11**,*
*Trey McBride added **11**. (That's some tight-end heaven.)*
*Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked **9**, Kyle Pitts Sr. had **8**,*
*The 49ers D got **8**, Kendre Miller **8**. Great!*

Total: **147**, the league-high score, and **+40.1 versus his own average**. That's a whopper-sized Wocket-in-the-pocket of a week. Chris's five-tight-end idea looked silly, then it put up the biggest number of the week.

And Aaron? Poor Aaron did plenty right.
**Jaylen Warren hit 31** and **Josh Allen hit 30**, two of the week's top six performances. **Malik Nabers** added **17** and **Blake Corum 11**.
*But then came the Glumms, the low-scoring ones,*
*Amon-Ra St. Brown with a sad little **3**,*
*And Dalton Kincaid, our **BUST** of the week,*
*With **2 points** for Aaron. Oh, what a bleak streak.*

Kincaid's **2** was the only skill player in a scoring lineup to land in the digest's bust column. Aaron lost by **38**, **109** to **147**, and fell **▼1** to **third** with **589.5** cumulative points. He's **3-2**, and his streak is **L2** after a **WWW** start. Chris gets a Whoville win at **2-3**, rising **▲1** to **seventh** with **534.5**.

## Fable the Second: Pete Who Would Not, Could Not Lose
### Pete 126, Mitch 70.5

*Would you lose in a box? Would you lose with a fox?*
*Would you lose to a Mitch in his red-and-white socks?*
*"I would NOT lose in a box! I would NOT lose with a fox!*
*I would not lose to Mitch, not in shoes, not in socks!*
*I will not lose here or there. I will not lose anywhere!*
*I am PETE. I am **5-0**. I am the lone unbeaten pear!"*

Pete is still perfect. He's the only unbeaten team left, with a **W5** streak (WWWWW, five in a row). He scored **126**, the second-best total of the week and **+18.27 over his own average**, and his quarterbacks did most of it. **C.J. Stroud** threw for **22** and **Brock Purdy** for **18**. **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** caught for **17**, and the running backs came three deep: **Jacory Croskey-Merritt 14**, **Rhamondre Stevenson 11**, **Samaje Perine 11**. **Drake London** added **9**.

*Mitch, dear Mitch, had a **Trevor Lawrence 22**,*
*And a **LaPorta 14**, which was nice, it is true.*
*But **Kyren Williams 8**, and **Brandin Cooks 8**,*
*And **McLaurin's 5**... well, it all came too late.*

Mitch finished with **70.5**, eleventh of twelve this week and **-15.5 versus his own average**. The margin was **55.5**, the **biggest blowout of the week**, and there were no close calls under ten points anywhere in the league, so this lopsided game gets the headline. Pete climbs **▲1** to **sixth** in points with **538.67**. Mitch drops **▼1** to **twelfth**, last in the league with **430**, and sits at **1-4**. He has a long, long chair in the Waiting Place, with a cushion and a sign that says "Reserved."

## Fable the Third: The Sean Who Was Rich, But Was Poor
### Mike 122, Sean 112

*Now Sean is a Sneetch with a star on his belly:*
*The most points of all! (It's a sight, said the deli.)*
*He's **first** in the points with a **598**,*
*But his record is **1-4**. Oh, the irony! Wait...*

This was the closest game of the week, at a margin of **10**. It still didn't sneak under the close-call line, but it was the only one that stayed tense. Sean's running-back bet in a quarterback league paid off again on paper. **Jahmyr Gibbs** ran for **31**, tied for fourth-best performance of the week. **Matthew Stafford** threw for **27**, **Derrick Henry** added **19**, **Brock Bowers 17**, and **Kirk Cousins 11**.

*But the Bills D said "zero." The Bucs D said "none."*
*Two zeroes! Two goose-eggs! Two holes in the sun!*

Meanwhile, Mike had a big Whoville night: **122** points, **+25.27 over his own average**, third-best in the league.
*Joe Burrow threw **26**! Parker Washington caught **25**!*
*Jacoby Brissett **24**! Oh, the Mike team's alive!*
*Justin Jefferson **14**, Matthew Golden **11**,*
*And the Saints D chipped **8**, which was Mike's little leaven.*

Mike wins **122-112**, goes to **2-3**, and climbs **▲1** to **ninth** with **483.67**. Sean's streak is **L2** and his last five read LLWLL. He keeps the cumulative crown at **598**, but the crown sits on a **1-4** head. A star on the belly doesn't count in the win column.

## Fable the Fourth: Joe, Who Crept Up the Mountain Like a Grinch on a Sleigh
### Joe 116.5, Bruce 102

*Up, up the hill, with a sack and a sled,*
*Crept Joe toward the summit where Sean sits ahead.*
*He stole no roast beast, he stole no Who-hash,*
*He just stole a **116.5** and took off in a dash.*

**James Cook III** ran for **25**. **Jordan Love** threw for **24**. **Isaiah Likely** went for **22** from the tight end spot, and **Mike Evans** (**19**) and **Jameson Williams** (**16**) handled the receiving. Two kickers, **Tyler Loop (6.5)** and **Andre Szmyt (4)**, kicked in the rest. It wasn't flashy, **-2.8 versus his own average**, but it was steady and it worked.

Bruce got a balanced spread. **Davante Adams 19**, **Garrett Wilson 17**, **Aaron Jones Sr. 17**, **Emanuel Wilson 11**. But his quarterbacks had a Gloom-Gack of a day, with **Bo Nix 5** and **Deshaun Watson 5**, and **Bijan Robinson** managed only **6**.
*Five and five and a six from Bijan?*
*That's a Waiting Place week, I'm afraid, Mister Man.*

Joe wins **116.5-102**, moves to **3-2** with a **W2** streak, and climbs **▲1** to **second** with **596.5**, just **1.5** behind Sean's **598**. Bruce scored **102**, ninth this week and **-6.4 versus his average**, and falls to **2-3** (**L2**). Oddly enough, he still rose **▲1** to **fourth** in cumulative points with **542**. He lost the game and gained a rung, which is the kind of thing that only happens in Seuss books and fantasy football.

## Fable the Fifth: Matt Who Had No Tight End (And Didn't Care a Bit)
### Matt 111, Eli 85

*Matt has no tight end. Not one! Not a single!*
*No tight end to block and no tight end to mingle!*
*"How can he win?" asked the Who-folk, perplexed.*
*"Like this," Matt replied. "Watch what happens next."*

**Bucky Irving** ran for **28**, seventh-best performance of the week. **Christian Watson** caught for **25**. **Ollie Gordon II** added **14**, while **Jalen Hurts** and kicker **Jason Myers** chipped **11** apiece. Toss in **Justin Herbert 8** and **Denzel Boston 8** and you get **111**, **+2.97 over his own average**.

Eli had a real hero in **Jameis Winston**, whose **27** matched Stafford's for the eighth-best mark in the league. **Puka Nacua** and **George Pickens** each caught for **17**, and **Dak Prescott** threw for **14**. After that it thinned out: **Will Reichard 7**, **Justice Hill 3**, and both defenses, the Rams and the Steelers, at **zero**.
*A Winston, a Puka, a Pickens, a Dak,*
*But nothing behind them to carry the pack.*

Matt wins by **26**, **111-85**. He's **4-1** with a **W2** streak and rises **▲1** to **fifth** with **540.17**, and he's done it without a single tight end. Eli lands at **85** (**-8 versus his average**), falls to **1-4**, and slips **▼1** to **tenth** with **465**. He takes the chair next to Mitch in the Waiting Place, and they can split a sandwich.

## Fable the Sixth: Dan's Collapse at the Bottom of the Slide
### Cal 104.5, Dan 57.5

*Dan had been winning. Four wins in a row!*
*WWWW, oh, the places he'd go!*
*But Week Five came along with a crunch and a crack,*
*And Dan's team went sliding right down the back.*

**Jayden Daniels** went for **33**, the **second-best performance in the league**, and Cal's team rode him all the way. **Braelon Allen** ran for **17**, **MarShawn Lloyd 16**, **Ja'Marr Chase 14**, **Kyler Murray 14**. Cal scored **104.5**, **+16.7 over his average**, a proper bounce.

And Dan? **Aaron Rodgers** threw for **21**, and then the bottom fell out. **J.K. Dobbins 11**, **Michael Penix Jr. 11**, **Javonte Williams 8**, **Khalil Shakir 6**, **Cairo Santos 0.5**, and the Seahawks and Chargers defenses at **zero** apiece.
*Half a point, Santos? A half? Just a smidge?*
*That's a kick that fell off of the edge of the bridge!*

Dan scored **57.5**, the **league low** (twelfth of twelve) and **-46.8 versus his own average**. That's the biggest drop below average in the league. Cal wins by **47**, gets to **2-3** with a **W1**, and rises **▲1** to **eleventh** with **439**. Dan's first loss leaves him at **4-1** (last five: WWWWL), and he tumbles **▼4** to **eighth** with **521.5**. That's the biggest fall of the week, and Dan gets a short stay in the Waiting Place.
