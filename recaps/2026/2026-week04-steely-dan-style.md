# CAN'T BUY A WIN: PFL Week 4
### *A Steely Dan Record in Six Tracks, with Liner Notes*

*Recorded on location in the Peninsula Football League. Forty takes, mixed for maximum irony. Every number on this record was cleared with the official digest. We don't do fake chords.*

---

## Side A

### Track 1: "Aja" (Joe 148.5, Sean 136)
*The long one. A drum solo and the best score of the week.*

Every Steely Dan record has one track where the session players stop playing it safe and just go off. In Week 4 that was **Joe**. His **148.5** led the league, **+28.5 vs his own average**, and it played like the title track of *Aja*: a room full of great players, all of them taking a solo.

**Brian Robinson Jr.** opened with **27**, the Purdie shuffle: deep in the pocket and impossible to stop. **Jonathan Taylor** added **22**, **Romeo Doubs** cut a **19**-point guitar break nobody saw on the charts, and the horn section of **Patrick Mahomes (16)** and **Jordan Love (16)** played in tight unison. Behind them were **James Cook III 14**, **Quinshon Judkins 11** and **Jameson Williams 9**.

The cruel part was who stood on the other side of the glass. **Sean** scored **136**, third-best in the week and **+14.5 vs his own average**, and still lost by **12.5**. **Chuba Hubbard (25)** and **Kirk Cousins (24)** sang lead, with **Alvin Kamara 16** and **Brock Bowers 14** on harmonies. Most weeks that wins.

Joe is **2-2** with a **W1** streak and moves **▲2** to **third** in cumulative points at **480**. The A- the draft gave him finally sounds earned.

### Track 2: "Deacon Blues" (Sean, the Interlude)
*They got a name for the losers in the world.*

Sean gives us the most Steely Dan sentence of the season. He's **first in cumulative points (486)**, he moved **▲2** this week, and he's **1-3**, with a last four of **LLWL**. He's the saxophone player in the song. He knows every chord, and he's still working the losing side of town. If points were wins, he'd be playing the big room.

### Track 3: "Reelin' in the Years" (Dan 113, Aaron 93.5)
*A big guitar riff, and a C+ grade the narrator was too smart to believe.*

The experts said **Dan** drafted like it was 2022 and gave him a **C+**. Now he's **4-0** on a **W4** streak, and the experts are the ones reelin'.

Dan won **113 to 93.5**, by **19.5**. **Kenneth Walker III** put up **28**, **Javonte Williams** answered with **27**, and **Aaron Rodgers**, the oldest record in the crate, played **26** like a reissue outselling the new releases. **Michael Penix Jr. (11)** and **Deebo Samuel Sr. (8)** filled in the rest. Dan was **-3 vs his own average**. He didn't need his best take, only a clean one.

**Aaron** took his first loss of the season and is now **3-1**, with a last four of **WWWL**. His **93.5** was **-26.63 vs his own average**. **Josh Allen** gave him **22**, **Malik Nabers 17** and **Cam Ward 13**, but **Amon-Ra St. Brown** played a quiet **6**, **Chase Brown** managed **3**, and both defenses went silent at **0**. Aaron slips **▼1** to **second** in points at **480.5**, half a point ahead of Joe's **480**. That's close enough to share a studio wall.

## Side B

### Track 4: "Peg" (Pete 95.5, Mike 64)
*Glossy, breezy, never breaks a sweat.*

**Pete** is the other C+ unbeaten, **4-0** on a **W4** streak, and he wins the way "Peg" sounds: smooth and almost too easy. He scored only **95.5**, **seventh** in the week and **-7.67 vs his own average**, and won by **31.5**. **C.J. Stroud** led with **22**, **Zay Flowers** soloed for **17**, **Brock Purdy** added **16**, and **Minnesota** and **Chicago** did the uncredited session work at **5** each.

This track also holds the week's **bust**. **Najee Harris** scored **2 points** for Pete, the only skill player in a scoring lineup at 2 or below. A "no backfield" team ran out a two-point running back, which is like a chord with the root missing. It didn't matter, and that's the annoying part.

**Mike** scored **64**, the **league low** and **-26.42 vs his own average**, even with **Joe Burrow** throwing **28**. Burrow played a great solo over a band that had already walked out: **Jacoby Brissett 10**, **Tucker Kraft 8**, **Matthew Golden 8**, **D'Andre Swift 3**, and both defenses at **0**. Mike drops **▼2** to **tenth** (**361.67**) at **1-3**.

### Track 5: "Kid Charlemagne" (Mitch 105, Chris 84.5)
*The great solo on the losing side.*

Everybody remembers Larry Carlton's solo on "Kid Charlemagne," a song about an empire falling apart around one guy. This week the solo was **Drake Maye's 29**, the **top individual score in the league**, and it came in a **loss**.

**Chris**, the draft's **D+** with five tight ends and two receivers, got **29** from Maye and **20** from **Jared Goff**, then very little: **Christian McCaffrey 11**, **Harold Fannin Jr. 8**, **Cam Skattebo 3**, and both defenses at **0**. That added up to **84.5**, **tenth** in the week. The skid is **L3** (**WLLL**), and Chris slips **▼1** to **eighth** at **387.5**.

**Mitch** won **105 to 84.5**, by **20.5**, for his first win. **Kyren Williams** led with **25**, then **Lamar Jackson 16**, **Sam LaPorta 14**, **Tee Higgins 12** and the **New York Giants Defense 8**. He was **+15.13 vs his own average** but is **1-3** and still **eleventh** in points at **359.5**. The first win feels great. The standings don't care.

### Track 6: "Hey Nineteen" (Eli 128, Bruce 92.5)
*The kids don't know the old songs, and this week they didn't need to.*

**Eli** drafted for 2028, and the future showed up early. His **128** was **fourth** in the week and **+33 vs his own average**, the biggest jump in the league. It beat **Bruce** by **35.5** for Eli's first win. **Kyle Monangai** led with **25**, **Jameis Winston** (not exactly nineteen) threw **24**, **Dak Prescott** and **Puka Nacua** added **17** each, and **Will Reichard** kicked **10**. Eli goes **▲3** to **ninth** at **380**, now **1-3**.

**Bruce** got **25** from **Bijan Robinson** and a surprising **22** from **Emanuel Wilson**, plus **14** each from **Tyler Shough** and **Deshaun Watson**. After that, three players scored **3** and **Chris Boswell** kicked **2.5**. Bruce's **92.5** was **-17.5 vs his own average**. He's **2-2** and drops **▼1** to **fifth** at **440**.

### Hidden Bonus Track: "Rikki Don't Lose That Number" (Matt 137, Cal 73.5)
*The blowout of the week gets a whole track to itself.*

**63.5 points**: the biggest margin of the week. **Matt** still has **zero tight ends** and scored **137** anyway, **second** in the league and **+29.71 vs his own average**. **Tetairoa McMillan** scored **28**, **Nico Collins 25**, **Bryce Young 22** and **Ollie Gordon II 17**. Matt is **3-1** and jumps **▲3** to **sixth** at **429.17**.

**Cal**, holder of the draft's only **A**, keeps losing the number. He scored **73.5**, **eleventh** in the week. **CeeDee Lamb** put up **20**, then **Carnell Tate 9** and **Tyler Allgeier 8**. Cal falls **▼2** to **twelfth**, last at **334.5**, **1-3** on an **L2** streak. Right now that A grade is the best number he's got.
