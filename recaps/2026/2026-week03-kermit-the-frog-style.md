## ACT ONE: The Headliner — Sean 141, Eli 72.5

Ladies and gentlemen, it gives me great pleasure to introduce our star of the week: **Jahmyr Gibbs, with 33 points for Sean!** The top performance in the whole league! Give him a hand! Give him *all* the hands! Animal, not *that* hand!

Now, you have to understand, Sean came into this week at 0-2, and it's not easy being winless. Believe me, I know. It's not easy being green, either, but at least green has never lost by double digits on a Sunday. Sean's draft plan was a bold one: bet on running backs in a quarterback league. People laughed. Statler and Waldorf laughed *very* loudly. And then this week the running backs and the quarterbacks all showed up together, like a big happy chorus line: Gibbs 33, Matthew Stafford 22, Derrick Henry 22, Kirk Cousins 21, and Brock Bowers 17. That's **141 points**, the league high, and 24.33 above Sean's own average. Sean jumped **three spots to third** in cumulative points at 1-2. That's a resurrection, folks! Somebody tell Gonzo to stop trying to set the stage on fire to celebrate!

Meanwhile, poor Eli. Eli had 72.5. George Kittle did his part with 22 and Dak Prescott added 16, but then things went sort of... Muppet-y. **Jaylen Waddle gave him 2 points**, one of the week's official busts, and Omarion Hampton added 3. And Eli played two kickers, Will Reichard (7) and Nick Folk (5.5), which is a very creative choice, like a stage show with two Swedish Chefs. The margin was **68.5 points**, the blowout of the week. The draft people graded Eli a B+ and said he bet on 2028. Well, it's still 2026, Eli's 0-3, and he slid **two spots to twelfth** at 252 cumulative. Hmmmm.

**Statler (balcony):** A B+! What's the B stand for?
**Waldorf:** "Bye week," apparently!
**Both:** Doh-ho-ho-ho!

## ACT TWO: The Heartbreak Hotel — Mike 104, Chris 103.5

Okay, folks, hold on to your felt. This is the one that had the whole backstage crew biting their fingernails. And Muppets don't even have fingernails!

**Mike beat Chris, 104 to 103.5. Point five. A half a point.** The closest game of the season so far. That's smaller than a fly. I've *eaten* things bigger than that margin!

Mike did it with a quarterback duet: Jacoby Brissett with 27 and Geno Smith with 26, both in the week's top ten. Matthew Golden added 17 and kicker Harrison Mevis kicked in 9, and I mean, every little point mattered, because Mike needed all of them. Mike's team scored 4.78 above its own average, got its first win, and moved **up one spot to eighth**. And he did it with his injury ward filling up (more on that later, in our Muppet News Flash).

Chris, oh, Chris. He scored 2.5 over his own average and still lost. Jared Goff put up 19, Harold Fannin Jr. matched him with 19, Christian McCaffrey and Michael Wilson each had 14, and the Denver Broncos Defense chipped in 8. But down at the bottom, Travis Etienne Jr., Cam Skattebo, and Tyler Warren each had 3. Here's the what-if that'll keep Chris up at night: any one of those guys finds a single extra point, just one, and it's Chris dancing across the stage instead of Mike. Remember, the draft folks gave Chris a D+ and pointed out he had *five tight ends*. Fannin, McBride, and Warren all counted this week, so the tight ends did show up. They just came up half a point short. Chris is 1-2, on a two-game losing streak, and **holds at seventh** with 303.

**Waldorf:** Half a point! I've seen bigger margins in *our* act!
**Statler:** Our act doesn't *have* a margin. Nobody's ever finished watching it!

## ACT THREE: Bruce's Big Musical Number — Bruce 138.5, Cal 93.5

And now, a song-and-dance extravaganza from Bruce! This week Bruce had three performers at 28 or more, which is more than the Electric Mayhem has *members who are awake*. **Tyler Shough 29, Bijan Robinson 28, and Bo Nix 28**, all in the league's top six. Garrett Wilson added 17 and Jeremiyah Love had 14. Total: **138.5**, second-best of the week, 22.67 above his own average, and a **45-point** win. Bruce is 2-1 and **up one spot to fourth** at 347.5.

Cal had the draft's only A grade. Everybody applauded. And now Cal is 1-2 with 93.5. It wasn't a *bad* show exactly. Ja'Marr Chase and Jordan Addison each had 14, the Washington Commanders Defense had 8, and the team was actually 6.5 over its own average. But Kyler Murray gave him just 5 at quarterback, Ashton Jeanty and Carnell Tate had 3 apiece, and you just can't win a sing-off with that against Bruce's choir. Cal still moved **up one spot to tenth** with 261, which is the kind of consolation prize you get in a box of stale popcorn.

## ACT FOUR: The Quiet Hit — Pete 122, Joe 99

Pete is the Rowlf the Dog of this league. He doesn't make a fuss. He just sits at the piano, plays, and wins. **Pete is 3-0!** The draft graded him a C+ with "the best WR room, no backfield." And this week the WR room and the quarterbacks carried the whole show: **Sam Darnold 32** (second-best in the league!), **Brock Purdy 29**, and **Jaxon Smith-Njigba 25**. Three top-ten performances from one roster! Drake London added 12. The backfield? Ty Johnson 3, Najee Harris 3. Just like the draft folks said. Pete finished with **122**, third-best of the week, 16.28 over his own average, and jumped **two spots to sixth** with 317.17. His record is running a little ahead of his cumulative total, but hey, the scoreboard doesn't ask how you felt.

Joe had 99, 11.5 below his own average. James Cook III gave him 20, Jordan Love 19, and Patrick Mahomes 16, but Jonathan Taylor and Ladd McConkey each chipped in just 3. The draft gave Joe an A-, and he's now 1-2 and **down two spots to fifth** at 331.5. The **23-point** margin was a pretty clear verdict.

## ACT FIVE: The Undefeated Twins

**Aaron 107, Matt 65.** Aaron is 3-0 and still **first** with 387 cumulative points. Here's the funny part: Aaron scored **22 below his own average** and still won by **42**! That's like forgetting half your lines and still getting a standing ovation. Josh Allen had 22 and Juwan Johnson had 19. Also, both of his defenses, the Texans and the Lions, scored 0.

And Matt... oh, Matt. **65 points, the league low**, 32.39 below his own average. Christian Watson and Bryce Young each had 14, Justin Herbert 11, but **Denzel Boston went bust with 2**, Tony Pollard had 3, and Saquon Barkley had 6. And yes, folks, Matt *still* has zero tight ends. Somebody please introduce Matt to Chris and his five tight ends. I'll host the meeting. I'll even bring snacks. Matt slipped from 2-0 to 2-1 and dropped **five spots to ninth** at 292.17.

**Dan 106.5, Mitch 94.5.** Dan's draft strategy was "it's still 2022," and, well, maybe it is! Aaron Rodgers threw for 24, Kenneth Walker III had 19, Michael Penix Jr. 16, and Travis Kelce 11. Dan is **3-0**, holds **second** at 351, and won by **12**, even though he was 10.5 below his own average. DJ Moore only had 3, but it didn't matter.

Mitch, bless him, keeps scoring and keeps losing. **94.5** points, 9.67 over his own average, ninth-best in the league this week, and still 0-3. Trevor Lawrence had 21, Lamar Jackson 19, and Tee Higgins and Terry McLaurin had 14 each. Mitch even moved **up a spot to eleventh** at 254.5. Mitch, you're the Gonzo of this league. You keep doing the stunt, and the stunt keeps landing on you. But we love you for it.
