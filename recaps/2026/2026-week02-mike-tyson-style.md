# Week 2: Everybody Had a Plan Until Sunday Punched Them in the Mouth

Listen to me. I'm gonna talk soft, because the soft voice is the one you remember when you're on the canvas looking at the lights.

Twelve guys walked into Week 2 with a plan. Draft grades in their pocket. Little spreadsheets. Confidence. That's beautiful. I love confidence. Confidence is a thing you carry right up until somebody rearranges your face. League averaged 96.54 points this week and four of six fights got decided by single digits. That ain't a league, that's an alley. Everybody's bleeding a little.

Let me size 'em up. One at a time. Like opponents.

---

## Aaron 146, Bruce 118 — The Executioner

Aaron is the scariest man in this league right now and he knows it. 146 points. That's **+6 over his own average**, which tells you something ugly: this is normal for him. He didn't have a great day. He had a Tuesday.

Josh Allen put up **40 points**. Forty. Top performance in the entire league, and the second straight week this man has walked in and detonated. Amon-Ra St. Brown added **25**, Cam Ward **22** — a second quarterback going for 22 like it's nothing — Chris Olave **14**, Dalton Kincaid **14**, Terrance Ferguson **11**, Brandon Aubrey **8**. Both his defenses, Houston and Detroit, gave him a clean **0** each and it didn't matter one bit. When you're hitting that hard, you don't need the jab.

Bruce didn't lose because he was bad. Bruce scored **118**, second-most in the league this week, **+13.5 over his own average**, and Davante Adams went for **28**, Tyler Shough **22**, Deshaun Watson **16**, Garrett Wilson **11**. That's a man who came to fight. He fought. He lost by 28 anyway. That's the cruelest thing in this sport — you train, you show up in the best shape of your life, and the other guy's just better that day. Bruce still climbed **five spots to 5th**, 209 cumulative. He's 1-1. He's fine. He just met the wrong man.

Aaron: 2-0, 280 cumulative, up two spots to **first**. Nobody else is close. Dan sits second at 244.5. That's not a lead, that's a restraining order.

---

## Matt 96.67, Mitch 76.5 — Discipline Beats Talent

Matt won by 20.17 and he did it with no tight ends. Still none. Two weeks and the man hasn't fixed it. You know what that is? That's fighting with one hand and winning anyway. Bryce Young **24**, Jalen Hurts **19**, Denzel Boston **14**, the Carolina defense **9.67**, Tetairoa McMillan **9**, Christian Watson **8**, Bucky Irving **6**. He scored **96.67 — that's 16.92 BELOW his own average** — and still walked out 2-0. A B-grade draft going undefeated on ugly wins. I respect ugly wins. Pretty don't pay.

Mitch is 0-2 and it hurts to watch. **76.5**, only 3.5 off his own average, which means this is who he is right now. Kyren Williams **14**, Lamar Jackson **11**, Sam LaPorta **11**, Dalton Schultz **9**, Chase McLaughlin **8.5**. Nothing above 14. Nothing. You can't win a fight with pillows. He's last in cumulative points at 160, down one spot to 12th, and his two losses have been the most one-sided of the whole winless crowd.

And here's the soft-spoken part: Chris is sitting on **five tight ends**. Matt has zero. Somebody pick up a phone. It's been two weeks. That's not strategy, that's stubbornness, and stubbornness gets you knocked out.

---

## Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67 — Survived on Fumes

Dan won by **2.83**. Closest thing to a death this week.

Dan scored **76.5 — that's 45.75 points below his own average**. Forty-five. He put up a league-high 168 in Week 1 and then his whole 2022 core aged out in seven days. Travis Kelce **17**, Stefon Diggs **16**, Kenneth Walker III **12**, Daniel Jones **11**, Harrison Butker **9.5**, Aaron Rodgers **6**. That's a scrapbook, not a roster. He finished **11th in weekly scoring** and still won, and still fell one spot to 2nd at 244.5.

I've seen fighters win rounds they had no business winning because the other guy was worse. Mike was worse. **73.67**, dead last in the league this week at week rank 12, **23.17 below his own average**. DeVonta Smith **17**, Joe Burrow **16**, Baker Mayfield **11**, and the New England defense **9.67** — his DST was his fourth-best scorer, which is the kind of sentence that should make a grown man quiet. De'Von Achane **3**. Justin Jefferson **3**. D'Andre Swift **3**. Matthew Golden **3**. Four threes. That's not bad luck, that's a whole week where nobody showed up.

Mike dropped **four spots to 9th**, 193.67 cumulative, 0-2 on an L2 streak. And here's the what-if that'll keep him up: **one more field goal**. Harrison Mevis kicked **2** points. One more make anywhere on that roster and Dan's cooling stack is 1-1 and the whole top of the standings looks different. That's how thin it was. 2.83.

## Joe 95, Chris 88 — The Body Shots Added Up

Seven points. Joe wins.

Patrick Mahomes **27**, Jonathan Taylor **22**, James Cook III **17**, Jordan Love **10**, Andre Szmyt **7**. Joe scored **95 — 21.25 under his own average** — and that's the thing about this week: almost nobody was good. Joe just had four guys land clean.

Chris had the third-best single performance in the league. **Jared Goff, 32 points.** Then Christian McCaffrey **16**, Malik Willis **11**, Trey McBride **8**, Tyler Warren **8**. And then the bottom fell out: Travis Etienne Jr. — **2 points**, the only bust in the league this week, the lone skill player anywhere in a scoring lineup at two or under. Two points, in a game he lost by **7**. Jadarian Price **3**, Harold Fannin Jr. **3** right behind him. That's the whole fight there. The little guys didn't answer the bell.

Joe's 1-1, 232.5, 3rd. Chris is 1-1, 199.5, 7th, riding an L1.

## Pete 90.67, Eli 88 — 2.67 and a Prayer

Pete is 2-0 and I don't think Pete sleeps well.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba went for **36** — second-highest score in the league this week — and Brock Purdy added **27**. Two men, 63 of Pete's 90.67 points. After that? Rashee Rice **6**, Andy Borregales **5**, Drake London **3**, Minnesota's defense **1.67**. That **1.67** is smaller than the margin of victory. Think about it. Pete survived by **2.67** and a defense he probably forgot he owned paid most of the rent.

Pete blew Cal out by 46.5 in Week 1 and now needs a heartbeat to get past Eli. He scored **6.92 below his own average** and stayed unbeaten. C+ draft grade. 2-0. Sitting 8th in cumulative points at 195.17 — the only unbeaten team in the bottom half of the standings. Everybody's got an opinion until the bell rings.

Eli, man. Eli's getting hit from every direction. Dak Prescott **29**, Omarion Hampton **14**, TreVeyon Henderson **14**, George Kittle **14**, Jaylen Waddle **9**. That's a real lineup. **88 points, only 1.75 under his own average** — he was exactly himself, and himself lost by 2.67. He's 0-2, down one to 10th, 179.5 cumulative, and his backfield is falling apart faster than he can replace it. Nick Folk kicked **2**, Will Reichard **6**. One better swing of a leg and he's 1-1.

That's fear, right there. Fear ain't the monster. Fear is 2.67.

## Cal 109.5, Sean 100 — The Best Loser in the League

Cal's the A-grade draft everybody mocked after Week 1. He answered. **109.5**, third-best this week, **+25.75 over his own average** — the biggest positive swing anybody posted. CeeDee Lamb **28**, Ja'Marr Chase **22**, Jake Ferguson **16**, Bhayshul Tuten **11**, and eights from MarShawn Lloyd, Braelon Allen and Jayden Daniels. He's 1-1, up one to 11th, though 167.5 cumulative says the Week 1 hole is still deep.

Sean is the saddest 0-2 I've ever seen. He scored **100 points** — fourth-best week in the league — and lost by 9.5. Matthew Stafford **32**, Kirk Cousins **24**, Jahmyr Gibbs **14**, Derrick Henry **11**, Chuba Hubbard **11**. A quarterback room held together with tape and prayer put up 56 by itself. A--grade draft, 100-point week, L2 streak. He sits 6th at 209 cumulative, dead even with Bruce — and ahead of Chris (199.5), Pete (195.17) and Cal (167.5), every one of whom owns a win he doesn't.

Sean, listen to me carefully: you're not doing anything wrong. The schedule is just punching you in the mouth. That happens. Keep your hands up.
