# PFL Week 1: I Ate the Whole Thing

So fantasy football is back. Which means twelve grown men have once again agreed to spend four months yelling at other people's knees.

*"Why is he doing fantasy football? He looks like he's never seen a football. He looks like he's made of bread."*

That's fair. I am a pale indoor man who owns a recliner with a personality. But I watched Week 1, and I have notes. Mostly food-shaped notes. The league averaged **111.58 points** this week, which is the fantasy equivalent of a medium portion, and roughly half the league looked at that number and said "yeah, I'll have the large."

## Dan 168, Joe 137.5 — the buffet game

Here's the highest-scoring game of the week, and the thing nobody wants to admit about buffets: it's not about quality, it's about volume, and Dan showed up with a plate the size of a hubcap.

**Caleb Williams put up 38 points.** That's the co-top score in the entire league. Then **Kenneth Walker III went for 28**, then **David Montgomery for 27** — three of the week's top ten performances on one roster. Dan drafted three running backs' worth of running back and then ate all of them. **DJ Moore 17. Javonte Williams 16. Stefon Diggs 11. Aaron Rodgers 11. Deebo Samuel Sr. 8.** That's **168**, best in the league.

The draft recap graded Dan a **C+** and said he "bet on 2022." Well, 2022 called, and 2022 brought snacks.

*"Wait, so the bad team won?"*

Yes. And it gets worse. Joe — graded **A-**, the "adult build," stars early and starters late — scored **137.5**, the *second-most points in the entire league*, and lost by **30.5**. **Patrick Mahomes 24. Jonathan Taylor 22. Jordan Love 22. Isaiah Likely 22.** Four guys at 22-plus. **Ladd McConkey 14. Demarcus Robinson 11.** Any other week that's a win and a nap. This week it's a participation ribbon.

That's fantasy football. You make a beautiful lasagna, you carry it to the table with the little mitts, and the schedule says "we're having cereal." Joe is 0-1 and ranked **2nd in cumulative points**. He is the richest man in the loss column.

## Cal 58, Pete 104.5 — the blowout, or, I don't want to talk about it

**46.5 points.** The biggest margin of the week. Pete got graded **C+** with the scouting report "best WR room, no backfield," and then went out and just... used the receivers. **Zay Flowers 20. Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17. Rashee Rice 8.** Add **Brock Purdy 21** and **C.J. Stroud 19**, sprinkle in **Jacory Croskey-Merritt 11**, and you've got **104.5** and a statement.

And Cal. Oh, Cal. Cal got the only **A** in the draft. The A. The one everybody nodded at. And Cal scored **58**, which is not a fantasy score, that's a bowling score. That's a *decent* bowling score, which makes it sadder.

**Ashton Jeanty put up 25**, basically half the team's output, which is the football version of one guy at a potluck bringing the entire meal while eleven people bring napkins. **Jayden Daniels 10. CeeDee Lamb 8. Jake Elliott 3.5. Bhayshul Tuten 3. Rome Odunze 3. Tyler Allgeier 3. Drew Stevens 2.5.** Cal sits **12th** in cumulative points at **58**, which is 110 points behind Dan after one week. One week!

*"Maybe the A was for effort."*

## Aaron 134, Sean 109 — dueling 38s and a kicker convention

This was the shootout that wasn't, because both guys brought a gun and only one guy brought friends.

**Josh Allen: 38 points.** Tied with Caleb Williams for the top score in the league. But Aaron didn't stop there — **Jalen Coker 25**, which I had to read twice, **Amon-Ra St. Brown 19, Chris Olave 12, Chase Brown 11, Juwan Johnson 11, Dalton Kincaid 9**. That's depth. That's a **B+** roster playing like it read the grade and got offended. **134.**

Sean, graded **A-**, bet on running backs in a quarterback league, and the running backs *delivered* — **Derrick Henry 33** and **Jahmyr Gibbs 28**, both top-ten performances, plus **Chuba Hubbard 16**. That is a gorgeous backfield. And it wasn't enough, because the rest of the plate was **Kirk Cousins 15**, the **Tampa Bay Buccaneers Defense 8**, and then two kickers — **Trey Smack 6.5** and **Wil Lutz 2.5** — which is a sentence I never expected to type about a team with an A-minus next to its name. **109**, and a loss by **25**.

What if Sean had one more quarterback game instead of the second kicker? What if. That's the whole hobby. "What if" and a bowl of chips.

## Mike 120, Matt 130.5 — the man with no tight ends

Matt drafted nineteen players and zero tight ends. Zero. The draft recap flagged it: *someone will charge him for one.* And then Matt scored **130.5** without one, which is the fantasy equivalent of making a grilled cheese with no cheese and it's somehow delicious and now I'm upset.

**Bryce Young 35** — third-highest score in the league. **Christian Watson 25. Jalen Hurts 21. Nico Collins 14. Denzel Boston 11.** Even **Jason Myers at 4.5** showed up to help. Matt wins by **10.5** and sits **4th**.

Mike lost, but not because Mike was bad. **D'Andre Swift went for 33**, fourth-best performance in the league. **Justin Jefferson 24. Joe Burrow 14. Baker Mayfield 14. Parker Washington 14. Jordan Mason 11.** That's **120** points and a **B-** roster acting like a contender. Then there's the kicker line: **Cameron Dicker 1**. One. In a game decided by 10.5. Mike, I'm not saying it cost you. I'm saying the number is sitting right there like the last piece of cake nobody will claim.

## Eli 91.5, Chris 111.5 — five tight ends walk into a bar

Chris got a **D+** at the draft. A D-plus! With five tight ends and two receivers — a "broken shape," they called it. And then Chris played three tight ends and won by **20**.

**Dallas Goedert 22. Trey McBride 14. Tyler Warren 8.** Plus **Jared Goff 16, Cam Skattebo 14, Malik Willis 14, Evan McPherson 9.5**, and the **San Francisco 49ers Defense 5**. It's not pretty. It's a casserole. But you know what? Casserole feeds people. **111.5**, and Chris is **1-0** while the A-graded teams file insurance claims.

Eli, the **B+** who "bet on 2028," got a nice glimpse of the future — **Jaxson Dart 24, Kyle Monangai 17, Dak Prescott 16** — and a fairly rough look at the present. **Omarion Hampton 8. Jakobi Meyers 8. Pittsburgh Steelers Defense 8. Nick Folk 4.5. Puka Nacua 3. Alec Pierce 3.** That's **91.5**, ninth in points, 0-1.

## Mitch 83.5, Bruce 91 — the only game that made anyone sweat

One game all week finished inside a single score. **7.5 points.** Everything else was a comfortable couch; this was the folding chair at the kids' table.

Bruce spent the draft passing on nine first-round quarterbacks and then drafting Nix, Shough and Watson — the "QB guilt" arc. And Bruce's Week 1 was saved by, I swear to you, **Tyler Shough putting up 30 points**, sixth-best in the league. **Bijan Robinson added 20. Deshaun Watson 11. Jeremiyah Love 8. Aaron Jones Sr. 8. Garrett Wilson 6. Chris Boswell 5. Emeka Egbuka 3.** Total: **91**. That is a win built entirely out of the guy nobody wanted. That's a Hot Pocket winning a cooking competition.

Mitch (**B** grade) got **Trevor Lawrence 26** and **Lamar Jackson 22** and **Breece Hall 17** and still landed at **83.5**, eleventh in the league. Eight players counted. Eight. **Kyren Williams 8, Tyler Bass 7.5, Tee Higgins 3**, two defenses at zero. When the top of your lineup is that good and the bottom is that quiet, you lose by 7.5 and you think about it during dinner. And during dessert. And at 2 a.m.

## Where it all leaves us

**Dan's 1-0 and on top with 168.** **Joe's 0-1 and second with 137.5** — the cruelest two rows in any spreadsheet anywhere. Then **Aaron (1-0, 134)**, **Matt (1-0, 130.5)**, **Mike (0-1, 120)**, **Chris (1-0, 111.5)**, **Sean (0-1, 109)**, **Pete (1-0, 104.5)**, **Eli (0-1, 91.5)**, **Bruce (1-0, 91)**, **Mitch (0-1, 83.5)**, and **Cal (0-1, 58)**.

Nobody moved in the rankings, because there was nowhere to move from. This is the one week all year where every arrow points at itself.

One week in and the league has already proven its thesis: the food you planned is never the food you get.

*"So what's the lesson?"*

The lesson is Tyler Shough scored 30 points. There is no lesson. Eat the cake.
