# The Half Point: A PFL Week 3 Recap About Nothing

*(Slap bass. Brick wall. A man in a blazer grabs the microphone.)*

---

## COLD OPEN: The Comedy Club

What is the *deal* with the half point?

Who needs a half point? You go to the store, you ask for half a sandwich, the guy looks at you like you're crazy. But in fantasy football, a half point is a whole thing. It's a *field goal kicker* thing. Kickers are the only people in America who still deal in half-points. They're like the last guys paying in quarters at the laundromat.

And this week, a half point decided an entire football game. **Mike 104, Chris 103.5.** Margin: **0.5.** That's not a win. That's a rounding error with a trophy.

So tonight, folks, we're doing a show about nothing. And by "nothing," I mean half of one point. We'll get there. First, let me tell you about everybody else.

*(Slap bass.)*

---

## SCENE ONE: Jerry's Apartment — "The Sponge-Worthy Running Back"

**Sean 141, Eli 72.5**

Sean walks in. He's 0-2. He's been sitting on the couch for two weeks like George after he got fired from the Yankees. Nobody's talking to him. The draft grades said A-minus, and what does he have? Nothing. Zero wins. He's the guy who goes to the gym every day and never loses a pound.

And then, Week 3 — **141 points.** The league-high. **+24.33 vs his own average.** He's up three spots to **third**, **350** cumulative, **1-2.**

And the centerpiece of the whole thing? **Jahmyr Gibbs, 33 points.** Top performance in the entire league. You know how Elaine had to decide whether a guy was "sponge-worthy"? Gibbs is sponge-worthy. Gibbs is *case*-worthy. Sean went down to the pharmacy and bought the whole shelf.

But here's the thing everyone forgot about: Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league. People laughed. "Running backs? In *this* economy?" And yet the quarterbacks showed up too: **Matthew Stafford 22, Kirk Cousins 21.** Kirk Cousins! That's like finding out your old gym teacher can play the cello. Throw in **Derrick Henry 22** and **Brock Bowers 17**, and that's not a lineup, that's a buffet.

Now, Eli. Poor Eli. B-plus in the draft, "betting on 2028," and it is still very much 2026. **72.5**, **0-3**, down two spots to **12th** with **252** cumulative. He had **George Kittle 22** and **Dak Prescott 16**, and after that it gets thin. Then there's the bust: **Jaylen Waddle, 2 points.** Two! Waddle. The name is already a joke. You name a guy Waddle, you can't act surprised when he doesn't get anywhere.

And Eli's lineup counted **two kickers**: **Will Reichard 7** and **Nick Folk 5.5.** Two kickers! That's the double dip! You don't take the chip, dip it, bite it, and dip it *again!* Margin: **68.5.** Blowout of the week. Eli's in the soup line and the man behind the counter says: *No wins for you.*

---

## SCENE TWO: Monk's Café — "The Man Who Won Ugly"

**Aaron 107, Matt 65**

Aaron is **3-0**. First in the league. **387** cumulative. And this week he played badly, **107, which is -22 vs his own average**, and he *still* won by **42.** That's the guy who shows up to the party in sweatpants and still goes home with the girl. You hate him. You also kind of want to be him.

**Josh Allen 22. Juwan Johnson 19.** Juwan Johnson at tight end! Which brings us to Matt.

Matt has **no tight ends.** Zero. Since the draft. Everybody's been telling him. It's like Kramer walking around without a wallet. Where do you keep your stuff? How do you *live*?

**65 points.** League low. **-32.39 vs his own average.** He dropped **five spots**, all the way to **9th**. He's **2-1**, and those two early wins are the only thing propping him up, like a bad sofa you won't throw out because you already paid for it.

**Christian Watson 14, Bryce Young 14**, and then **Saquon Barkley 6.** And the bust, **Denzel Boston, 2 points.** Meanwhile Chris has *five* tight ends and two receivers. These two should trade. It's the easiest deal in the world! Matt needs a tight end, Chris has a tight end *surplus*. It's like one guy with no bread and one guy with all the bread and they won't talk because of some thing that happened at a wedding.

---

## SCENE THREE: The Coffee Shop Booth — "The Half Point"

**Mike 104, Chris 103.5**

Here's our show about nothing. Closest game of the season.

Chris puts up **103.5.** It's a nice number. It's actually above his own average, **+2.5.** **Jared Goff 19. Harold Fannin Jr. 19**, one of the famous five tight ends, finally paying rent. **Christian McCaffrey 14, Michael Wilson 14.** The **Denver Broncos Defense** chips in **8**, which for a defense this week is basically a hat trick.

But then you look at the rest of the tight-end collection. **Trey McBride 6. Tyler Warren 3.** He's got tight ends like Jerry has cereal. Big shelf, lots of boxes, and he only ever eats a couple.

And Mike trots out a quarterback room nobody was writing sitcoms about: **Jacoby Brissett 27, Geno Smith 26.** Both in the league's top eight performances. That's like losing your keys and finding two better sets in the couch.

**Matthew Golden 17.** And then, the kicker. Literally, the kicker. **Harrison Mevis, 9 points.** Chris's kicker, **Evan McPherson: 5.5.**

A half-point loss is the worst kind of loss. At least a blowout you can walk away from. A half point stays with you. It's the low-talker of losses. You didn't hear it, you just nodded along, and now you're wearing the puffy shirt. Any half a point anywhere in that Chris lineup, one more catch from a tight end, *any* of the five, and we're telling a different story.

Chris is **1-2** with a **two-game losing streak**, holding at **7th** with **303.** Mike is **1-2**, snaps out of it with his first win, up one to **8th** with **297.67.** And Mike's not out of the woods: his name is all over the injury report at the bottom of this thing. Serenity now.

---

## SCENE FOUR: Kramer Bursts In — "The Quiet Unbeaten"

**Pete 122, Joe 99**

*(Door flies open. Kramer slides in.)*

Pete! **Pete is 3-0!** The draft said C-plus! Dan got a C-plus too, and *he's* 3-0! Nobody cares about grades when you're *winning*, Jerry!

**Sam Darnold 32.** Second-best score in the league. **Brock Purdy 29. Jaxon Smith-Njigba 25.** The best receiver room in the league, no backfield to speak of (**Ty Johnson 3, Najee Harris 3**), and it doesn't matter. Pete's the guy who doesn't own a car and still gets everywhere on time.

**122**, **+16.28 vs his own average.** Up two spots to **6th**, but only **317.17** cumulative. The record's ahead of the scoring. It's like having a great job title and a tiny apartment.

Joe, meanwhile. A-minus draft. **99 points**, **-11.5 vs his own average**, and down two to **5th** with **331.5.** **James Cook III 20, Jordan Love 19, Patrick Mahomes 16.** Respectable! But then **Jonathan Taylor 3** and **Ladd McConkey 3.** Two threes. That's not a lineup, that's a shrug. Margin: **23.**

---

## SCENE FIVE: George's Parents' House — "The Surge"

**Bruce 138.5, Cal 93.5**

Serenity NOW, Cal!

Cal got an **A** in the draft. The top grade. And he's **1-2.** That's George getting into a fancy private school and then getting kicked out for something with a sandwich.

Bruce, another C-plus, went out and scored **138.5**, second-best of the week, **+22.67 vs his own average.** **Tyler Shough 29, Bijan Robinson 28, Bo Nix 28.** Three guys at 28-plus! Then **Garrett Wilson 17** and **Jeremiyah Love 14.** Bruce is **2-1**, up one spot to **4th**, **347.5** cumulative. And look at the top: **Aaron 387, Dan 351, Sean 350, Bruce 347.5.** That's not a race anymore, that's a crowded elevator.

Cal did the thing where you do everything right and it still doesn't work. **Ja'Marr Chase 14, Jordan Addison 14, CeeDee Lamb 11.** The **Washington Commanders Defense** gave him **8.** But **Kyler Murray, 5** points at quarterback. In *this* league. And **Ashton Jeanty 3.** Cal actually *rose* a spot to **10th** (**261**, **+6.5 vs his own average**) while losing by **45.** That's a very Seinfeld thing to happen. Worse day, better standing.

---

## SCENE SIX: Newman's Apartment — "The Old Guys"

**Dan 106.5, Mitch 94.5**

*(Knock knock.)* "Hello... Mitch."

Mitch is **0-3.** But here's the strange part: Mitch keeps *scoring.* **94.5**, **+9.67 vs his own average.** He's up one spot to **11th** (**254.5**). He's the guy who keeps going on great first dates and never gets a callback. **Trevor Lawrence 21, Lamar Jackson 19, Tee Higgins 14, Terry McLaurin 14.** That's a real lineup! It's just that someone always shows up with a better one.

This week that someone was Dan. Dan bet on 2022, and it's apparently still 2022 in Dan's house. **Aaron Rodgers 24**, tenth-best performance in the league. **Kenneth Walker III 19, Michael Penix Jr. 16**, **Javonte Williams 14, Deebo Samuel Sr. 14**, and **Travis Kelce 11.** Dan is **3-0**, **2nd**, **351** cumulative, and he did it with **106.5**, **-10.5 vs his own average.** Margin: **12.** Not pretty. Doesn't have to be. Like Jerry dating a woman just for her apartment, it's not about love, it's about results.

---

## CLOSING STAND-UP

So three weeks in: **Aaron, Dan, and Pete, 3-0.** All three with **W3** streaks. **Mitch and Eli, 0-3**, both with **L3** streaks. The C-plus guys are unbeaten, the A guys are sweating, and a game was decided by a kicker's half point.

Which is exactly what I've been saying. It's a show about *nothing.* And nothing turns out to be worth half a point.

*(Slap bass. Fade out.)*
