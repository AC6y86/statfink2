# The Herd: The Peninsula Draft, Roster by Roster

So I sat with the Peninsula draft board this morning. Had my coffee. And here's what I've learned over twenty years of doing this: anybody can make a first-round pick. The first round is a party. Rounds 6 through 19 are the marriage. That's where you find out who actually built something and who just showed up with a nice haircut.

Nineteen rounds. Twelve teams. Let's go through every roster, top to bottom, and I'm going to give each one a grade. Not a "did you get a good player" grade. A "did you build a team" grade. There's a difference.

## Aaron

QB: Josh Allen, Cam Ward. RB: Chase Brown, Jaylen Warren, Rico Dowdle, Blake Corum, Woody Marks. WR: Amon-Ra St. Brown, Chris Olave, Malik Nabers, Luther Burden III, Jalen Coker. TE: Dalton Kincaid, Juwan Johnson, Terrance Ferguson. K: Brandon Aubrey, Cam Little. D: Texans, Lions.

Here's what Aaron did. He took the best quarterback in football at one, and then he built a receiver room that's young, fast, and going to be around for a decade. Amon-Ra, Nabers, Olave. That's a real room. The running backs are a committee. Chase Brown's the lead, everybody behind him is a guy who needs an injury to matter. And then Aaron took a kicker in round 13 and another kicker in round 17, which tells me Aaron is the guy at the airport three hours early. He's not wrong. He's just not fun. Solid foundation, thin backfield, a little too much insurance.

**Grade: B+.** The Volvo. Safe, well made, nobody's jealous.

## Sean

QB: Matthew Stafford, Kirk Cousins, Tua Tagovailoa. RB: Jahmyr Gibbs, Derrick Henry, Isiah Pacheco, Chuba Hubbard, Alvin Kamara, Raheem Mostert. WR: Marvin Harrison Jr., DK Metcalf, Brian Thomas Jr., Rashid Shaheed, Josh Downs. TE: Brock Bowers. K: Wil Lutz, Trey Smack. D: Bills, Bucs.

Sean is the only guy in this league who watched nine quarterbacks go in the first round and said, "I'll pass." He took Gibbs at two and Henry in the third and just kept taking running backs. Six of them. Then he got Brock Bowers in round NINE, which, I'm sorry, is a crime. Now, the quarterback room. Stafford, Cousins, Tua. The combined age of that room is roughly the Roman Empire. Those are three guys who can absolutely throw for 300 yards on a Sunday and absolutely retire on a Tuesday. Sean drafted Raheem Mostert, who the board lists as a free agent, so one of those six running backs doesn't currently have an employer. But the construction is real. Elite backfield, elite tight end, serviceable receivers, and quarterbacks held together with tape and prayer.

**Grade: A-.** A great steakhouse with an old wait staff.

## Mitch

QB: Lamar Jackson, Trevor Lawrence. RB: Kyren Williams, Breece Hall, RJ Harvey, Nicholas Singleton, Adam Randall. WR: Tee Higgins, Terry McLaurin, Jerry Jeudy, Xavier Worthy, Makai Lemon, De'Zhaun Stribling. TE: Sam LaPorta, Dalton Schultz. K: Chase McLaughlin, Tyler Bass. D: Giants, Cowboys.

This is the most balanced roster on the board, and I mean that as a compliment and a warning. Two quarterbacks, five backs, six receivers, two tight ends. Every position group is fine. Not one of them is elite except the guy under center. Lamar is a superstar. After that, Mitch's roster is a very good hotel chain. Kyren, Breece, Higgins, McLaurin, LaPorta. You've stayed there. It was clean. You don't remember the name. The thing I'd worry about is the receivers. Six of them and not one true WR1. Tee Higgins is the best of the bunch, and Tee Higgins is a great WR2. That's the ceiling on this team.

**Grade: B.** Marriott. Consistent. Never the story.

## Bruce

QB: Bo Nix, Tyler Shough, Deshaun Watson. RB: Bijan Robinson, Jeremiyah Love, Aaron Jones, Jonah Coleman, Tank Bigsby. WR: Davante Adams, Garrett Wilson, Emeka Egbuka, Courtland Sutton, KC Concepcion, Zavion Thomas. TE: Colston Loveland. K: Chris Boswell, Joey Slye. D: Ravens, Browns.

Bruce took Bijan at four and Jeremiyah Love in the second, and for about ninety minutes he had the best draft in the league. Then he took Bo Nix in round three, which is fine, and then Tyler Shough in round six, which is a question, and then Deshaun Watson in round 13, which is a cry for help. Bruce, in a league where nine quarterbacks went in round one, you passed on all of them and then drafted three of them later like you were trying to make up for it. That's not a plan. That's guilt. The receivers are veterans. Davante, Garrett Wilson, Sutton. They'll catch the ball. Whether anyone throws it to them well is the whole season.

**Grade: C+.** Beautiful house, questionable plumbing.

## Mike

QB: Joe Burrow, Baker Mayfield, Jacoby Brissett, Geno Smith. RB: De'Von Achane, D'Andre Swift, Jordan Mason, Rachaad White, Tyjae Spears, James Conner. WR: Justin Jefferson, DeVonta Smith, Parker Washington, Matthew Golden. TE: Tucker Kraft. K: Cameron Dicker, Harrison Mevis. D: Patriots, Saints.

Mike drafted four quarterbacks. Four. Burrow and Baker are a great one-two. Brissett and Geno are two roster spots Mike lit on fire in a parking lot. And here's the thing, it cost him. Look at that receiver room. Justin Jefferson, DeVonta Smith, and then Parker Washington and Matthew Golden. That's a Ferrari, a nice Lexus, and two rental cars. If Jefferson misses a month, Mike's starting lineup gets very quiet very fast. The running backs are deep but they're all the same guy. Six backs who'd be great in a committee and none you'd bet the house on. Mike drafted like a guy who's afraid of injuries at quarterback and not afraid of them anywhere else.

**Grade: B-.** Top heavy. Great quarterback, great receiver, and then a long hallway.

## Matt

QB: Jalen Hurts, Justin Herbert, Bryce Young. RB: Saquon Barkley, Bucky Irving, Tony Pollard, Kenneth Gainwell, Keaton Mitchell. WR: Nico Collins, Tetairoa McMillan, Christian Watson, Chris Godwin Jr., Quentin Johnston, Jalen Nailor, Denzel Boston. TE: None. K: Jason Myers, Jake Bates. D: Packers, Panthers.

Matt drafted nineteen players and zero tight ends. Zero. Not a bad one. Not a backup. None. Now, I've been doing this a long time, and I've seen guys punt on kickers, I've seen guys punt on defense. I have never seen a man look at nineteen rounds and say, "tight end is a myth." And here's the thing, the rest of the roster is legitimately good! Hurts and Herbert is the best quarterback pairing in the league. Saquon and Bucky Irving is a great backfield. Nico Collins is a WR1. Matt built a beautiful house and forgot to put in a bathroom. Somebody is going to have to trade him a tight end and they're going to charge him for it.

**Grade: B.** Would be an A. Nobody told him about the position.

## Joe

QB: Patrick Mahomes, Jordan Love. RB: Jonathan Taylor, James Cook III, Quinshon Judkins, Chris Rodriguez Jr., Brian Robinson. WR: A.J. Brown, Jameson Williams, Ladd McConkey, Mike Evans, Michael Pittman Jr., Romeo Doubs, Demarcus Robinson. TE: Isaiah Likely. K: Tyler Loop, Andre Szmyt. D: Jaguars, Chiefs.

Here's how the adults draft. Joe took Jonathan Taylor at seven, James Cook at 18, and then Mahomes fell to him at 31 and he didn't overthink it. That's three cornerstones in three rounds, and then Joe did the boring, smart thing: he took a wide receiver in almost every round after that. A.J. Brown, Jameson Williams, McConkey, Mike Evans, Pittman. Seven receivers. Not one of them is a superstar in 2026, but five of them are starters, and that's how you survive a season. The tight end spot is Isaiah Likely and a shrug. The backup quarterback is Jordan Love, who is a very good backup quarterback and a very expensive one. This is the Kansas City roster. Quiet. Deep. Built to be annoying in December.

**Grade: A-.** No glaring hole, no glaring star past round three. That's a playoff team.

## Dan

QB: Caleb Williams, Daniel Jones, Aaron Rodgers. RB: Kenneth Walker, Javonte Williams, David Montgomery, J.K. Dobbins, Kaelon Black. WR: DJ Moore, Stefon Diggs, Deebo Samuel Sr., Khalil Shakir, Wan'Dale Robinson, Troy Franklin. TE: Travis Kelce. K: Harrison Butker, Cairo Santos. D: Seahawks, Chargers.

Dan's roster is the 2022 Pro Bowl. Diggs, Deebo, Kelce, Rodgers, Montgomery, Javonte. Every one of those guys was a household name, and every one of them has a birthday coming up that they'd rather not talk about. Caleb Williams is the one bet on the future, and it's a real bet. Here's my problem. There's no youth in the receiver room. There's no youth in the backfield. Dan drafted a team that would've won this league three years ago, and I say that with affection because I own a lot of things that were great three years ago. Kelce in round 14 is still Kelce. Rodgers in round 12 is still, technically, Rodgers.

**Grade: C+.** A great classic rock station. You know every song. None of them are new.

## Eli

QB: Dak Prescott, Jaxson Dart. RB: Omarion Hampton, TreVeyon Henderson, Jonathon Brooks, Kyle Monangai, Zach Charbonnet, Justice Hill. WR: Puka Nacua, George Pickens, Jaylen Waddle, Alec Pierce, Jakobi Meyers. TE: George Kittle, Mark Andrews. K: Will Reichard, Nick Folk. D: Rams, Steelers.

Eli drafted like a venture capitalist. Hampton, TreVeyon Henderson, Jonathon Brooks, Monangai, Jaxson Dart. Young, cheap, high upside, and about half of them are going to be on the waiver wire by Halloween. But the top of this roster is legitimately strong. Dak's a top-ten quarterback in a good year. Puka is a top-five receiver in any year. Pickens and Waddle are real. And then Eli took George Kittle in round 11 and Mark Andrews in round 14, which means he's got two starting tight ends while Matt has none. That's not luck. That's reading the room. The backfield is six lottery tickets. If two of them hit, this is a top-three team.

**Grade: B+.** High ceiling, mushy floor, and the best tight end room in the league.

## Chris

QB: Drake Maye, Jared Goff, Malik Willis. RB: Christian McCaffrey, Travis Etienne Jr., Cam Skattebo, Jadarian Price, Josh Jacobs. WR: Michael Wilson, Ja'Kobi Lane. TE: Trey McBride, Tyler Warren, Harold Fannin Jr., Kyle Pitts Sr., Dallas Goedert. K: Ka'imi Fairbairn, Evan McPherson. D: Broncos, 49ers.

Okay. I need everybody to sit down for this one. Chris drafted five tight ends and two wide receivers. Two! Michael Wilson and Ja'Kobi Lane. That's the entire receiver room. Meanwhile there are five tight ends on this roster, which is more tight ends than some NFL teams carry. Here's the thing, and I want to be fair: Drake Maye and McCaffrey is a fantastic start. Trey McBride is a top-three tight end. Josh Jacobs in round 13 is either a genius pick or a sign that Chris stepped away and the autodraft has a strange sense of humor. But you cannot play five tight ends. You can play one, maybe two. Chris built a roster where three of his best players can't be in the lineup at the same time. That's not a team. That's a very specific collection.

**Grade: D+.** Elite pieces, broken shape. Somebody's going to fleece him for a tight end and he's going to be thrilled.

## Cal

QB: Jayden Daniels, Kyler Murray. RB: Ashton Jeanty, Bhayshul Tuten, MarShawn Lloyd, Tyler Allgeier, Mike Washington Jr., Braelon Allen, Dylan Sampson. WR: Ja'Marr Chase, CeeDee Lamb, Rome Odunze, Carnell Tate, Jordan Addison. TE: Jake Ferguson. K: Jake Elliott, Drew Stevens. D: Eagles, Commanders.

Cal drafted the best top four in the league. Jayden Daniels, Ja'Marr Chase, CeeDee Lamb, Ashton Jeanty. That's a franchise quarterback, two of the five best receivers alive, and the most exciting young back in football. And then Cal did something I really respect. He took seven running backs, and six of them are backups on their NFL teams. Tuten, Lloyd, Allgeier, Washington, Braelon Allen, Sampson. That's not a backfield. That's a handcuff collection. Cal drafted every single guy who becomes a star the moment someone else gets hurt. It's cold. It's smart. Kyler Murray in round six as the backup is the best insurance policy anyone bought. The tight end is Jake Ferguson in round 15, which is a shrug, but Cal doesn't need a tight end when he's got Chase and Lamb.

**Grade: A.** Elite top, ruthless bottom. This is the team I'd least want to play.

## Pete

QB: Brock Purdy, C.J. Stroud, Sam Darnold. RB: Rhamondre Stevenson, Jacory Croskey-Merritt, Ty Johnson, Najee Harris, Connor Heyward. WR: Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Drake London, Zay Flowers, Rashee Rice, Jayden Reed, Cooper Kupp. TE: Hunter Henry. K: Eddy Piñeiro, Andy Borregales. D: Vikings, Bears.

Pete had the last pick in round one and the first pick in round two, and he used them on Purdy and JSN. Then Drake London, then Zay Flowers, then Rashee Rice. Pete built a wide receiver room that could start for most teams in this league. And then he got to running back and, I'm going to be honest, it looks like he lost interest. Rhamondre Stevenson in round five is fine. Croskey-Merritt and Ty Johnson and Connor Heyward is a group of guys you'd struggle to pick out of a lineup. Najee Harris in round 12 might be the best back on the roster and that's a sentence that should worry Pete. Three quarterbacks is one too many. Hunter Henry with the very last pick of the draft is a tight end in the way that a folding chair is furniture.

**Grade: C+.** Best receiver room in the league. Nobody to hand the ball to.

## The Big Picture

Here's what I keep coming back to. The two best-built rosters, Cal and Joe, didn't do anything crazy. They took stars early and starters late and never fell in love with a position. The two most interesting rosters, Chris and Matt, each ignored an entire position, one by drafting too much of it and one by drafting none of it. Those two should be on the phone with each other by Monday.

Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league and might be right. Bruce bet on three mediocre quarterbacks and is probably wrong. Dan bet on 2022. Eli bet on 2028. Everybody else is somewhere in the middle, which is where most people live.

And I'll say this, I say it every year: the draft is a Tuesday. The season is a Sunday. Nobody remembers the draft grades. They remember the standings.
