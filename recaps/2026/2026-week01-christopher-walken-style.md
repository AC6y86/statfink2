# Week One. Wow. — The PFL, As Explained By A Man In A Folding Chair

*(Read this slowly. With pauses. Where there shouldn't be any.)*

So.

Here we are. Week... *One*. The season, it's a baby. It doesn't walk yet. It doesn't talk. It just sits there and it *scores* — 111.58 points on average, which is the league average, which is a number I find... comforting. Like a warm towel.

But nobody wants the average. Nobody comes to my house and says, Chris, tell me about the *average*.

They want the 168.

## Dan 168, Joe 137.5 — The Big One

Dan. C+ in the draft. A *C plus*. Somebody looked at his roster, the Diggs, the Deebo, the Rodgers, the whole 2022 of it all, and said: fine. Acceptable. Slightly above... acceptable.

Then Dan puts up **168**. The most points in the league. And he does it with a *backfield*, baby. Caleb Williams, quarterback, **38 points**. Kenneth Walker III, **28**. David Montgomery, **27**. Three men. Top ten in the whole league. All in one house. That's not a lineup, that's a *family reunion* and everybody brought a dish.

DJ Moore chips in 17. Javonte Williams, 16. Diggs, 11 — the old man still has it, he still has the... thing. Rodgers, 11. Deebo, 8. Cairo Santos with the 6. Two defenses, Seattle and the Chargers, zero and zero, and you know what? When you've got 168 you don't ask the defenses for anything. You let them sit. You let them enjoy the game.

And across the table: Joe. **137.5**. Second-most points in the *entire league*. An A- draft grade. The "adult" build — stars early, starters late, seven starters at receiver on the bench, very grown-up, very sensible, like a man who irons his socks.

Mahomes, 24. Jonathan Taylor, 22. Jordan Love, 22. Isaiah Likely, 22 — a tight end doing *tight end things*. Ladd McConkey 14. Demarcus Robinson 11. Mike Evans 8. Tyler Loop 6.5. The Chiefs defense, 5.

One hundred and thirty-seven and a half points... and a **loss**. Zero and one. That's the cruelty. That's the little... the little *worm* in the apple. You can be the second-best team in the league on Sunday and go home with nothing but a hat.

## Cal 58, Pete 104.5 — The Demolition

Now. This one hurt to watch. I had to look away. I looked at a lamp.

Cal was the **A**. The best grade in the draft. The adult in the room. And Cal scored **58 points**. Fifty-eight! Ashton Jeanty did his job — **25** — and then, you know, the rest of the room went quiet. Jayden Daniels, 10. CeeDee Lamb, 8. Jake Elliott 3.5. Bhayshul Tuten 3, Rome Odunze 3, Tyler Allgeier 3, Drew Stevens 2.5. Eagles defense, zero. Commanders defense, zero.

Pete — C+, "best WR room, no backfield" — wins by **46.5**. The biggest gap in the league. Brock Purdy 21, Zay Flowers 20, C.J. Stroud 19, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17, Jacory Croskey-Merritt 11, Rashee Rice 8, Eddy Pineiro 5.5, Rhamondre Stevenson 3. A hundred and four and a half. Efficient. Tidy. Like a man who packs a suitcase with *tissue paper*.

Here's the thing about a 46.5-point win. It doesn't *feel* like a game. It feels like weather.

## Mitch 83.5, Bruce 91 — The Only One That Breathed

The only game all week inside a single score. **Seven and a half points.** That's it. That's the whole margin. You could lose that in a *couch*.

Bruce — the guy with the QB guilt, the man who passed on nine first-round quarterbacks and then went and took Nix and Shough and Watson like a man ordering the third-cheapest wine — gets **30 points from Tyler Shough**. Thirty! From *Shough*! Bijan Robinson 20, Watson 11, Jeremiyah Love 8, Aaron Jones Sr. 8, Garrett Wilson 6, Boswell 5, Egbuka 3. **91.**

Mitch: Trevor Lawrence 26, Lamar Jackson 22, Breece Hall 17, Kyren Williams 8, Tyler Bass 7.5, Tee Higgins 3, two defenses at zero. **83.5.** And a loss.

What if Higgins goes for 11 instead of 3? What if one of those zeroes is a *whisper*? Mitch wins. Mitch is 1-0 and I'm writing a different paragraph and everybody's happy. But no. Seven and a half. The little man in the... the little man doesn't care about your feelings.

## Aaron 134, Sean 109 — The Shootout

Josh Allen. **38 points.** Tied for the best score of the week with Caleb Williams, which is — and I want to be clear about this — *the exact same number*. Thirty-eight and thirty-eight. Two men, different rooms, identical thunder.

And Aaron, the B+, the guy who drafted *two kickers* — he only needed one, Cam Little, 6 — got **25 from Jalen Coker**, 19 from Amon-Ra St. Brown, 12 from Chris Olave, 11 from Chase Brown, 11 from Juwan Johnson, 9 from Dalton Kincaid, 3 from Blake Corum. **134.**

Sean, the A-, the man who bet on running backs in a quarterback league — and the backs *showed up*. Derrick Henry, **33**. Jahmyr Gibbs, **28**. Chuba Hubbard 16. Tampa's defense, 8. Two kickers of his own, Smack 6.5 and Lutz 2.5. And the quarterback room held together with tape and prayer? Kirk Cousins. Fifteen.

**109.** Twenty-five short. The tape held. The prayer... needed more.

## Mike 120, Matt 130.5 — The Ten-and-a-Half

Matt drafted nineteen players and *zero tight ends*. And on Sunday he started zero tight ends, and won anyway, which is either genius or the funniest thing I've seen all year. Bryce Young, **35**. Christian Watson, **25**. Jalen Hurts 21, Nico Collins 14, Denzel Boston 11, Bucky Irving 8, Saquon Barkley 6, Tetairoa McMillan 6, Jason Myers 4.5. **130.5.**

Mike, the B-, gave it everything: D'Andre Swift **33**, Justin Jefferson **24**, Burrow 14, Mayfield 14, Parker Washington 14, Jordan Mason 11, Matthew Golden 6, DeVonta Smith 3, and Cameron Dicker with — one. One point. **120** total, 0-1, fifth in the league in points. Ten and a half the wrong way.

## Eli 91.5, Chris 111.5 — The D+ Rises

Chris got a **D+** in the draft. Five tight ends, two receivers, a shape that made people laugh out loud. And then Dallas Goedert goes for **22**, Trey McBride 14, Tyler Warren 8 — the tight ends, *the tight ends*, they carried him. Goff 16, Skattebo 14, Malik Willis 14, McPherson 9.5, the 49ers defense 5, and three quiet 3s from McCaffrey, Jadarian Price, and Michael Wilson. **111.5.** One and oh.

Eli — B+, betting on 2028 — got the future early: Jaxson Dart **24**, Kyle Monangai 17, Dak 16, Hampton 8, Meyers 8, Pittsburgh's defense 8, Folk 4.5, Nacua 3, Pierce 3. **91.5.** 2028 is going to be *fantastic*. 2026 is going to require patience.

## The Standings. The Ladder. Whatever You Want To Call It.

No movement arrows yet — everybody just... arrived. **Dan** sits at 1, 168 cumulative, week rank 1. **Joe** 2nd at 137.5 with an 0-1 record, which is a sentence that should be illegal. **Aaron** 3rd, 134, 1-0. **Matt** 4th, 130.5. **Mike** 5th, 120, 0-1. **Chris** 6th, 111.5. **Sean** 7th, 109. **Pete** 8th, 104.5 — *eighth in points, first in humiliation delivered*. **Eli** 9th, 91.5. **Bruce** 10th, 91 — tenth in points and a winner, the exact photo-negative of Joe. **Mitch** 11th, 83.5. And **Cal**, the A, twelfth. 58.

Six teams with a W streak, six with an L. Every matchup a first meeting. No history. No revenge. Just... first impressions. And first impressions, as I have learned the hard way, are usually wrong, and always *permanent*.
