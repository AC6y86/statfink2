# Week 1, 2026: Begun, the Peninsula Clone Wars Have

Hmmm. Sit. Listen, you will.

Long the offseason was. Draft grades you collected like lightsabers on a rack — shiny, sharp, meaningless until swung. Then came Sunday, and the Force moved through twelve rosters, and the Force does not read draft recaps. Yes, hrrrm. Twelve teams entered. Six left as winners. Six left with much to learn.

Average, the league scored 111.58 points. Above it, six. Below it, six. Balance, the Force seeks. Cruel, its methods are.

## The Summit: Dan 168, Joe 137.5

Highest-scoring game of the week, this was. Both teams above 137, and still only one walked away happy. Unfair? Mmm. Fair, the Force is not. Consistent, it merely is.

C+, the draft grades called Dan. "Bet on 2022," they said of him — Diggs, Deebo, Rodgers, the oldest core in the league. And truly, the ancients answered only in whispers: Stefon Diggs 11, Aaron Rodgers 11, Deebo Samuel Sr. 8. But behind the old order, an apprentice waited. **Caleb Williams, 38 points** — co-highest score in all the league. Beside him, a backfield of three suns: Kenneth Walker III 28, David Montgomery 27, Javonte Williams 16. DJ Moore added 17. Three of Dan's men — Williams, Walker, Montgomery — stood inside the week's top ten performances. No other owner placed three. Judge him by his grade, do you? Hmmph. 168 points, a C+ scored.

And Joe? A-, they graded him. The "adult build," they called it — stars early, starters late. Adults, he fielded: Patrick Mahomes 24, Jonathan Taylor 22, Jordan Love 22, Isaiah Likely 22, Ladd McConkey 14, Demarcus Robinson 11, and even the Kansas City Chiefs Defense for 5. Second-most points in the entire league, Joe scored — 137.5, more than every team but Dan. Against any other opponent this week, won he would have. Against Dan, 0-1 he is. L1, his streak. Fear leads to anger. Anger leads to checking the other five scoreboards. Suffering, that way lies.

Bottom of the standings, rank 12, 0-1. A grades, the Force does not accept as currency, mmm. But always in motion, the future is. One week, this is. One week only.

## The Heartbreak: Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5

Single digits, only one game was decided by. 7.5 points, the margin. Closest thing to a duel we had.

Bruce's QB guilt, the draft recap named it — nine first-round quarterbacks he let pass, then reached for Nix, Shough, Watson. Laughed, some did. Then **Tyler Shough threw for 30 points**, sixth-highest performance in the entire league, and Bijan Robinson added 20, and Deshaun Watson 11 more, and the guilt was absolved — for one week. Jeremiyah Love 8, Aaron Jones Sr. 8, Chris Boswell 5. Ninety-one, and never has 91 felt so heavy in the hand.

Mitch, B-graded, brought quarterbacks of his own: Trevor Lawrence 26 — tenth-best score in the league — and Lamar Jackson 22, with Breece Hall 17 and Tyler Bass 7.5. Eight men counted for Mitch; his two defenses gave him nothing, zero and zero, the Giants and the Cowboys alike. Hmmm. Trust in New York and Dallas, you did. Punished, you were. 0-1, L1, rank 11. Cruel irony, taste it: Eli LOST this week with 91.5 — more points than Bruce WON with. Size matters not. Schedule matters. Always, schedule matters.
