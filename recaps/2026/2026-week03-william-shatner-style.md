# Captain's Log: PFL Week 3 ... The Final ... Frontier

*As delivered from the bridge by William Shatner. Pauses. Are. Intentional.*

---

**Captain's Log. Stardate. Week... Three.**

Fantasy football. The final frontier. These are the voyages of the Peninsula Football League. Its twelve-owner mission: to explore strange new box scores... to seek out new lineups and new... *tight ends*... to boldly go where no 65-point team has gone before.

And one team. Did. Go there.

I have commanded starships. I have wrestled a Gorn. I have negotiated hotel rates for *millions* of Americans. And I have never... *never*... seen a week quite like this one. Let us begin.

---

## Log Entry One: The Wrath of... GIBBS

**Sean 141, Eli 72.5**

Space. It is cold. It is vast. And for two weeks, Sean was adrift in it, 0-2, a derelict vessel with the lights off.

Then... the engines... *ignited*.

Jahmyr Gibbs. **33 points.** The week's top performance, the brightest star in the whole miserable galaxy. I have seen supernovas with less acceleration. Beside him, Derrick Henry went for 22, a man built like a Klingon battle cruiser and roughly as polite. And the quarterbacks that everyone said Sean ignored? Matthew Stafford, 22. Kirk Cousins, 21. Brock Bowers added 17 from the tight end chair.

141 points. The league high. **+24.33 against his own average.** Sean shot up three spots to **third** in cumulative points at **350**, and he's 1-2. It's his first win of the season. The draft graders gave him an A-minus and then watched him lose twice. Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league. Madness, they said. Well. Risk... is our *business*.

And Eli. Oh, Eli. Poor Eli. George Kittle gave you 22, and Dak Prescott gave you 16, and then... silence. Omarion Hampton, 3. Jaylen Waddle, **2 points**, a *bust*, a phaser set to "mildly warm." Two kickers in the lineup, Will Reichard 7 and Nick Folk 5.5, because when the backfield is gone you use whatever's left in the torpedo bay.

**72.5 points. A 68.5-point margin.** The blowout of the week. Eli falls **two spots to twelfth**, **0-3**, 252 cumulative. The B-plus draft grade, the bet on 2028... it looks further away than Rigel.

## Log Entry Two: The Half-Point... of No Return

**Mike 104, Chris 103.5**

*(leans forward)*

Half. A. Point.

Do you understand what that *is*? It's a field goal extra... fraction. A sneeze. The margin between a warp core breach and a pleasant afternoon on Risa.

Mike had problems. Baker Mayfield: thumb. De'Von Achane: gone to injured reserve. A lesser captain would have abandoned ship. But Mike found two old officers in the brig and handed them the helm. **Jacoby Brissett, 27. Geno Smith, 26.** Matthew Golden added 17. Harrison Mevis, a *kicker*, delivered 9. Rachaad White 8, Parker Washington 8, D'Andre Swift 6.

104.

And Chris. The man with five tight ends and two receivers, which is, and I say this lovingly, the roster of a man who was beamed down to the draft without a tricorder. And yet! Harold Fannin Jr., a tight end, **19**. Jared Goff, **19**. Christian McCaffrey 14, Michael Wilson 14. The Denver defense chipped in 8, Malik Willis 9.

103.5.

Now. Think about this. *Travis Etienne Jr.* scored **3** for Chris, and he's now out with a hamstring. Cam Skattebo, 3. Tyler Warren, 3. Trey McBride, a tight end of some reputation, 6. One more yard from any one of them... and the log reads differently. It is the closest game of the season so far. Chris drops to **1-2** on an **L2** streak, holding at seventh. Mike, the B-minus draft, climbs **one spot to eighth** at 1-2.

I've said it before. I'll say it again. *There's no such thing... as the no-win scenario.* Ask Mike. Ask Chris, and he will say... the opposite.

## Log Entry Three: Pete's Quiet... Mission

**Pete 122, Joe 99**

Pete has no backfield. Pete has the best receiver room in the Federation. And Pete... is **3-0**.

Sam Darnold: **32 points**, second-best in the league. Brock Purdy: **29**. Jaxon Smith-Njigba: **25**, ninth on the week's top list. Drake London 12, Cooper Kupp 8. His running backs? Ty Johnson 3, Najee Harris 3. It doesn't matter! When your quarterbacks throw like *that*, you don't need legs. You need *antennae*.

122 points, third-best of the week, **+16.28** against his own average. Pete jumps **two spots to sixth** in cumulative points at **317.17**. Sixth! With a perfect record! The record is running ahead of the scoring, like a shuttlecraft that left before the mothership. The C-plus draft grade looks... *embarrassed*.

Joe, graded A-minus, did not lack effort. James Cook III gave 20, Jordan Love 19, Patrick Mahomes 16, Brian Robinson Jr. 11. But Jonathan Taylor, **3**, and Ladd McConkey, **3**, did not answer the hail. 99 points, **-11.5** against his average, and Joe **slides two spots to fifth**, 1-2.

## Log Entry Four: The Surge

**Bruce 138.5, Cal 93.5**

Some captains win with finesse. Bruce won with *photon torpedoes*. Three of them.

**Tyler Shough, 29. Bijan Robinson, 28. Bo Nix, 28.** Three players at 28 or more, three top-six performances of the entire week. Garrett Wilson added 17, Jeremiyah Love 14. **138.5**, second-highest score in the league, **+22.67** against his own average. Bruce rises **one spot to fourth** at 347.5, now **2-1**.

Cal, the draft's lone **A**. The valedictorian. The man who was supposed to be commanding this fleet. Ja'Marr Chase 14, Jordan Addison 14, CeeDee Lamb 11, Bhayshul Tuten 11, Mike Washington Jr. 11. Kyler Murray, at quarterback, in a quarterback league... **5**.

Five.

93.5 points. Actually **+6.5** against his own average, and he climbed **one spot to tenth**. But a 45-point loss, 1-2. The A-student is on academic probation... from the Academy.

**Aaron 107, Matt 65**

And he won. By **42**.

Josh Allen, 22. Juwan Johnson, a tight end, 19. Luther Burden III and Cam Ward, 11 each. Aaron is **3-0**, first in the league, **387 cumulative points**, and he did it on a bad day. That, cadets, is what a flagship looks like.

Now... Matt.

*(long pause)*

**Sixty. Five.**

The league low. **-32.39** against his own average. Twelfth of twelve in weekly points. Matt came into this week **2-0**. He leaves **2-1**, **down five spots to ninth**. Christian Watson 14, Bryce Young 14, Justin Herbert 11, Keaton Mitchell 11. Saquon Barkley, 6. Tony Pollard, 3. Denzel Boston, **2 points**, the week's other *bust*.

And still. Still! **Zero tight ends.** Somewhere out there, Chris is sitting on five of them like a Ferengi on a pile of latinum. Matt. Chris. Make the *deal*. I've negotiated worse at Priceline.

## Log Entry Six: The Winless... Wanderer

**Dan 106.5, Mitch 94.5**

DJ Moore, 3. **106.5**, a 12-point win, **3-0**, second in cumulative points at **351**. Another C-plus team, perfect.

And he is **0-3**. Streak: **L3**. Mitch is the Kobayashi Maru made flesh. He keeps scoring. He keeps losing. *Nobody* reprogrammed the simulation for him.
