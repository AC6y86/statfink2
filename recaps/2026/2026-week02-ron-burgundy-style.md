# PFL Channel 4 News at 6: Week 2, With Ron Burgundy

*(Theme music. Jazz flute sting. A man in a burgundy suit turns to Camera One.)*

Good evening, Peninsula. I'm Ron Burgundy, and this is the Week 2 broadcast of the Peninsula Football League. The studio smells of rich mahogany. My hair has not moved in eleven years. Twelve grown men entered the field of play, six of them left with their dignity, and I am contractually obligated to tell you about all of it.

Our top story: **Aaron dropped 146 points on Bruce's head like a sack of scented candles.**

## TOP STORY — AARON 146, BRUCE 118

Great Odin's raven. Josh Allen scored **40 points**. Forty. That is the top performance in the entire league this week, and it follows a 38-spot in Week 1, which means Aaron's quarterback has now put up back-to-back weeks that most owners couldn't achieve with a full roster and a prayer circle. Add **Amon-Ra St. Brown at 25**, **Cam Ward at 22**, and a matched pair of 14s from **Chris Olave** and **Dalton Kincaid**, and Aaron posted 146 — **+6 versus his own average**, which is the terrifying part. That wasn't a fluke. That's his *Tuesday*.

Bruce did not roll over. **Davante Adams went for 28**, **Tyler Shough added 22**, **Deshaun Watson 16**, and Bruce's 118 was **+13.5 above his own average** and the second-best score of the week. On most weeks, 118 wins you a trophy and a steak dinner. This week it got him a 28-point loss and a handshake. Bruce falls to 1-1 on an L1 streak, though he did rocket **▲5 spots to 5th** in cumulative points at 209. I'm not even mad. That's amazing.

Aaron is 2-0, ranked **1st**, **280 cumulative points**, up **▲2**, and he is, and I quote myself, kind of a big deal.

## PANDA WATCH — THE TWO-POINT GAMES

*(Leaning in. Grave voice.)* The league average was **96.54 points** this week, and four of six games were decided by single digits. We go now to Panda Watch.

**Pete 90.67, Eli 88.** Margin: **2.67**. Pete's **Jaxon Smith-Njigba put up 36 points** — the second-highest total in the league — and **Brock Purdy added 27**, and Pete *still* nearly lost, because his two big men accounted for the bulk of a 90.67 that came in **-6.92 below his own average**. Eli countered with **Dak Prescott at 29** and a three-man wall of **Omarion Hampton 14, TreVeyon Henderson 14, and George Kittle 14**. A field goal's worth of anything — one more grab from **Jaylen Waddle (9)**, one more swing of the leg from **Nick Folk (2)** — and Eli is 1-1 and Pete is mortal. Instead Pete is **2-0** on a W2 streak and sitting 8th, and Eli is **0-2**, **▼1 to 10th**. I'm in a glass case of emotion for Eli. He drafted for 2028 and 2028 has not returned his calls.

**Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67.** Margin: **2.83**, and folks, this was two men fighting over a parking space. Dan — who dropped a league-high 168 last week — cratered to 76.5, which is **-45.75 versus his own average**, the single ugliest delta on the board. **Travis Kelce 17, Stefon Diggs 16, Kenneth Walker III 12, Daniel Jones 11**, and a **Harrison Butker 9.5** that, I want you to understand, was the entire margin of victory and then some. Milk was a bad choice. The 2022 stack is showing its age.

Mike lost anyway, because Mike posted the **worst score in the league at 73.67** (week rank 12, **-23.17** versus his own average) despite **DeVonta Smith's 17**, **Joe Burrow's 16**, and — *this is a real sentence* — **9.67 points from the New England Patriots Defense**, tied for the best DST outing of the week. Mike falls to **0-2** and slides **▼4 to 9th**. Dan stays **2-0** but drops **▼1 to 2nd** with 244.5. A win is a win. It's science.

## ALSO IN SPORTS

**Cal 109.5, Sean 100.** Sean scored a hundred points and lost. Again. **Matthew Stafford 32**, **Kirk Cousins 24**, **Jahmyr Gibbs 14**, **Derrick Henry 11**, **Chuba Hubbard 11** — the A-minus draft is producing, the record is not. Sean is **0-2** on an L2 streak, which is a phrase I don't enjoy saying about a man with that much talent. Cal, meanwhile, bounced back from his Week 1 humbling with **CeeDee Lamb 28**, **Ja'Marr Chase 22**, **Jake Ferguson 16**, and a lovely committee of **8s from MarShawn Lloyd and Braelon Allen**. That's 109.5, **+25.75 above Cal's own average** — the biggest positive swing in the league. Cal is 1-1 and **▲1 to 11th**, which tells you exactly how big a hole Week 1 dug.

**Joe 95, Chris 88.** Margin of 7, and it was **Patrick Mahomes at 27** and **Jonathan Taylor at 22** doing the heavy lifting, with **James Cook III adding 17**. Joe wins despite scoring **-21.25 below his own average** — he goes to 1-1, **▼1 to 3rd**, 232.5 cumulative. Chris had the individual fireworks: **Jared Goff went 32**, **Christian McCaffrey 16**, and then the tight end museum opened for business — **Trey McBride 8, Tyler Warren 8, Harold Fannin Jr. 3**. And here is your **Bust of the Week**, brought to you by Sex Panther cologne: **Travis Etienne Jr., 2 points** for Chris. Two. Points. Sixty percent of the time it works every time — this was the other forty. Squeeze eight more points out of Etienne and Joe's 95 does not survive the hour. Chris falls to 1-1, **▼1 to 7th**.

**Matt 96.67, Mitch 76.5.** The blowout nobody mentions. **Bryce Young 24** and **Jalen Hurts 19** led a spread-it-around 96.67 for Matt — plus **9.67 from the Carolina Panthers Defense**, matching Mike's Patriots for the week's top defensive outing. Mitch's best was **Kyren Williams at 14**, with 11s from **Lamar Jackson** and **Sam LaPorta**, and a **Chase McLaughlin 8.5** that outscored most of his skill players. Matt is **2-0**, holding steady at **4th** with 227.17. Mitch is **0-2**, **▼1 to dead last at 160 cumulative**, 120 points behind Aaron. Stay strong, Mitch. Also, Matt: you still have zero tight ends, and Chris has five of them. I'm not a general manager, I'm a news anchor — but even Baxter can do that math.
