# PUMPING IRON: THE PFL WEEK 3 WORKOUT
### Six sets. No excuses. No girly-men. (A few girly-men.)

Listen to me. Come with me if you want to live.

I have seen many things in my life. I have seen Mr. Olympia stages, I have seen Skynet, I have seen a man named Dan win three straight with Aaron Rodgers and Travis Kelce. But this week in the Peninsula Football League, I saw something more beautiful than all of these. I saw **the pump**. Some of you felt it. Some of you did not feel it at all. And some of you, I am sorry to say, looked at the barbell, cried, and went home to drink a protein shake made with *skim milk*. Milk is for babies.

The league average this week was 103.92 points. That is the bar. Today we find out who lifted it and who got crushed underneath it. We go set by set. Do it. Do it NOW.

## THE MAIN EVENT: SEAN 141, ELI 72.5
### "To crush your enemies, see them driven before you..."

Conan was asked, "What is best in life?" This week the answer was simple: Jahmyr Gibbs.

**33 points.** The top performance in the entire league. Sean's running back walked into Week 3 like the T-800 walks into a police station, and he did not leave until everything was on fire. And Gibbs did not lift alone. Matthew Stafford 22. Derrick Henry 22. Kirk Cousins 21. Brock Bowers 17. That adds up to **141 points**, the best score of the week and +24.33 over Sean's own average.

Remember the draft? Everybody said Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league. Everybody laughed. Well, the running backs delivered, and the quarterback room of Stafford and Cousins delivered 22 and 21 too. Sean was 0-2. He was supposed to be terminated. But I told you in the first movie: *I'll be back.* Sean is back. First win of the year, now 1-2, and he rockets **up three spots to third** with 350 cumulative points, sitting right behind Dan's 351. Consider this a resurrection.

And poor Eli. Eli, Eli, Eli. **72.5 points**, a 68.5-point beatdown, the biggest margin of the week. George Kittle tried. He gave 22, the only real muscle on the roster. Dak Prescott gave 16, Jakobi Meyers 11. But then we come to Jaylen Waddle: **2 points**. One of the busts of the week. This is not a wide receiver. This is a man who walked into the gym, looked at the squat rack, and asked where the smoothie bar was. Eli was even starting two kickers, Will Reichard 7 and Nick Folk 5.5. Two kickers! This is not a lineup, this is a cry for help.

The B+ draft grade. The "bet on 2028" plan. It is now 0-3, and Eli **drops two spots to twelfth**, with 252 points, last in the league. Eli, stop whining. You have work to do.
