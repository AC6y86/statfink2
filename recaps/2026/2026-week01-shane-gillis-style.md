# PFL Week 1: The C+ Kids Are Running This League Now

Alright. Dude. I'm not gonna lie to you — I had a whole thing prepared about how the draft grades were gonna hold up, and then Week 1 happened and the entire report card got put in the garbage disposal.

Quick refresher for the people who came in late, probably drunk, probably from the parking lot: the draft recap handed out grades. Cal got an **A**. Sean got an **A-**. Joe got an **A-**. And down at the bottom, sitting in the back of the class eating paste, were **Bruce C+, Dan C+, Pete C+**, and **Chris D+**, who drafted five tight ends like a guy buying five identical work shirts because they were on sale.

Week 1 result: the C+ kids went a combined 4-0. The A students went 0-3. That's not a fantasy football league, that's a Central PA high school reunion. The valedictorian's selling insurance and the kid who got a D+ in shop class owns four excavators now. That's just how it works, bro.

League average was **111.58**. Remember that number, it's gonna come up, because some of these guys lapped it and one guy looked at it through binoculars.

---

## Dan 168, Joe 137.5 — The Highest-Scoring Game, And The Cruelest

This was the best game of the week and it was also a crime scene.

Dan "bet on 2022" at the draft. Diggs, Deebo, Rodgers — the oldest core in the league, a roster that smells like a Buffalo Wild Wings in 2022. And it worked immediately. **Caleb Williams put up 38.** **Kenneth Walker III 28. David Montgomery 27.** Three guys in the league's top 10 out of one roster. DJ Moore chipped in **17**, Javonte Williams **16**, and then the antiques showed up too — **Diggs 11, Rodgers 11, Deebo 8**. Dan finished at **168**. He's the number one team in the league, C+ and all.

Now Joe. Joe got the A-. Joe's bench was "seven starters at WR," the adult build, the responsible guy. Joe scored **137.5**, the second-most points in the entire league, and **lost by 30.5.** Mahomes **24**, Jonathan Taylor **22**, Jordan Love **22**, Isaiah Likely **22** — that's four guys at 22 or better and it didn't matter even a little bit. Joe sits **2nd in the league at 137.5 cumulative points with an 0-1 record.** That's the fantasy football equivalent of doing everything your dad told you to do and still getting yelled at in the driveway.

And then, because God has a sense of humor, **A.J. Brown went on IR on Sep 13.** Same week. Rough.

---

## Pete 104.5, Cal 58 — Somebody Call Somebody

Blowout of the week, **46.5** points, and it wasn't even that competitive-looking.

Pete, C+, "best WR room, no backfield," goes out and gets **Brock Purdy 21, Zay Flowers 20, C.J. Stroud 19, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17** and a running back named **Jacory Croskey-Merritt for 11** who I'm fairly confident Pete cannot pick out of a lineup. He scored **104.5** — under league average! — and won by more than any other team in the league. That's the dream. That's winning a bar fight by falling on the guy.

Cal — the **A** grade, the "adult build," stars early — scored **58**. Fifty-eight, dude. **Ashton Jeanty went for 25**, which means Jeanty by himself was worth almost half the roster's output. After that it's **Jayden Daniels 10, CeeDee Lamb 8**, and then a parade of guys at **3, 3, 3, 3.5, and 2.5.** Drew Stevens kicked **2.5**. That's not a lineup, that's a bar tab.

Cal is 12th. The A student is dead last. I'm sorry, man. It's Week 1. You can still turn this into a "and that's when I found my faith" story.

---

## Aaron 134, Sean 109 — Two Guys Emptying Clips

**Josh Allen went for 38** and tied Caleb Williams for the best score in the league. Then Aaron got **25 from Jalen Coker**, which nobody saw coming, plus **Amon-Ra St. Brown 19, Chris Olave 12, Chase Brown 11, Juwan Johnson 11, Dalton Kincaid 9.** Total: **134**, good for a 25-point win and 3rd in the league. And yes — the guy who drafted two kickers got **6 from Cam Little.** Vindicated. Absolute war hero.

Sean's the one I feel for. Sean went **A-**, bet on running backs in a quarterback league, and the backs *delivered*: **Derrick Henry 33, Jahmyr Gibbs 28, Chuba Hubbard 16.** That's a gorgeous top three. Problem is the rest of the roster was **Kirk Cousins 15**, the Bucs D for **8**, and *two kickers* — **Trey Smack 6.5 and Wil Lutz 2.5** — in a league where the winner had a QB score 38. The QB room "held together with tape and prayer" produced 15 points. Tape lost. **109, 0-1.**

---

## Matt 130.5, Mike 120 — The Guy With No Tight Ends Didn't Need One

Matt drafted 19 players and zero tight ends. Everyone said someone would charge him for one. Then **Bryce Young dropped 35**, **Christian Watson dropped 25**, **Jalen Hurts added 21**, **Nico Collins 14**, **Denzel Boston 11**, and Matt won **130.5 to 120** without a single tight end on the books. Saquon Barkley chipped in **6**. Bucky Irving **8**. He's 1-0 and 4th overall and he does not want to hear your trade offer.

Mike's 120 would've beaten four other teams this week, which is the worst sentence in fantasy football. **D'Andre Swift went for 33** — top-five score in the league — and **Justin Jefferson 24**, and Parker Washington of all people posted **14**. But Mike ran two quarterbacks at **14 and 14** (Burrow and Mayfield), got **3 from DeVonta Smith**, and **1 point from Cameron Dicker.** One. A single point from your kicker in a **10.5-point loss.** That's the what-if right there. Dicker has a normal Sunday and we're talking about a different game.

---

## Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5 — The Only Game Anybody Had To Sweat

The one close call of the week. **7.5 points.** Everything else was decided by 10.5 or more.

Bruce is the guy with the QB guilt. Passed on nine first-round quarterbacks, then drafted Nix, Shough and Watson, and spent the whole draft looking like a man who left the oven on. **Tyler Shough scored 30.** THIRTY. Sixth-best score in the league. Add **Bijan Robinson 20, Deshaun Watson 11, Jeremiyah Love 8, Aaron Jones Sr. 8, Garrett Wilson 6, Chris Boswell 5** and Bruce gets to **91** and a win. He's 10th in cumulative points and 1-0, which means he's simultaneously the worst good team and the best bad team in America.

Mitch had the better-looking box score and lost. **Trevor Lawrence 26, Lamar Jackson 22, Breece Hall 17, Kyren Williams 8, Tyler Bass 7.5** — and then it just stopped. **Tee Higgins 3.** Two defenses at zero. Eight guys counted, **83.5** total. One more field goal, one more garbage-time catch from Higgins, and Mitch flips the whole week. He's 11th. He is going to think about this until Thursday.

---

## Chris 111.5, Eli 91.5 — The D+ Roster Works, Apparently

Chris got a **D+** for drafting five tight ends and two wide receivers, a roster shaped like a golf bag with five putters. Week 1: **Dallas Goedert 22, Trey McBride 14, Tyler Warren 8.** The putters went in. Add **Jared Goff 16, Cam Skattebo 14, Malik Willis 14, Evan McPherson 9.5, 49ers D 5**, and Chris wins by **20** at **111.5**. Christian McCaffrey, the elite piece, the guy the grade was propped up on, scored **3.** Didn't matter. D+ is 1-0.

Eli bet on 2028 and, look — 2028 is not this week. **Jaxson Dart 24** is a genuinely fun start, **Kyle Monangai 17**, **Dak Prescott 16**, Steelers D **8**, and then **Puka Nacua 3** and **Alec Pierce 3** and you're staring at **91.5.** Eli's at 9th. The lottery tickets are scratched but not cashed.

---

## Where It All Leaves Us

**Dan's on top at 168.** Then **Joe 137.5, Aaron 134, Matt 130.5, Mike 120, Chris 111.5, Sean 109, Pete 104.5, Eli 91.5, Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5, Cal 58.** Dan, Aaron and Matt all turned top-four scores into wins; Joe and Mike are the two guys walking around with big numbers and an L; and Pete and Bruce are the two guys walking around undefeated with scores below the 111.58 league average. Everybody's streak is exactly one game long, because it's Week 1 and nobody's earned an adjective yet.

Nobody in a scoring lineup fell off a cliff this week — the disappointments were the quiet kind. McCaffrey **3**, Nacua **3**, Saquon **6**, CeeDee **8**. Guys you paid a mortgage for who showed up and did the bare minimum, like a cousin helping you move.
