# THE CAGE TAPES: PFL WEEK ONE, TAKE ONE — "NOT THE SCOREBOARD!"

*[The actor enters. He is wearing a snakeskin jacket. It is a symbol of his individuality and his belief in personal freedom. He does not sit down. He never sits down.]*

Okay. OKAY. Listen to me.

I've made two hundred movies and I've been eaten by bees, I've stolen the Declaration of Independence, I've had another man's face surgically attached to my own skull, and I am telling you — with the full weight of my thespian soul — that **nothing** prepared me for Week One of the Peninsula Football League.

This is not acting. This is NOUVEAU SHAMANIC. Let's go. Twelve owners. Six matchups. One take.

---

## ACT I: THE HIGH-SPEED CHASE (Dan 168, Joe 137.5)

Put the bunny back in the box, Joe. PUT IT BACK.

One hundred thirty-seven and a half points. That is a *winning* score in most weeks on most planets. Joe got Patrick Mahomes for 24, Jonathan Taylor for 22, Jordan Love for 22, Isaiah Likely for 22 — a beautiful, symmetrical, A- draft grade of a lineup, four men all humming at the same frequency like a chorus of monks — and it bought him **absolutely nothing**, because Dan showed up driving a truck full of 2022 nostalgia and 168 points.

Caleb Williams: **38**. Kenneth Walker III: **28**. David Montgomery: **27**. Three of the week's top nine performances, all in one lineup, all in one backfield-shaped avalanche. And the beautiful part — the part that makes me want to scream into a canyon — is that the old guys Dan supposedly overpaid for showed up too. Stefon Diggs, 11. Aaron Rodgers, 11. Deebo Samuel Sr., 8. The oldest core in the league went out and got the **highest score in the league**.

They gave Dan a **C+** on draft day. He answered with 168. That's high praise from such a low-life grade.

---

## ACT II: THE FACE/OFF (Aaron 134, Sean 109)

Two men. Two quarterbacks. One trades faces with the other.

Josh Allen dropped **38** for Aaron — co-king of the week, tied to the point with Caleb Williams, which is the kind of cosmic coincidence that makes me believe in something. Then Jalen Coker showed up with **25** out of absolutely nowhere, Amon-Ra St. Brown added 19, Chris Olave 12, and the twin tight ends Juwan Johnson (11) and Dalton Kincaid (9) did honest, unglamorous work. **134.** B+ draft, A-minus execution.

Sean did not lose because Sean was bad. Sean lost because of **arithmetic**, and arithmetic has no mercy and no agent. Derrick Henry: **33**. Jahmyr Gibbs: **28**. Chuba Hubbard: 16. That's 77 points from three running backs — the RB bet, paid in full, cash money. But that quarterback room they said was "held together with tape and prayer"? Kirk Cousins, 15 points. The tape held. The prayer did not. Final: **134-109**, Aaron by 25.

---

## ACT III: THE NAIL-BITER (Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5)

*[whispering now]*

This is the one. The only game all week decided by a single score. Seven and a half points. SEVEN. AND. A. HALF.

Bruce spent draft day passing on nine — NINE — first-round quarterbacks and then drafting Nix, Shough, and Watson like a man ordering the third-cheapest wine out of spite. And then **Tyler Shough went out and scored 30 points.** Thirty! Bijan Robinson added 20, Jeremiyah Love and Aaron Jones Sr. chipped in 8 apiece, Chris Boswell kicked 5, and the whole guilt-ridden contraption limped across the line at **91**.

Mitch had Trevor Lawrence at **26** and Lamar Jackson at 22 and Breece Hall at 17 and still came up at **83.5**. What if — WHAT IF — Mitch finds seven and a half more points anywhere on that sheet? Tyler Bass gave him 7.5 all by himself. One more field goal. One. The margin of the entire game was sitting in a kicker's right foot.

I'm the bad guy? How'd that happen?

---

## ACT IV: THE TIGHT END HEIST (Matt 130.5, Mike 120)

Here is my favorite lie the league told itself: **Matt has zero tight ends.** Drafted nineteen players, didn't take one. Someone was supposed to charge him rent for it.

Matt scored 130.5 without ever once looking at the position. **Bryce Young, 35 points.** Christian Watson, **25**. Jalen Hurts, 21. Nico Collins, 14. Denzel Boston, 11. That's a man building a house with no doors and living in it happily.

Mike's 120 deserved better — D'Andre Swift erupted for **33**, fourth-best performance in the entire league, and Justin Jefferson added 24. But Mike's quarterbacks split the difference at 14 apiece (Burrow and Mayfield), and Cameron Dicker's **1 point** hangs over this thing like a curse. One point from the kicker. Mike lost by 10.5. I'm not saying it's the kicker's fault. I'm saying I've seen men lose their hand AND their bride, and I know what a one-point kicker looks like when it's staring back at you at 4 a.m.

---

## ACT V: THE D+ REVENGE (Chris 111.5, Eli 91.5)

They gave Chris a **D+**. The lowest grade in the room. Five tight ends and two receivers, they said. Broken shape, they said.

Chris won with the tight ends. **Dallas Goedert, 22. Trey McBride, 14. Tyler Warren, 8.** Forty-four points from the position nobody else wanted, plus Jared Goff 16, Cam Skattebo 14, Malik Willis 14, the 49ers defense for 5, and Evan McPherson booting **9.5**. Eleven-eleven point five. The broken shape scored.

Eli, betting on 2028, got a glimpse of it: Jaxson Dart, **24**, a rookie-flavored jolt of the future, Kyle Monangai 17, Dak Prescott 16, the Steelers defense for a tidy 8. But 91.5 is 91.5 and the future does not pay this week's rent. Chris by 20.

---

## ACT VI: NOT THE BEES (Pete 104.5, Cal 58)

**NOT THE BEES! NOT THE BEEEEES!**

Fifty-eight points. Cal — the **A** grade, the consensus best draft in the league, the "adult build," stars early and starters late — put up **58 points**, the lowest score in the PFL by a full 25.5 off eleventh place. Ashton Jeanty was magnificent with **25**. And then the rest of the sheet reads like a ransom note written in threes: Jayden Daniels 10, CeeDee Lamb 8, Jake Elliott 3.5, Bhayshul Tuten 3, Rome Odunze 3, Tyler Allgeier 3, Drew Stevens 2.5. Take Jeanty out and Cal scored 33.

Pete, a **C+**, needed nothing heroic and did nothing heroic — Brock Purdy 21, Zay Flowers 20, C.J. Stroud 19, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17, and the backfield everyone said he didn't have coughed up 11 from Jacory Croskey-Merritt. **104.5-58.** A 46.5-point demolition, the biggest gap of the week. The best WR room in the league just steamrolled the best draft in the league.

How in the name of Zeus's butthole does that happen in Week One?

---

## THE LEDGER *[he finally stops pacing]*

League average: **111.58**. Dan sits at **1** with 168 cumulative, Joe second with 137.5 and an 0-1 record — the cruelest pairing of numbers on the page, 30.5 points of loss. Aaron 3rd (134, 1-0), Matt 4th (130.5, 1-0), Mike 5th (120, 0-1), Chris 6th (111.5, 1-0), Sean 7th (109, 0-1), Pete 8th (104.5, 1-0), Eli 9th (91.5, 0-1), Bruce 10th (91, 1-0), Mitch 11th (83.5, 0-1), Cal 12th (58, 0-1). No rank movement yet — there's nothing to move *from*. Six teams on a one-game win streak, six on a one-game losing streak, every matchup a first meeting, no scores to settle. Yet.

And the bust reel? Empty. Every single player in every scoring lineup this week cleared two points. Not one villain in the whole picture. I'm almost disappointed.
