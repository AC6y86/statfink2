# No Respect, I Tell Ya: PFL Week 4, Live from the Back Room

*(He walks out, tugging at a red tie that's already strangling him. Grabs the mic. Mops his forehead with a handkerchief.)*

Thank you, thank you, hey, easy. Whoa, what a crowd, what a crowd. Twelve owners and not one of 'em clapping. That's okay, I'm used to it. I tell ya, I get no respect. None! And this week I found eleven guys in the Peninsula Football League who know exactly what that's like. Okay, ten. One guy scored 148.5 and he's out in the parking lot doing donuts.

Let me tell ya about this week, folks. It was rough. It was so rough the league average was 105.92 and more than half the league still couldn't reach it.

---

## Sean vs. Joe: The King of No Respect

Let's start with my guy. My people. **Sean.** Sean is **first in the whole league in cumulative points, 486**, up two spots. First! And his record? **1-3.** I tell ya, this guy is me with a fantasy team.

Sean puts up **136** this week. Third-best score in the league, **+14.5 over his own average**. Chuba Hubbard goes for **25**. Kirk Cousins, **24**. Alvin Kamara, **16**, Brock Bowers **14**, Derrick Henry **11**. A guy does all that, you'd think he gets a parade. No! He gets **Joe.**

Joe walks in with **148.5**, the best score in the whole league, **+28.5 over his own average**, and wins by **12.5**. Brian Robinson Jr. puts up **27**. Jonathan Taylor, **22**. Romeo Doubs, **19**. And then Joe's got *two* quarterbacks, Patrick Mahomes **16** and Jordan Love **16**, both cashing checks. James Cook III throws in **14**, Quinshon Judkins **11**. The man had more guys show up than my wedding.

That's the kind of luck Sean's got. With **136**, he'd have beaten every other team on the board except Joe and Matt. Every one! He had to draw the one guy who went to the moon. I know how that goes. I went to a fortune teller once, she took one look at my palm and said "you lose to Joe." Joe's now **2-2**, up two spots to **third at 480**, and he's starting to strut. Sean's still sitting on the most points in the league with a record that looks like my bowling scores. No respect, I tell ya!

---

## Drake Maye and Chris: Twenty-Nine Points and Still No Respect

Now here's your top individual performance of the week, folks. **Drake Maye, 29 points.** Number one in the whole league. Kid plays like a hero. And who's he play for? **Chris.** And what happens to Chris? Chris **loses**. Of course he does!

Chris's **84.5** goes down to **Mitch's 105**, a **20.5**-point beating. Maye gives you **29**, Jared Goff adds **20**, Christian McCaffrey **11**, Harold Fannin Jr. **8**, and then the lights go out. Chris drafted himself five tight ends and two receivers. Five tight ends! What's he running, a moving company? The draft experts gave him a **D+**, and he's out there proving them right like it's a hobby. **1-3**, three straight losses, last five **WLLL**, down one to **eighth at 387.5**. That's tenth in the week. I tell ya, Maye goes for 29 and Chris loses anyway. That's not football, that's my love life.

Meanwhile Mitch finally gets his **first win**. Kyren Williams **25**, Lamar Jackson **16**, Sam LaPorta **14**, Tee Higgins **12**, and the New York Giants Defense chips in **8**. The *Giants* defense! Mitch is so happy he's buying drinks. Not for anybody here, but still. He's **1-3**, still **eleventh at 359.5**, didn't move an inch. You win a game and you don't even move up! That's a Dangerfield special right there.

## Dan vs. Aaron: The C+ Kid Stays Perfect

**Dan**. Draft grade? **C+**. Record? **4-0**. I tell ya, I got a C+ once, my father held a funeral. Dan's out here running the table.

Dan beats Aaron **113 to 93.5**, a **19.5**-point win. Dan built a roster like it's 2022 and it worked. Kenneth Walker III, **28**. Javonte Williams, **27**. Aaron Rodgers, **26**. Rodgers! The guy's been in the league so long, I think he backed up my grandfather. Michael Penix Jr. adds **11**. Dan's still only fifth in the week at **113 (-3 vs own avg)** and slid **two spots to fourth at 464**, but who cares? He's undefeated. Four straight wins. **WWWW.**

Aaron, on the other hand. Rough night. **93.5**, which is **-26.63 vs his own average**, eighth in the week. Josh Allen gives you **22**, Malik Nabers **17**, Cam Ward **13**, and then it falls apart like my marriage. Amon-Ra St. Brown, **6**. Chase Brown, **3**. Luther Burden III, **3**. Aaron's still **3-1** and **second in points at 480.5**, down one. But I'll tell ya, when your team scores 26 under its own average, even the scoreboard's embarrassed to look at ya.

## Pete vs. Mike: Ugly Wins Count Too

**Pete** is the other **C+ unbeaten**, folks. **4-0.** And he gets there with **95.5**, seventh-best in the league this week. Seventh! He's winning like I get dates. Not because I'm good, the other guy just didn't show up.

C.J. Stroud **22**, Zay Flowers **17**, Brock Purdy **16**, and two defenses, the Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears, **5 apiece**. Rhamondre Stevenson **10**. And here's your **bust of the week**: **Najee Harris, 2 points**. Two! I've got a cousin who'd get two points just by tripping onto the field. Pete's got the best receivers and no backfield, and Najee proved it. Pete's **-7.67 vs his own average**, down one to **seventh at 412.67**, but he wins by **31.5**. Undefeated! Ugly, but undefeated, like my uncle.

Because **Mike** put up **64**. Lowest score in the whole league. **-26.42 vs his own average.** And you wanna know the worst part? **Joe Burrow scored 28.** Tied for the second-best performance in the league, and it **lost**. Burrow gets 28, Jacoby Brissett adds **10**, Tucker Kraft **8**, Matthew Golden **8**, Cameron Dicker **7**, and then D'Andre Swift **3** and two defenses at a big fat **0**. Mike's **1-3**, down two to **tenth at 361.67**. Burrow and Maye, the top QBs of the week, both lost. That's this league. A man does his job and gets nothing. I can relate! I'm the Joe Burrow of nightclubs.

Kyle Monangai, **25**. Jameis Winston, **24**. Jameis Winston! You never know which one you're gettin'. This week Eli got the good one. Tyler Shough and Deshaun Watson, **14** each. And then Jeremiyah Love **3**, Colston Loveland **3**. Two "Loves" on one roster and neither gave him any. I know the feeling, Bruce. Bruce is **2-2**, down one to **fifth at 440**.

## Matt vs. Cal: The Blowout of the Week

No close calls this week, folks. Nobody won by under ten. Nobody was sweatin'. But one guy got *buried*.

**Matt 137, Cal 73.5.** A **63.5**-point margin, the biggest of the week. That's not a game, that's an autopsy.

Matt doesn't even have a **tight end**. Zero! He forgot an entire position, like me forgetting my anniversary, and still scored **137**, second in the league, **+29.71 vs his own average**. Tetairoa McMillan **28**, Nico Collins **25**, Bryce Young **22**, Ollie Gordon II **17**, Jake Bates **9**. Matt jumps **three spots to sixth at 429.17** and he's **3-1**. No tight end, no problem. I've got a whole body and no luck at all.

And **Cal**. Oh, Cal. The draft experts gave Cal an **A**. The top grade in the whole draft! And now? **Dead last, twelfth at 334.5**, down two, **1-3**, two straight losses. CeeDee Lamb gave him **20**, and after that it's Carnell Tate **9**, Tyler Allgeier **8**, Ashton Jeanty **6**, Kyler Murray **6**. Cal's got two kickers in the lineup, Jake Elliott **5** and Drew Stevens **4.5**, and the kickers were the most reliable guys he had. When your kickers are your best players, you're in trouble. Cal got an A in the draft. I got an A once too. It was on a parking ticket.

## Where It All Stands

So here's the board, folks. At the top it's **Sean (486)**, **Aaron (480.5)**, **Joe (480)**, six points covering three guys. Then **Dan at 464** and **Bruce at 440**. The only unbeatens are **Dan and Pete, both 4-0**, both graded **C+**, and they're fourth and seventh in points. The guy with the most points is **1-3**. The A-grade guy is in last. That's this league, folks. Nothing goes the way it's supposed to. It's my life story in a spreadsheet.

Next week: can Sean's points ever turn into wins? Can Dan and Pete stay perfect? Can Cal and Mike climb up from the cellar? I don't know. Nobody tells me anything. No respect!
