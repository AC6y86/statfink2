# PFL Week 2: Twelve Guys, Six Games, And A Whole Lot Of Zero

Have you ever noticed that fantasy football is the only hobby where grown men schedule their emotional lives around a guy in Buffalo?

Week 2 is in the books. League average: **96.54 points**. That's the number. That's what "average" got you — 96.54 — and seven of you couldn't even manage that, which tells you something about the human capacity for confidence in the absence of evidence.

Let's start with a word. **"Defense."** Beautiful word. Comes from the Latin, means *to ward off.* In this league it means *a slot on your roster that produces nothing and asks for nothing and gives you nothing and just sits there like a lawn ornament.* Look at the lineups. Houston Texans Defense: **0**. Detroit Lions Defense: **0**. Jacksonville, Kansas City, Baltimore, Cleveland, Buffalo, Tampa Bay, Denver, San Francisco, Chicago, New Orleans, the Rams, the Steelers, Philadelphia, Washington, the Giants, Dallas, the Chargers, Green Bay — **zero, zero, zero, zero, zero.** A rosary of nothing. Twelve owners drafted defenses and this week they collectively defended the concept of the number zero. Congratulations, fellas. You've invented a unit of measurement.

## AARON 146, BRUCE 118: THE MAN WITH THE ARM AND THE MAN WITH THE ALIBI

**Josh Allen: 40 points.** That's your week. That's your league's top performance, and it came from one guy, on one team, throwing a ball around while eleven other owners typed "lol" into a group chat.

Aaron put up **146** — the highest score of the week, **+6 versus his own average**, which is the most infuriating stat in this whole document, because it means *this is just what he does now.* He didn't get lucky. He got Tuesday. Amon-Ra St. Brown chipped in **25**, Cam Ward **22**, Chris Olave **14**, Dalton Kincaid **14**, Terrance Ferguson **11**, Brandon Aubrey **8**, Chase Brown **6**, Blake Corum **6**, and — say it with me — Houston **0** and Detroit **0**. He scored 146 points with two lawn ornaments in the lineup.

Bruce scored **118**. In any other week that's a nice afternoon. Davante Adams **28**, Tyler Shough **22**, Deshaun Watson **16**, Garrett Wilson **11**, Aaron Jones Sr. **9**, and then a little conga line of eights — Egbuka, Coleman, Bigsby — Joey Slye **5**, Bijan Robinson **3**. Bruce went **+13.5 versus his own average** and lost by **28**. That's not a defeat, that's a diagnosis. You can do everything right and still get run over, and if that isn't the whole American experience in one box score I don't know what is. He climbed **five spots to 5th** anyway, at **209** cumulative, which is the league's way of handing you a participation ribbon soaked in gasoline.

## MATT 96.67, MITCH 76.5: THE TIGHT END SHORTAGE AND THE TIGHT END SURPLUS

Matt won by **20.17** — the biggest gap of the day after Aaron's demolition — and he did it with **zero tight ends on the roster**, two weeks running. Two weeks! The man looked at an entire position group, an entire category of large human being, and said *no thank you.* And he's **2-0**. Meanwhile Chris is sitting on a warehouse of tight ends like a guy hoarding canned goods for a war that never comes. These two should talk. They won't. Nobody ever does.

Bryce Young **24**, Jalen Hurts **19**, Denzel Boston **14**, and then — stop the presses — the **Carolina Panthers Defense, 9.67**. A defense! That scored! Somebody call the Vatican. Tetairoa McMillan added **9**, Christian Watson **8**, Bucky Irving **6**, Jason Myers **4**, Tony Pollard **3**.

Mitch put up **76.5** on **-3.5 versus his own average**, which means this is just his ceiling wearing a floor's clothing. Kyren Williams **14**, Lamar Jackson **11**, Sam LaPorta **11**, Dalton Schultz **9**, Chase McLaughlin **8.5** — when your kicker is fifth on the team you're not playing fantasy football, you're doing arithmetic. Mitch is **0-2** and dead last at **160** cumulative points. Somebody hug him. Then take his phone away.

## THE COIN-FLIP QUARTET: FOUR GAMES, FOUR MARGINS UNDER TEN

Four of six games were one-score games. *Four.* You know what they call that? "Parity." **"Parity."** There's a word invented by a man who lost. Parity is what you say when everybody's mediocre and you need it to sound like a system.

**Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67. Margin: 2.83.** Dan went from a league-high 168 in week 1 to **76.5**, which is **-45.75 versus his own average** — the single largest faceplant on the page — and *still won.* Travis Kelce **17**, Stefon Diggs **16**, Kenneth Walker III **12**, Daniel Jones **11**, Harrison Butker **9.5**, Aaron Rodgers **6**, Seattle's defense **5**. That's not a lineup, that's a class reunion. He finished **11th in weekly scoring out of twelve** and is **2-0**. Mike was **12th** at **73.67**, **-23.17 versus his own average**, with a **New England Patriots Defense at 9.67** doing more than DeVonta Smith's **17** and Joe Burrow's **16** had any right to ask for. Mike drops **four spots to 9th** and sits **0-2**. Two-point-eight-three. You could sneeze that. Mike, somewhere in that lineup, one first down — one! — and you're 1-1 and Dan's telling everyone he's rebuilding.

**Pete 90.67, Eli 88. Margin: 2.67.** The tightest game of the week. Pete blew Cal out by 46.5 last week and this week he needed **Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 36 points** — second-best performance in the league — plus Brock Purdy's **27** just to survive by the width of a decimal. Then it was C.J. Stroud **12**, Rashee Rice **6**, Borregales **5**, Drake London **3**, Minnesota's defense **1.67**. One-point-six-seven! And it *mattered!* Take that away and Pete loses. The Vikings defense is now load-bearing. Eli got Dak Prescott **29**, Omarion Hampton **14**, TreVeyon Henderson **14**, George Kittle **14**, Jaylen Waddle **9** and lost anyway. Eli is **0-2**, scored **-1.75 versus his own average** — meaning he played basically exactly as well as he always does — and that's the cruelest thing in this whole recap. Consistency is a lovely quality in a person and a death sentence in a schedule.

**Joe 95, Chris 88. Margin: 7.** Mahomes **27**, Jonathan Taylor **22**, James Cook III **17**, Jordan Love **10** — and Joe still finished **-21.25 versus his own average**, because Mike Evans gave him **3** and Brian Robinson Jr. gave him **3**. Chris had **Jared Goff at 32**, tied for third-best in the league, Christian McCaffrey **16**, Malik Willis **11**, and then the tight end warehouse opened up: McBride **8**, Warren **8**, Fannin **3**. And for our **official Bust of the Week**: **Travis Etienne Jr., 2 points.** Two. "Bust." Another word. A "bust" is also a sculpture of somebody's head, which is appropriate, because that's what Chris was holding in his hands at 4:15 on Sunday.

**Cal 109.5, Sean 100. Margin: 9.5.** Cal was the week's third-highest scorer at **+25.75 versus his own average** — the biggest positive swing anybody posted — and he did it on CeeDee Lamb **28**, Ja'Marr Chase **22**, Jake Ferguson **16**, Bhayshul Tuten **11**, and a committee of eights: Jayden Daniels, MarShawn Lloyd, Braelon Allen. Cal's now **1-1** but **11th at 167.5 cumulative**, which is the fantasy equivalent of a beautiful house on a fault line. Sean scored **100 points and lost.** He's **0-2**. He has scored a hundred-plus twice and has nothing to show for it but Matthew Stafford's **32**, Kirk Cousins' **24**, Jahmyr Gibbs' **14**, and a quiet interior scream. **Sixth in the standings at 209, zero wins.** They call it "bad luck." It isn't luck. It's a schedule, and a schedule is just a stranger deciding your year in July.

## THE STANDINGS, OR: THE ILLUSION OF ORDER

**Aaron 1st at 280. Dan 2nd at 244.5. Joe 3rd at 232.5. Matt 4th at 227.17.** Then **Bruce and Sean, dead even at 209** — one of them 1-1, the other 0-2, same points, different feelings. Chris **199.5**, Pete **195.17**, Mike **193.67**, Eli **179.5**, Cal **167.5**, Mitch **160**.

Now here's the joke the league is telling and nobody's laughing at yet: the **draft grades**. Cal got an **A** and is 1-1 and eleventh in points. Sean got an **A-** and is **0-2**. Eli got a **B+** and is **0-2**. And Dan and Pete, both graded **C+**, are both **2-0**. Grades. We hand out grades for a thing that hasn't happened yet, then act surprised when the future shows up wearing a different outfit.

Four unbeaten — Aaron, Dan, Matt, Pete. Four winless — Sean, Mike, Eli, Mitch. Four in the middle. Twelve men, neatly sorted into thirds, like the universe is running a filing system. It isn't. It's a coin, and it's been flipping in the dark for two weeks.

- **Joe:** A.J. Brown (WR, NE) — IR, high-ankle sprain, about six weeks. Demarcus Robinson (WR, SF) — Out, 3-to-6 weeks, high ankle.

"A significant amount of time." That's my favorite. That's every human life ever lived, right there, and they use it to describe a running back's lower spine.

So that's Week 2. Six games, four of them decided by less than ten points, one guy named Allen scoring 40, one guy named Etienne scoring 2, and a long gray parade of defenses scoring absolutely nothing at all.

Next week they'll all tell you they're due. **"Due."** There's a word for people who don't understand mathematics. Nobody's due. The ball is not aware of you. Play your guys and take your beating like a citizen.
