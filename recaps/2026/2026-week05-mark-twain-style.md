# The Adventures of the Peninsula Football League, Week 5

*Being a True Account, Mostly, Set Down by an Old Steamboat Pilot Who Has Seen a Great Many Foolish Things on the River and Now Has Six More*

---

> **NOTICE.** Persons attempting to find a motive in this narrative will be prosecuted; persons attempting to find a moral in it will be banished; persons attempting to find a close game in it will be shot, for there were none. BY ORDER OF THE AUTHOR, per the Chief of Ordnance.

---

## Explanatory

A good many people believe the statistics of a fantasy football league are a kind of scripture. I've taken the opposite position: get your facts first, then you can distort them as you please. In this account I've kept the facts and spared you most of the distortion.

When I was a cub pilot, the leadsman would heave his line into the brown water and sing out what he found. "Mark twain!" meant two fathoms, safe water. "Quarter less three" meant somebody had better wake up. "No bottom" meant the Lord had stopped taking an interest. I took soundings on all six games of Week 5. The league averaged **105.25**, the narrowest margin was 10, the widest was 55.5, and under "Close Calls" the digest prints "(none this week)," which is the only unvarnished sentence this league has ever produced.

---

## Chapter I. In Which a Man With Five Tight Ends Is Proved Right, to the Great Annoyance of Everyone

> *Pudd'nhead Wilson's Calendar for Week 5: Few things are harder to put up with than a good example, unless it is a lucky one.*

**Chris 147, Aaron 109** — *"By the deep four!"*

Chris was 1-3 coming in, and the town had already measured him for the box. Reports of his demise turned out to be greatly exaggerated. He put up **147**, the best score in the league and **+40.1** over his own average, and he carries himself now like a deacon who found a winning ticket in the collection plate and has decided it was Providence.

The centerpiece was **Drake Maye, 40 points**, the top performance in the whole league. Jared Goff added **32**, the third-best, so Chris owned two of the top three, which is how fortunes get made in this country: by accident, followed by forty years of lectures about thrift.

The storylines record that Chris carries five tight ends and two receivers. That's a steamboat loaded entirely with anchors, and the anchors floated: Tyler Warren 14, Trey McBride 11, Kyle Pitts Sr. 8. Christian McCaffrey gave 11, Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked 9, and the San Francisco defense pitched in 8. Chris is still **2-3**, up one place to seventh at 534.5.

Aaron has a grievance, and I'll hear it. Jaylen Warren put up **31** and Josh Allen **30**, both top-ten performances, and Malik Nabers gave 17. Then he called on Amon-Ra St. Brown, who gave 3, and on **Dalton Kincaid, who gave 2 points**, the week's official bust and the only name on the digest's list. You'll remember the celebrated jumping frog of Calaveras County, the one some stranger filled with quail shot so he just sat there and heaved his shoulders. Kincaid had that look. Aaron's 109 ran -8.9 against his average. He's 3-2 on a two-game losing streak and slips one place to third at 589.5.

## Chapter II. A Wreck, Viewed With Christian Charity From the Bank

> *Calendar: Be good and you will be lonesome. Go 5-0 and you will be sixth.*

**Pete 126, Mitch 70.5** — *"Mark three! Quarter less... no bottom, sir."*

This was the biggest margin of the week, **55.5**, and I'll say nothing unkind about the losing side. I'll just report it, which is worse.

Pete is **5-0** with a W5 streak, the last unbeaten man in the PFL. C.J. Stroud gave 22, Brock Purdy 18, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17, Jacory Croskey-Merritt 14. The storylines say Pete has "no backfield," and Rhamondre Stevenson and Samaje Perine went for 11 apiece anyway, which is what a backfield will do once nobody expects anything of it. Pete's 126 was second in the league and **+18.27** over his average. For all of that the undefeated man rises just one place, to **sixth at 538.67**. In this league winning and scoring are like two Missouri cousins: related by blood, and they haven't spoken since the funeral.

Mitch's 70.5 ranked 11th of 12, -15.5 against his average. Trevor Lawrence gave 22 and Sam LaPorta 14. Past that the raft came apart in the spring rise: Kyren Williams 8, Brandin Cooks 8, Tyler Bass 7, Terry McLaurin 5, and both defenses 0. Mitch is 1-4 and last at 430. I'd send condolences, but condolences are only the manners we use to cover our relief.

---

## Chapter III. Concerning the Richest Pauper in the County

> *Calendar: Sean has enough points to buy the whole town, and the town won't sell him a single win.*

**Mike 122, Sean 112** — *"Quarter less three!"*

This was the nearest thing to a horse race all week, and it was settled by 10.

Sean leads the league in cumulative points at **598** and is **1-4** on a two-game losing streak. He has the finest house on the bluff and he sleeps in the barn. Jahmyr Gibbs gave **31** and Matthew Stafford **27**, both top ten. Derrick Henry gave 19, Brock Bowers 17, Kirk Cousins 11. The storylines say he bet on running backs in a quarterback league, and the bet paid. What sank him was his pair of defenses, Buffalo and Tampa Bay, at 0 apiece, who spent the afternoon on the hurricane deck admiring the scenery. His 112 was fifth-best in the league. In most towns that buys you supper. Here it bought a loss.

Mike went for **122**, third-best and **+25.27** over his average. Joe Burrow gave 26 and Jacoby Brissett 24, and Parker Washington's 25 beat Justin Jefferson's 14 by a wide stretch of river. When the deckhand outworks the captain, the captain ought to reread his contract. The New Orleans defense added 8. Mike is 2-3 and climbs one place to ninth at 483.67.

---

## Chapter IV. The Scorekeeper Keeps His Own Score, and It Comes Out Fine

> *Calendar: Always do right. It will gratify some people and astonish the rest.*

**The Scorekeeper 116.5, Bruce 102** — *"Mark twain!"*

Safe water and a steady hand on the wheel. The scorekeeper's 116.5 was -2.8 against his own average, an honest workmanlike crossing with no brass band. James Cook III gave 25, Jordan Love 24, Isaiah Likely 22, Mike Evans 19, Jameson Williams 16. The margin was 14.5. The scorekeeper is 3-2 on a W2 and climbs one place to **second at 596.5**, just **1.5** behind Sean. That gap is narrower than some arguments I've had with my own conscience, and I lost most of those too.

Bruce's 102 ranked ninth, -6.4 against his average. Davante Adams (19), Garrett Wilson (17) and Aaron Jones Sr. (17) pulled hard on the oars. Bijan Robinson gave 6, and his quarterbacks Bo Nix and Deshaun Watson gave 5 apiece. They put me in mind of the King and the Duke in *Huckleberry Finn*: two gentlemen of large reputation who rolled into town, performed something, and left the audience wondering what it had paid for. Bruce is 2-3 on a two-game skid, and yet he *rose* one place, to fourth at 542. He didn't climb. He stood still while the men around him fell through the floor, which is how a great many reputations in this country get made.

## Chapter V. A Short Sermon Against Good Advice

> *Calendar: Nothing so needs reforming as other people's habits, unless the man is 4-1.*

**Matt 111, Eli 85** — *"Quarter less four."*

Deep enough, and nobody aboard can say why. The storylines remind us again that Matt has **zero tight ends**. He has ignored the reminder and is **4-1** on a W2. Tom Sawyer got the whole neighborhood to whitewash his fence and thank him for it. Matt has got the league to watch him win without the position everybody else considers necessary, and I suspect he's charging admission.

Bucky Irving gave **28**, seventh-best in the league. Christian Watson gave 25 and Ollie Gordon II 14. Jason Myers kicked 11, exactly what Jalen Hurts managed at quarterback, and a man whose kicker has tied his quarterback has bigger mysteries to solve than tight ends. Matt's 111 was +2.97 over his average, and he rises one place to fifth at 540.17.

Eli got **27** from Jameis Winston, ninth-best in the league, plus 17 apiece from Puka Nacua and George Pickens and 14 from Dak Prescott. Then the bottom fell out: Justice Hill 3, both defenses 0. Eli's 85 ran -8 against his average. He's 1-4 and slips one place to tenth at 465.

---

## Chapter VI. Aground, With All Hands Accounted For and One Kicker Unaccounted For

> *Calendar: Prosperity is the best protector of principle, and Dan has just been robbed of both.*

**Cal 104.5, Dan 57.5** — *"No bottom!"* The leadsman stopped singing and went below to pray.

Dan came to the landing the way a steamboat does, unbeaten, whistle blowing, the whole town down at the wharf. He left with his first loss, **57.5 points**, the league low and **-46.8 against his own average**, and fell **four places** to eighth at 521.5. Aaron Rodgers did his duty with 21. J.K. Dobbins and Michael Penix Jr. gave 11 apiece, Javonte Williams 8, Khalil Shakir 6. Then **Cairo Santos kicked 0.5**, the shallowest sounding on the whole river. I've known men to get half an education, half a marriage and half a conscience, but I never saw a man get half a point until now. The Seattle and Los Angeles Chargers defenses gave 0 each, so at least Santos wasn't the low man in the lineup.

Cal rode **Jayden Daniels's 33**, the second-best performance in the league, to a 47-point win. Braelon Allen gave 17, MarShawn Lloyd 16, and Ja'Marr Chase and Kyler Murray 14 apiece. Cal's 104.5 was **+16.7** over his average. He's 2-3 and climbs one place to eleventh at 439. Prosperity is a fickle passenger. She rides with you until you start depending on her, then she gets off at the next landing without paying her fare. Dan has found out which landing that was.

---

## Conclusion, Being a Chart of the River

There are three kinds of lies: lies, damned lies, and cumulative standings. Sean is first at 598 and 1-4. The scorekeeper is second at 596.5, Aaron third at 589.5, Bruce fourth at 542, Matt fifth at 540.17 (4-1), Pete sixth at 538.67 (5-0), Chris seventh at 534.5, Dan eighth at 521.5 (4-1), Mike ninth at 483.67, Eli tenth at 465, Cal eleventh at 439, and Mitch twelfth at 430.

The undefeated man is sixth, the points leader has one win, and the man who scored 57.5 is still 4-1. If anybody tells you this league makes sense, ask to see his figures, and then count your spoons.
