# PFL Week 4: I Wrote This Recap on a Napkin, Then I Lost the Napkin, So This Is the Second Draft

*A set by Mitch Hedberg, delivered mostly facing the curtain. Sunglasses stay on. The mic stand stays crooked.*

---

Alright. Alright, thanks. I'm gonna do some fantasy football. I don't play fantasy football. I used to watch football, and I still do, but I used to too.

There's a guy in this league named Mitch. Not me. A different Mitch. I'm not sure there should be two of us, but I'm not gonna be the one to leave. I'll get to him. He won, which is weird, because I never do.

So the Peninsula Football League has twelve owners and they all go by their first names, like a support group. "Hi, I'm Cal." *Hi, Cal.* "I scored 73.5 points." ...We'll get to Cal too. Cal's gonna need a minute.

## Bit #1: Joe and Sean (the one where both guys showed up)

Joe beat Sean **148.5 to 136**. That's the highest score of the week. 148.5. I don't even know what half a point is. Somebody kicked a ball and it went halfway through? "That's good... ish."

Joe had **Brian Robinson Jr. at 27**, **Jonathan Taylor at 22**, and **Romeo Doubs at 19**. Romeo Doubs. That's a good name. Sounds like a guy who writes poems and then crosses most of them out. Then Joe had two quarterbacks, **Patrick Mahomes at 16** and **Jordan Love at 16**, exactly the same. That's not a quarterback room, that's a mirror. **James Cook III** threw in **14**. Joe went **+28.5 vs his own average**. He's 2-2 now with a W1 streak, and he went up two spots to **third in cumulative points with 480**.

And Sean. Poor Sean. Sean scored **136**, which was **+14.5 vs his own average**, and it was the **third**-best total of the week. And he lost. That's like getting dressed up for a party and the party is in the other direction. **Chuba Hubbard 25**, **Kirk Cousins 24**, **Alvin Kamara 16**, **Brock Bowers 14**. That's a good meal. It's just that Joe ordered more.

Here's the thing about Sean, though. Sean is **first in the whole league in cumulative points, 486**, up **two spots**. And he's **1-3**. I like that. That's a guy with the most money in the room who can't get a cab. The draft-grade people gave Sean an A-minus. He's got the points. He just doesn't have the... other thing. The winning thing. I forget what it's called.

## Bit #2: Matt and Cal (the blowout, I'm sorry, Cal)

There were **no close calls** this week. Nothing under ten points. Everybody either won by a lot or lost by a lot. It's like the whole league took the escalator, and nobody took the stairs.

And the biggest one was Matt over Cal, **137 to 73.5**. That's a margin of **63.5**. I've had sandwiches with less of a gap between the bread.

Matt had **Tetairoa McMillan at 28**, **Nico Collins at 25**, **Bryce Young at 22**, and **Ollie Gordon II at 17**. **Jake Bates** kicked in **9**. And here's the part I love: Matt has **zero tight ends**. None. Still. He just doesn't have one. That's like going camping without a tent and then it doesn't rain, and you feel smart, but you didn't do anything. Matt went **+29.71 vs his own average**, put up the **second**-best score of the week, moved up **three spots to sixth** with **429.17** cumulative, and he's **3-1**. No tight ends. Tight end is optional now, I guess. Like the second "e" in "judgement."

Cal. Cal got an **A** on his draft grade. An A. And he's **last in points, twelfth, 334.5**, down **two spots**, **1-3**, streak L2. That's like getting an A on the driving test and then you drive into the DMV. **CeeDee Lamb** gave him **20**. Then **Carnell Tate 9**, **Tyler Allgeier 8**. His quarterback, **Kyler Murray**, had **6**. Cal started two kickers, **Jake Elliott 5** and **Drew Stevens 4.5**, which I respect. If you're gonna lose, lose with extra kickers. Cal finished **-10.13 vs his own average**, which honestly is not that far off. Cal's average is just... Cal's average.

Dan slipped **two spots to fourth** in cumulative points at **464**. He's undefeated and he's going down in the standings. That's an escalator that's going up while you're walking down. You're winning, but you look tired.

Aaron had **Josh Allen at 22**, **Malik Nabers 17**, **Cam Ward 13**, then it gets quiet. **Amon-Ra St. Brown, 6**. Six. Amon-Ra St. Brown has a saint in his name and he gave you six. Aaron went **-26.63 vs his own average**, **eighth** in the week, and dropped **one spot to second** in cumulative at **480.5**. He's **3-1**, so he'll be fine. He just had a day. We all have a day. I have a lot of days. I'm having one right now.

## Bit #4: Mitch and Chris (the other Mitch)

So, the other Mitch. Mitch beat Chris **105 to 84.5**. It's Mitch's **first win**. Mitch used to lose every week. He still does, but not this week.

**Kyren Williams 25**, **Lamar Jackson 16**, **Sam LaPorta 14**, **Tee Higgins 12**. And the **New York Giants Defense** gave him **8**, which is the most any defense scored this week. A defense scored points. I didn't know they were allowed. Mitch went **+15.13 vs his own average** and is still **eleventh** in cumulative points, **359.5**, no movement. Streak W1. I'm happy for him. I'm not gonna call him. But I'm happy.

Chris is the saddest story of the night, and I'll tell you why. Chris had **Drake Maye at 29**. That was the **number one individual performance in the whole league**. The best player on the field all week was on Chris's team. And Chris lost by **20.5**.

That's the centerpiece, folks. Drake Maye, **29**. Number one. Then **Kenneth Walker III**, **Joe Burrow**, and **Tetairoa McMillan** all tied behind him at **28**. Maye's the top. And it didn't matter. That's like winning the raffle at a funeral.

Chris also had **Jared Goff at 20** and **Christian McCaffrey at 11**. And then... Chris has **five tight ends and two receivers**. Five tight ends. That's not a roster, that's a family reunion where everyone has the same job. **Harold Fannin Jr.** gave him **8**. Chris got a **D+** on his draft, and he's **1-3**, streak **L3**, last five WLLL, **tenth** in the week at **84.5**, **-12.38 vs his own average**, down **one spot to eighth** in cumulative at **387.5**. Five tight ends. I'd trade one of them for a receiver. Or a sandwich. A sandwich can't drop a pass, because nobody throws it to the sandwich.

Pete's thing is "best receiver room, no backfield," and this week that was extremely true. **C.J. Stroud 22**, **Zay Flowers 17**, **Brock Purdy 16**. Two defenses, **Minnesota Vikings 5** and **Chicago Bears 5**. Then in the backfield, **Rhamondre Stevenson 10**, and then the week's official **bust**: **Najee Harris, 2 points**. Two. Najee Harris got two points. That's not a running back, that's a running back's pulse. Pete scored **95.5**, **-7.67 vs his own average**, **seventh** in the week, and won by **31.5** anyway. Pete dropped **one spot to seventh** in cumulative, **412.67**. Undefeated and seventh. It's like being the tallest guy in an elevator that's going down.

Mike scored **64**. That's the **league low**. **Twelfth** out of twelve. **-26.42 vs his own average**. And here's the heartbreak, if you can call 31.5 points a heartbreak, which you can't, but I'm gonna. **Joe Burrow scored 28.** Twenty-eight! Tied for second-best in the league! And behind him it went **Jacoby Brissett 10**, **Tucker Kraft 8**, **Matthew Golden 8**, **Cameron Dicker 7**, **D'Andre Swift 3**. Joe Burrow carried that team like a guy carrying a couch up the stairs by himself while his friends say "you got it, you got it." He didn't got it.
