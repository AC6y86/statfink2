# It's a Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood: PFL Week 2

*Hello, neighbor. Come in. Let me hang up this jacket and put on my sweater — there, that's better — and change into my sneakers. Won't you sit with me a while? We have twelve friends to talk about, and some of them had a hard week, and I think that's worth talking about slowly.*

## Feeding the Fish, Counting the Points

The whole neighborhood scored an average of **96.54 points** this week. That's a number. Numbers can feel very big and very important, but they're just a way of counting what happened, and counting is something we can do together without anybody getting scared.

Four of the six games were decided by fewer than ten points. Four. That's a lot of neighbors going to bed on Sunday night with their hearts beating a little quickly. It's alright to feel that way.

## Aaron 146, Bruce 118

Do you know what I like about **Josh Allen**? He scored **40 points** this week, and last week he scored a great many points too, and he still got up on Monday morning and was simply himself. Forty points is the best anyone did in the whole neighborhood, and it carried **Aaron to 146** — his week rank of 1, and **+6 above his own average**, which is a hard thing to do when your own average is already the envy of everybody.

Aaron had helpers, the way we always do. **Amon-Ra St. Brown** brought **25**. **Cam Ward** brought **22**. **Chris Olave** and **Dalton Kincaid** each brought **14**, **Terrance Ferguson 11**, **Brandon Aubrey 8**, and **Chase Brown** and **Blake Corum 6** apiece. Aaron is **2-0**, sitting at rank 1 with **280 cumulative points**, having climbed **two spots**.

Now — I want you to notice something. **Bruce scored 118.** That was the second-best score in the entire neighborhood, **+13.5 above his own average**. **Davante Adams** had **28**. **Tyler Shough** had **22**. **Deshaun Watson** had **16**, **Garrett Wilson 11**, and **Aaron Jones Sr. 9**, with **Emeka Egbuka**, **Jonah Coleman**, and **Tank Bigsby** each bringing **8**. Bruce did almost everything right and lost anyway.

That happens, neighbor. You can do your very best work and still have a hard day, and it does not mean your work wasn't good. Bruce rose **five places** in the standings to rank 5, with **209** points. He is **1-1**. I like him just the way he is.

## Matt 96.67, Mitch 76.5

**Matt** won by **20.17** — the biggest margin of the day after Aaron's — and he did it without a single tight end on his team, which is a bit like building a house with no doorknobs. **Bryce Young** brought **24**, **Jalen Hurts 19**, **Denzel Boston 14**, the **Carolina Panthers Defense 9.67**, **Tetairoa McMillan 9**, **Christian Watson 8**, and **Bucky Irving 6**. Matt is **2-0** and held steady at rank 4 with **227.17**.

**Mitch** scored **76.5** and is **0-2**, at rank 12 with **160** points. **Kyren Williams** gave him **14**, **Lamar Jackson** and **Sam LaPorta 11** each, **Dalton Schultz 9**, **Chase McLaughlin 8.5**. Here's what I'd want Mitch to know: he was only **3.5 below his own average**. He didn't fall apart. The day simply belonged to someone else. Sometimes the trolley goes to a place you didn't ask for, and it still comes back.

## Dan 76.5, Mike 73.67

Oh, this one. **2.83 points.**

**Dan** fell from a league-high week in the neighborhood all the way down to **76.5**, which is **45.75 below his own average** — the steepest drop anybody had. **Travis Kelce** managed **17**, **Stefon Diggs 16**, **Kenneth Walker III 12**, **Daniel Jones 11**, **Harrison Butker 9.5**, **Aaron Rodgers 6**, and the **Seattle Seahawks Defense 5**. Dan's week rank was 11th out of twelve. And he *won*. He's still **2-0**, still rank 2 with **244.5** points, having slipped just **one spot**.

**Mike** scored **73.67** — the lowest total in the neighborhood, **23.17 below his own average** — and fell **four places** to rank 9 at **193.67**, and he is **0-2**. **DeVonta Smith** gave him **17**, **Joe Burrow 16**, **Baker Mayfield 11**, and the **New England Patriots Defense 9.67**. After that came a long, quiet row of threes: **De'Von Achane 3**, **Justin Jefferson 3**, **D'Andre Swift 3**, **Matthew Golden 3**, and **Harrison Mevis** with **2**.

Neighbor, the margin was **2.83**. Any one of those neighbors on Mike's team turning a 3 into a 6 would have changed the whole afternoon. That's a very hard thing to carry around. But feelings like disappointment are mentionable, and anything mentionable is manageable. Mike is allowed to be sad about 2.83, and Mike is allowed to come back next week.

## Joe 95, Chris 88

**Joe** won by **7**. **Patrick Mahomes** brought **27**, **Jonathan Taylor 22**, **James Cook III 17**, **Jordan Love 10**, and **Andre Szmyt 7**, with **Romeo Doubs 6** and threes from **Mike Evans** and **Brian Robinson Jr.** It was **21.25 below Joe's own average**, and it was enough, and "enough" is a perfectly wonderful thing to be. Joe is **1-1**, rank 3 with **232.5** points, down one place from where he stood.

**Chris** got **32 points from Jared Goff** — the third-best performance in the whole neighborhood — and **16 from Christian McCaffrey**, and **11 from Malik Willis**, and then **Trey McBride 8** and **Tyler Warren 8** from that famous crowded room of five tight ends. And at the very end of the list sat **Travis Etienne Jr.**, with **2 points**.

I'd like to say something about Travis. Two points is a small number. But a person isn't a number, and neither is a Sunday. Chris slipped one place to rank 7 with **199.5** and is **1-1**. Tomorrow is a new day.

## Pete 90.67, Eli 88

**2.67 points.** The closest game in the neighborhood.

**Jaxon Smith-Njigba** scored **36** — second-best anywhere this week — and **Brock Purdy** added **27**, and **C.J. Stroud 12**, and even the **Minnesota Vikings Defense** chipped in **1.67**, which turned out to matter an awful lot. **Pete is 2-0**, holding steady at rank 8 with **195.17** points, though his **90.67** was **6.92 below his own average**. Two wins, and both of them held together with careful hands.

**Eli** scored **88**, just **1.75 below his own average**. **Dak Prescott** gave him **29**. **Omarion Hampton**, **TreVeyon Henderson**, and **George Kittle** each gave **14**. **Jaylen Waddle 9**. **Will Reichard 6**. **Nick Folk 2**. And Eli is **0-2**, down one place to rank 10 with **179.5**.

Eli's week was very nearly a good week. It was a good week that arrived on the wrong day. And his neighborhood has been visited by more than its share of doctors lately, which we'll get to.

## Cal 109.5, Sean 100

**Cal** scored **109.5** — third-best this week and **+25.75 above his own average**, the biggest jump over one's own self that anybody managed. **CeeDee Lamb 28**. **Ja'Marr Chase 22**. **Jake Ferguson 16**. **Bhayshul Tuten 11**, and **8** each from **Jayden Daniels**, **MarShawn Lloyd**, and **Braelon Allen**, with **Drew Stevens 5** and **Jake Elliott 3.5**. Cal is **1-1** now, up one place to rank 11 with **167.5**.

**Sean** scored **100** — his fourth-best-in-the-neighborhood day — behind **Matthew Stafford's 32**, **Kirk Cousins' 24**, **Jahmyr Gibbs' 14**, and **11** apiece from **Derrick Henry** and **Chuba Hubbard**. And Sean is **0-2**, even while sitting at rank 6 with **209** cumulative points — the same **209** as Bruce, who is 1-1.

Isn't that something? Two neighbors, the exact same pile of points, two different stories. The scoreboard doesn't always tell you who you are.

## The Neighborhood of Make-Believe

Four friends are **2-0**: Aaron, Dan, Matt, and Pete. Four friends are **0-2**: Sean, Mike, Eli, and Mitch. Four are **1-1**: Joe, Bruce, Chris, and Cal.

Some of those unbeaten neighbors were told before the season that their teams weren't very good, and some of the winless ones were told theirs were wonderful. I think that's worth remembering the next time somebody hands you a grade on a piece of paper. You are not what somebody wrote about you in September.

## Goodbye, Neighbor

*Let me change back into my jacket.*

You've made this week a special week by just your being you. Aaron scored 146 and Mike scored 73.67 and neither of those numbers says anything at all about whether you're a good person. There's only one person in this whole world like each of you, and I like you exactly the way you are.

I'll be back next week. Won't you be my neighbor?
