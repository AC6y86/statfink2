# PFL PrimeTime, Week 3: "He Could... Go... ALL... THE... WAY!"

*Chris Berman calling the fastest highlights in the Peninsula Football League*

---

Hello again, everybody, and welcome to PFL PrimeTime! Week 3 is in the books, and what a week it was. We had a 141-point explosion, a 68.5-point beatdown, and a game decided by half a point. HALF a point! You can't make this stuff up, folks, and that's why they play the games.

Three teams are still perfect: Aaron, Dan and Pete, all 3-0. Two teams are still looking for their first win: Mitch and Eli, both 0-3. Tom Jackson isn't here tonight, so you're stuck with me. Let's go to the videotape!

---

## THE CENTERPIECE: Jahmyr "Gibbs Me Shelter" Gibbs

We start with the top performer of the week, and it isn't close. Jahmyr "Gibbs Me Shelter" Gibbs put up **33 points** for Sean, the best line in the league. He takes the handoff, he's at the 40, the 30, the 20... he... could... go... ALL... THE... WAY! Whoop!

Back in the Herd-style draft recap, the talking heads said Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league and gave him an A-minus anyway. This week the bet paid off. Gibbs got 33, Derrick "Hooked on" Henry added 22, and the quarterback room of Matthew "Stafford Loan" Stafford (22) and Kirk "Kissin' Cousins" Cousins (21) chipped in too. Brock "Bowers of Babylon" Bowers put up 17 at tight end, and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers Defense added 5.

That's **141 points**, the most in the league, and **+24.33 over his own average.** It was Sean's first win of the year.

---

## SEAN 141, ELI 72.5: The Blowout of the Week

Eli was on the other side of that one, and it was ugly. Sean won by **68.5**, the biggest margin of the week. Eli's 72.5 was 11.5 below his own average, and he falls two spots to **twelfth, dead last**, at 0-3 with 252 cumulative.

There was one bright spot. George "Kittle Bit Country" Kittle put up **22** at tight end. Dak Prescott added 16 and Jakobi Meyers had 11. After that, the lineup went rumblin', bumblin', stumblin'. Jaylen "Waddle Ya Know" Waddle made the **busts list** with **2 points**. Eli also had two kickers counting, Will Reichard (7) and Nick Folk (5.5), while Omarion Hampton managed 3.

Eli got a B-plus draft grade for betting on 2028. Right now he's 0-3 in 2026, and the backfield injuries keep coming.

---

## MIKE 104, CHRIS 103.5: The Heartbreak Hotel

Now pull up a chair. This was the closest game of the season so far, a **0.5-point** finish. Tick... tick... tick... tick... BOOM!

Mike won it with a pair of quarterbacks. Jacoby "Brisket" Brissett threw up **27**, and Geno "Gino's East" Smith added **26**. Those are the 7th and 8th best lines in the league. Matthew "Golden Years" Golden caught 17 and Harrison Mevis kicked in 9. Those 9 points from Mevis loom large, since Chris's kicker, Evan McPherson, had 5.5. In a half-point game, every kick counts.

Chris was right there. Jared "Goff the Deep End" Goff scored 19, Harold "Fannin the Flames" Fannin Jr. added 19, Christian "Cup of Coffee" McCaffrey had 14 and Michael Wilson had 14. The Denver Broncos Defense put up 8. Chris was **+2.5 over his own average** and still lost. Travis Etienne Jr., Cam Skattebo and Tyler Warren each managed 3.

Here's the what-if that'll keep Chris up at night. His lineup had Fannin **and** Trey McBride (6) **and** Tyler Warren at tight end. This is the five-tight-end, two-receiver roster from draft night, and it came up one half-point short. Somewhere, Matt, who has zero tight ends, is looking at that roster like a man staring at a buffet through the window. Make the trade, fellas!

Mike moves up one spot to **8th** at 1-2 (297.67 cumulative). Chris holds at **7th** at 1-2 (303), but he's on an **L2 streak**. Oh, the humanity.

---

## PETE 122, JOE 99: Pete Keeps Winning

Pete got a C-plus draft grade, and he's **3-0**, folks. The narrative was "best WR room, no backfield," and this week the passing game carried him the whole way.

Sam "Dar-nold Palmer" Darnold put up **32**, second-best in the league. Brock "Pie-in-the-Sky" Purdy added **29**, and Jaxon "Smith-Njigba and a Half" Smith-Njigba caught **25**. That's three top-10 performances on one roster. Drake London added 12 and Cooper Kupp 8. The backfield? Ty Johnson 3, Najee Harris 3. Pete didn't need a running game because he had the air traffic control tower.

Pete scored 122, **+16.28 over his own average** and the third-best total of the week. He jumps **two spots to 6th**, but he's only at 317.17 cumulative. His record is running ahead of his scoring, and he's got a 3-0 record either way.

Joe (A-minus on draft night) scored 99, **-11.5 vs his own average.** James Cook III had 20, Jordan Love 19 and Patrick Mahomes 16, but Jonathan Taylor gave him just 3 and Ladd McConkey 3. Joe slides **two spots to 5th** at 1-2 with 331.5.

---

## BRUCE 138.5, CAL 93.5: Bruce Rolls

Bruce also got a C-plus on draft night, and this week he dropped the second-best score in the league: **138.5**, **+22.67 over his own average.** Three players scored 28 or more:

- Tyler "Shough Shine Boy" Shough: **29**
- Bijan "Mustafa" Robinson: **28**
- Bo "Diddley" Nix: **28**

Garrett Wilson added 17 and Jeremiyah Love 14. Bruce won by **45** and climbs **one spot to 4th** at 2-1 with 347.5 cumulative.

Cal was the A-grade darling of the draft, and he's **1-2**. He scored 93.5, which was actually **+6.5 over his own average**, but it was nowhere close. Ja'Marr Chase had 14, Jordan Addison 14, CeeDee Lamb 11, and Bhayshul Tuten and Mike Washington Jr. had 11 apiece. The Washington Commanders Defense added 8. The quarterback slot, Kyler Murray, managed just 5, and Ashton Jeanty had 3. In a league where Bruce's quarterbacks scored 29 and 28, a 5 from your QB makes it tough. Cal moves up one spot to **10th** (261), a small consolation.

---

## AARON 107, MATT 65: Aaron Keeps the Lead

Aaron is the league leader, and this was one of his worse weeks: 107, **22 below his own average.** He still won by **42**. Josh "Party" Allen threw for **22**, Juwan Johnson caught **19** at tight end, and Luther Burden III and Cam Ward each added 11. Aaron is **3-0**, still **first** with **387** cumulative points, and he's got a W3 streak.

Matt had a bad week. **65 points**, the lowest in the league, **-32.39 vs his own average.** He came in 2-0 and fell **five spots to 9th** at 2-1 (292.17). Christian Watson and Bryce Young had 14 each, and Justin Herbert and Keaton Mitchell had 11 each. Saquon Barkley managed 6. Denzel Boston made the **busts list** with **2 points**.

And there's the tight end problem again. Aaron got 19 from his tight end. Matt has none on the roster. Chris has five, and he just lost by half a point. Somebody pick up the phone!

---

## DAN 106.5, MITCH 94.5: Dan Stays Unbeaten

Dan was the other C-plus, and he's **3-0.** Aaron "Rodgers and Hammerstein" Rodgers threw for **24**, tenth-best in the league. Kenneth "Walk This Way" Walker III added 19, Michael Penix Jr. 16, and Javonte Williams and Deebo Samuel Sr. had 14 each. Travis "Kelce Grammar" Kelce added 11. The draft room said Dan bet on 2022, and it's still working. He won by **12**, which is closer than he'd like at 106.5 (**-10.5 vs his own average**), but he holds at **2nd** with **351**.

Mitch is **0-3**, and he's the unluckiest team in the league. He scored 94.5, **+9.67 vs his own average**, and still lost. Trevor "Lawrence of Arabia" Lawrence had 21, Lamar Jackson 19, Tee Higgins and Terry McLaurin 14 each, and Wan'Dale Robinson 11. His losing streak is up to L3. He did move **up one spot to 11th** (254.5), so he's out of the basement for now.
