# FIRST TAKE: PFL WEEK 1 — "STAY OFF THE WEED, CAL"

*Ladies and gentlemen, welcome in. I'm Screamin' A, and let me be clear — LET ME BE CLEAR — we sat here all summer listening to twelve grown men tell us about their drafts. Their process. Their VISION. Well, the games got played. And the games, my friends, do not care about your vision.*

---

## SEGMENT ONE: DAN 168, JOE 137.5 — THE HIGHEST-SCORING GAME IN THE LEAGUE, AND SOMEBODY HAD TO LOSE IT

Here's what I'm not gonna do. I'm not gonna sit here and pretend this was fair.

Joe put up **137.5 points**. One hundred thirty-seven and a half! That's the second-most points anybody scored in this entire league in Week 1. Joe beats Aaron this week. Joe beats Matt. Joe beats Mike, Chris, Sean, Pete, Eli, Bruce, Mitch and Cal. Joe beats *ten of eleven opponents* with that number — and he's sitting at **0-1** because the schedule handed him **Dan**, who dropped **168** on him like a piano off a fire escape.

And who did it? **Caleb Williams, 38 points.** **Kenneth Walker III, 28.** **David Montgomery, 27.** Three men, three top-ten performances in one lineup. THREE! Meanwhile Dan's "bet on 2022" core showed up like an old band on a reunion tour — **Stefon Diggs 11**, **Aaron Rodgers 11**, **Deebo Samuel Sr. 8** — and you know what? Nobody boos the encore when the headliner's already given you 93 points off the top.

Joe did everything a man can do. **Patrick Mahomes 24. Jonathan Taylor 22. Jordan Love 22. Isaiah Likely 22.** Four twenty-plus performers. That A- draft grade? EARNED. And it bought him precisely nothing.

Now here's the part that hurts. The draft recap called Joe one of the two "adult builds" in this league. And the adult build lost to a **C+**. Dan got graded C+ and hung 168. HOW DARE YOU question the C+! Actually — hold up, hold up, hold up. Don't get it twisted. It's one week. But Dan sits at **Rank 1 with 168 cumulative points**, and everybody else is chasing.

---

## SEGMENT TWO: AARON 134, SEAN 109 — TWO HAYMAKERS, ONE MAN LEFT STANDING

**Josh Allen, 38 points.** Tied with Caleb Williams for the highest individual score in the entire Peninsula Football League this week. That's not production, that's a STATEMENT.

And Sean? Sean didn't flinch. **Derrick Henry, 33. Jahmyr Gibbs, 28. Chuba Hubbard, 16.** Sean bet on running backs in a quarterback league — six backs, the whole philosophy — and those backs delivered exactly what he paid for. But I'm gonna tell ya somethin' about arithmetic, and arithmetic is undefeated: Sean's quarterback room, the one the draft recap said was "held together with tape and prayer," produced **Kirk Cousins, 15**. Fifteen. Against thirty-eight.

The prayer did not get answered, my friends.

Aaron didn't need heroics after Allen. He needed help, and **Jalen Coker gave him 25**, **Amon-Ra St. Brown 19**, **Chris Olave 12**, **Juwan Johnson 11**, **Chase Brown 11**. Balanced. Businesslike. B+ football. And yes — Aaron drafted two kickers, and **Cam Little kicked in 6** of them. I'm not saying the second kicker is a plan. I'm just saying **1-0** looks better than A- and **0-1**.

---

## SEGMENT THREE: PETE 104.5, CAL 58 — THE BEATDOWN OF THE WEEK

**FORTY-SIX AND A HALF POINTS.** That's the margin. That's not a loss, that's a public accounting.

Cal came into this season with the only **A** on the board. The A! The adult build! Stars early, starters late! And Cal scored **58 points** in a week where the league averaged **111.58**. FIFTY-EIGHT! **Ashton Jeanty put up 25** — a quarter of Cal's entire output came from one man — and after that it's **Jayden Daniels 10**, **CeeDee Lamb 8**, and then a parade of threes and a **Drew Stevens 2.5** that I'm still trying to emotionally process.

And Pete? The **C+**. The man with "the best WR room, no backfield." He got **Brock Purdy 21, Zay Flowers 20, C.J. Stroud 19, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17** — and then a running back named **Jacory Croskey-Merritt gave him 11** to slam the door. Pete won the biggest blowout in the league with the eighth-highest score in the league, and quite frankly, that's the most Pete thing imaginable. He didn't need to be great. He needed to be present. Cal wasn't.

---

## SEGMENT FOUR: MATT 130.5, MIKE 120 — THE TIGHT END THAT WASN'T

The man with **zero tight ends** put up 130.5 points. I've got questions. I've got CONCERNS.

**Bryce Young, 35.** Third-highest individual score in this league. **Christian Watson, 25. Jalen Hurts, 21. Nico Collins, 14. Denzel Boston, 11.** Matt built a roster without a single tight end and beat a man who had **Justin Jefferson** and a **33-point D'Andre Swift** in his lineup.

Because here's what happened to Mike. Mike got **Swift 33** — fourth-best performance in the league — and **Jefferson 24**, and then his quarterbacks split the check, **Joe Burrow 14 and Baker Mayfield 14**, and his kicker **Cameron Dicker gave him ONE POINT.** One! Matt's **Jason Myers went 4.5**. In a game decided by **10.5**, you cannot have your kicker show up in flip-flops. You just CAN'T.

---

## SEGMENT FIVE: CHRIS 111.5, ELI 91.5 — THE D+ GETS THE LAST LAUGH

Chris got graded **D+**. D-PLUS! Five tight ends and two receivers, they said. Broken shape, they said.

Well — the broken shape went out there and played tight end football with a smile on its face. **Dallas Goedert, 22. Trey McBride, 14. Tyler Warren, 8.** Add **Jared Goff 16**, **Cam Skattebo 14**, **Malik Willis 14**, and an **Evan McPherson 9.5**, and the man wins by **20 points**. Meanwhile Matt over in Segment Four is out here rostering nobody at the position. SOMEBODY GET THESE TWO MEN ON THE PHONE. That trade is sitting there like a twenty-dollar bill on the sidewalk.

Eli bet on 2028 and, God bless him, 2028 showed up early in spots — **Jaxson Dart 24**, **Kyle Monangai 17**, **Dak Prescott 16**, the **Pittsburgh Steelers Defense 8**. But **91.5** doesn't beat anybody who came to work.

---

## SEGMENT SIX: BRUCE 91, MITCH 83.5 — THE ONLY GAME IN THIS LEAGUE WORTH SWEATING

**7.5 points.** That was the margin. The ONLY single-digit game on the entire slate.

And I want you to appreciate the poetry here. Bruce — the man with the QB GUILT, the man who passed on nine first-round quarterbacks and then drafted Nix, Shough and Watson — was carried to victory by **Tyler Shough, 30 points**, sixth-best in the league. THE GUILT PAID HIM BACK. Add **Bijan Robinson 20**, **Deshaun Watson 11**, **Chris Boswell 5**, and Bruce escapes.

Mitch had **Trevor Lawrence 26** and **Lamar Jackson 22** and **Breece Hall 17** and lost by seven and a half. You know what his kicker **Tyler Bass** scored? **7.5.** The exact margin. I'm not saying it's cruel. I'm saying the football gods have a sense of humor and it is MEAN.

---

## THE STANDINGS, AND WHY YOU SHOULD BE NERVOUS

Everybody's got one game. Nobody's got history — every matchup this week was a first meeting. But points are points, and the ledger reads: **Dan 1st (168)**, **Joe 2nd (137.5)**, **Aaron 3rd (134)**, **Matt 4th (130.5)**, **Mike 5th (120)**, **Chris 6th (111.5)**, **Sean 7th (109)**, **Pete 8th (104.5)**, **Eli 9th (91.5)**, **Bruce 10th (91)**, **Mitch 11th (83.5)**, **Cal 12th (58)**.

Six men are **1-0**: Dan, Aaron, Matt, Chris, Pete, Bruce. Six are **0-1**: Joe, Mike, Sean, Eli, Mitch, Cal. And Bruce is **1-0 sitting 10th in points** while Joe is **0-1 sitting 2nd**. That's this league. That's football. Nobody said it was fair. I said it was BEAUTIFUL.
