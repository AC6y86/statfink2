# BOOM! The All-Madden Week 3 Telecast: Turduckens, Telestrators, and a Half-Point You Could Fit in a Thermos

*Live from the PFL broadcast booth. Coach is at the telestrator. The Madden Cruiser is parked out back with the engine running.*

---

## PREGAME: Now Here's What You've Gotta Understand

Well, lemme tell ya something about fantasy football. The team that scores more points than the other team? That team usually wins. I've been saying that for years, and this week it held up in all six games, including one where the difference was *half a point.* Half a point! You can't even eat half a turducken. I've tried. You just end up eating the whole thing and then a little of the next one.

Three weeks in and we've got three unbeaten teams: **Aaron, Dan, and Pete, all 3-0.** On the other side you've got **Mitch and Eli, both 0-3.** And in the middle you've got one heck of a pile of guys in the trenches, and that's where it gets fun.

Okay. Grab the telestrator. I'm drawing circles.

---

## GAME ONE: Sean 141, Eli 72.5. BOOM. DOINK. WHAP.

We're starting with the big one, because that's what you do. You serve the turkey first.

**Sean put up 141 points, most in the league this week,** and it was 24.33 above his own average. Now here's a guy, **Jahmyr Gibbs**. He scored **33 points**, the best single performance in the whole PFL this week. I'm drawing a big yellow line on the screen right now. Watch this. Gibbs gets the ball, and *whap*, he's through the hole, and *boom*, he's in the second level, and now there's nobody there, just grass and a guy in a striped shirt running along behind him. That's a running back! That's what football is all about!

Now let me tell you about Sean's draft. Everybody said he bet on running backs in a quarterback league. Well, lemme tell ya, this week he had both. **Derrick Henry went for 22**, and Henry's the kind of guy you don't tackle, you just sort of *agree* to go to the ground with him. Then in the quarterback room, **Matthew Stafford 22** and **Kirk Cousins 21.** Throw in **Brock Bowers with 17** at tight end and you got yourself a whole Thanksgiving table.

And Eli? Eli scored **72.5**, second-lowest in the league. **George Kittle** did his part with **22**, and Kittle's one of those guys I love, a tight end who'll block you and then laugh about it. **Dak Prescott had 16.** But then you look over at the receivers and **Jaylen Waddle gave him 2 points.** Two! That's one of the week's busts, and I'm circling it in red. **Omarion Hampton managed 3.** Eli played two kickers, **Will Reichard 7** and **Nick Folk 5.5**, and when your kickers are keeping you in it, pal, you're not in it.

**Final margin: 68.5.** Biggest of the week. The Cowherd draft recap gave Eli a B+ and said he was betting on 2028. Well, it's still 2026, and he's **0-3**, **12th in the standings with 252 cumulative**. Sean jumped **up three spots to third at 350**, and he's 1-2 now after a season that started with two losses. That's a resurrection. Somebody get that man a drumstick.

---

## GAME TWO: Mike 104, Chris 103.5. The Half-Point Heartbreaker.

Oh, now THIS is a football game. This is the kind of game where you're sitting in the booth and you forget to eat. Almost.

**Mike 104, Chris 103.5. Margin: 0.5.** The closest game of the season. You know what half a point is? That's a guy stepping out of bounds a yard early. That's a kicker's ball going *doink* off the upright instead of right down the middle.

Mike won it with quarterbacks, and I love that. **Jacoby Brissett 27** and **Geno Smith 26.** Now Brissett's a big, tough guy, the kind of quarterback who stands in there and takes the shot and gets back up and spits out a little grass. Then there's **Matthew Golden with 17** at receiver. And here's where I want you to look, I'm drawing it now: kicker **Harrison Mevis had 9**. Chris's kicker, **Evan McPherson, had 5.5.** In a half-point game, that's where the telestrator goes. Right there. On the kickers. Nobody ever talks about the kickers.

Chris had a whole bunch of tight ends. The draft book says five tight ends and two receivers, and this week three of those tight ends counted: **Harold Fannin Jr. 19, Trey McBride 6, Tyler Warren 3.** **Jared Goff threw for 19**, **Christian McCaffrey 14**, **Michael Wilson 14**, and the **Denver Broncos defense put up 8.** But then down at the bottom, **Travis Etienne Jr. 3** and **Cam Skattebo 3.** One more half-point from either one of those guys and we're talking about a tie.

Mike's **1-2 now, up one spot to eighth** (297.67). Chris is **1-2, seventh** (303), with **two losses in a row.** Tough actin' Tinactin, Chris. That one's gonna itch.

---

## GAME THREE: Bruce 138.5, Cal 93.5. Three Horses Pullin' the Wagon.

Bruce scored **138.5**, second-best in the league, 22.67 above his average. And you want to know how? He had three guys at 28 or better. That's not a lineup, that's a stampede.

**Tyler Shough, 29. Bijan Robinson, 28. Bo Nix, 28.** I'm drawing three arrows and they're all going the same way, straight downfield. Bijan's one of those backs where you just go "oh, look at that" over and over, because he does something every play that makes you go "oh, look at that." **Garrett Wilson added 17** and **Jeremiyah Love chipped in 14.**

Cal had a bunch of guys in the double digits: **Ja'Marr Chase 14, Jordan Addison 14, CeeDee Lamb 11, Bhayshul Tuten 11, Mike Washington Jr. 11.** Lotta 11s and 14s. That's like having a bunch of good offensive linemen and no quarterback. And speaking of quarterbacks, **Kyler Murray had 5.** In this league! Five! You need the quarterback, Cal. It's the PFL. Quarterbacks are the meat of the turducken.

**Bruce wins by 45**, goes to **2-1** and climbs **one spot to fourth at 347.5.** Cal, who got an **A** from the draft guys, is **1-2 and tenth** at 261. Grades are nice. Points are nicer.

---

## GAME FOUR: Pete 122, Joe 99. The C+ Fella Keeps Winning.

Pete's **3-0.** They gave him a C+ in the draft. Here's the thing about a C+: it doesn't block anybody.

**Sam Darnold 32** (second-best performance of the week), **Brock Purdy 29**, and **Jaxon Smith-Njigba 25.** Now JSN is a guy who just gets open. You don't know how. He's standing there, then he's not standing there, then he's got the football and he's going *thataway*. **Drake London added 12.** The draft book said Pete had the best receiver room and no backfield, and sure enough, **Ty Johnson 3, Najee Harris 3.** Didn't matter. When your quarterbacks score 32 and 29, you can have a backfield made of folding chairs.

Joe did fine for a while. **James Cook III 20, Jordan Love 19, Patrick Mahomes 16, Brian Robinson Jr. 11.** But then **Jonathan Taylor 3** and **Ladd McConkey 3**, and that's where the drive stalls. That's a third-and-long you're not converting.

**Pete wins by 23,** moves **up two spots to sixth** (317.17). Now here's a funny thing. Pete's got the undefeated record but he's only sixth in cumulative points. The record's out ahead of the scoring. Joe drops **two spots to fifth** at 331.5, **1-2.**

---

## GAME FIVE: Aaron 107, Matt 65. Winning Ugly Is Still Winning.

Aaron scored **107**. That's **22 below his own average**, and he **still won by 42.** That's the kind of week where you play bad and it doesn't matter because the other guy played worse.

**Josh Allen had 22.** Josh Allen's a big ol' quarterback, and when he runs he's not sliding, he's looking for somebody to hit. **Juwan Johnson had 19 at tight end**, **Luther Burden III 11, Cam Ward 11.**

And Matt... oh boy. **Matt scored 65, dead last in the league,** 32.39 below his average. **Christian Watson 14, Bryce Young 14, Justin Herbert 11, Keaton Mitchell 11.** Then **Saquon Barkley, 6**, and **Denzel Boston, 2.** That's the other bust of the week and I'm circling it too. And he played **zero tight ends**, because he's got zero tight ends. You know who has a whole garage full of tight ends? Chris. Just saying. I'm not a general manager. I just draw on the screen.

Aaron stays **3-0**, stays **first at 387**. Matt's **2-1** but **fell five spots to ninth** (292.17). Biggest drop of the week.

---

## GAME SIX: Dan 106.5, Mitch 94.5. The Old Guys Keep Grindin'.

Dan's team is built like an old offensive line. Everybody's been around, everybody knows where to go. **Aaron Rodgers 24** (tenth-best performance in the league), **Kenneth Walker III 19, Michael Penix Jr. 16, Javonte Williams 14, Deebo Samuel Sr. 14,** and **Travis Kelce 11.** They said he bet on 2022. Well, 2022 is **3-0**.

Mitch scored **94.5**, which is **9.67 above his average**, and he still lost. **Trevor Lawrence 21, Lamar Jackson 19, Tee Higgins 14, Terry McLaurin 14.** That's a nice, balanced day, and it wasn't enough. **Dan wins by 12.**

Dan stays **3-0, second at 351**. Mitch is **0-3** but moved **up one spot to 11th** (254.5). He keeps scoring, he keeps losing. Somebody's gonna pay for that eventually, and I hope I'm in the booth when it happens.
