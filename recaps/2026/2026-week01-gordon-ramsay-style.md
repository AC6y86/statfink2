# PFL KITCHEN NIGHTMARES — WEEK 1 SERVICE REPORT

**Chef's note, taped to the pass, written in Sharpie, underlined twice:**
Twelve kitchens. One dinner service. And I've just walked through the lot of
them and, my God, some of you should be arrested by the health department.

Right. Aprons on. Let's go through this service station by station, because
the league average tonight was **111.58 points** and roughly half of you
couldn't hit it with a GPS and a hostage negotiator.

---

## Station One: Dan 168, Joe 137.5

FINALLY. Some good food.

I walk into Dan's kitchen expecting a car crash — this is the man the draft
critics stamped **C+**, the man who "bet on 2022," the oldest core in the
league, Diggs and Deebo and Rodgers like a tasting menu written in 2022 and
reheated in a microwave. And what does he plate up? **168 points.** The
highest number anybody put on a plate all week.

**Caleb Williams, 38.** Beautiful. Seasoned, rested, cooked through, the
single best dish in the building. Behind him a backfield that would make a
French brigade weep: **Kenneth Walker III 28** and **David Montgomery 27**,
three of the week's top nine performances all coming out of one pass.
**Javonte Williams 16**, **DJ Moore 17**, and yes, the antiques — **Stefon
Diggs 11**, **Aaron Rodgers 11**, **Deebo Samuel Sr. 8** — all of them showed
up and did their job without setting the curtains on fire.

And Joe? Joe scored **137.5**. That is the SECOND-MOST POINTS IN THE ENTIRE
LEAGUE and he's standing there holding an L like a waiter holding a dropped
soufflé. **Patrick Mahomes 24. Jonathan Taylor 22. Jordan Love 22. Isaiah
Likely 22.** Four dishes at 22 or better out of an **A-** graded draft, the
"adult build," and the reward is **0-1**. That's not bad cooking, love.
That's bad luck. That's being handed a perfect fillet and seated next to a
man with a blowtorch.

What if Joe's 137.5 lands in literally any other kitchen this week? He beats
all five of them. Every single one. Instead: second in cumulative points,
**0-1**, and a streak that reads **L1**.

---

## Station Two: Pete 104.5, Cal 58

Shut it DOWN.

Cal, come here. You were handed the best grade in the draft — **A**. Stars
early, starters late, the so-called adult build. And you served **58 points**.
FIFTY. EIGHT. That's not a lineup, that's a garnish. **Ashton Jeanty 25** did
everything a man can do, **Jayden Daniels 10** turned up, and then the rest of
the menu is **CeeDee Lamb 8**, **Jake Elliott 3.5**, and a parade of threes —
**Bhayshul Tuten 3, Rome Odunze 3, Tyler Allgeier 3** — like somebody sent out
nine identical amuse-bouches and called it dinner. It's RAW. It's stone cold
in the middle and it's RAW.

Pete, meanwhile — graded **C+**, drafted the best receiver room and no
backfield at all — needed **104.5** and barely broke a sweat doing it.
**Brock Purdy 21, Zay Flowers 20, C.J. Stroud 19, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 17**, and
a **Jacory Croskey-Merritt 11** off the bench of a backfield everyone said
didn't exist. **46.5 points** of daylight. The biggest gap in the league.

That's the thing about grades, isn't it? You can't eat a grade.

---

## Station Three: Aaron 134, Sean 109

Now THIS is how you run a shootout.

**Josh Allen, 38 points.** Tied for the best single performance of the entire
week, and I'd frame it. Aaron — the man who, and I want everyone to hear this,
**drafted two kickers** — builds a **134** out of Allen plus **Jalen Coker 25**
(where did HE come from?), **Amon-Ra St. Brown 19**, **Chris Olave 12**, and
a pair of elevens from **Chase Brown** and **Juwan Johnson**. Balanced. Hot.
Out the door on time.

Sean cooked. Genuinely cooked. **Derrick Henry 33** and **Jahmyr Gibbs 28**,
two top-eight performances in the same kitchen, **Chuba Hubbard 16**, the six
backs in a quarterback league doing exactly what he drafted them to do. But
the QB room "held together with tape and prayer" produced **Kirk Cousins 15**,
and only eight names counted for him all night. **109 points** and a beating.

You can have the best protein in the building, sweetheart. If half the stove
isn't lit, the plate goes out cold.

---

## Station Four: Matt 130.5, Mike 120

The man with **ZERO TIGHT ENDS** just won a football game, and I need a
moment.

**Bryce Young, 35.** Third-best dish of the week. Add **Christian Watson 25**,
**Jalen Hurts 21**, **Nico Collins 14**, **Denzel Boston 11**, and Matt's
**130.5** goes out the door without a single tight end in the building — and
somewhere across the dining room, Chris is sitting on a walk-in full of them.
Somebody make the trade before I do it myself.

Mike lost this by **10.5** with **D'Andre Swift at 33**, the fourth-best
performance in the league. **Justin Jefferson 24. Parker Washington 14.**
Twin fourteens from **Joe Burrow** and **Baker Mayfield**. And then the
kicker: **Cameron Dicker, 1 point.** One. A single, solitary point. Mike, if
Dicker gives you a normal night, we're in overtime and I'm ordering more wine.

---

## Station Five: Chris 111.5, Eli 91.5

The **D+** draft. The broken shape. Five tight ends and two receivers and
everybody laughing at the mise en place.

Chris won by **20**.

**Dallas Goedert 22. Trey McBride 14. Tyler Warren 8.** Three tight ends,
forty-four points, and I don't care if it looks mad on paper — it went out
hot. **Jared Goff 16**, **Cam Skattebo 14**, **Malik Willis 14**, **Evan
McPherson 9.5**, **San Francisco 49ers Defense 5**. A **111.5** assembled out
of spare parts and spite.

Eli — the **B+**, the man who "bet on 2028" — got **Jaxson Dart 24**, **Kyle
Monangai 17**, **Dak Prescott 16**, and **Pittsburgh Steelers Defense 8**, and
still finished on **91.5**. The future might be gorgeous, love. Tonight it's
a lot of promising ingredients still in the fridge.

---

## Station Six: Bruce 91, Mitch 83.5

The only plate in the building that came down to the last ticket. **7.5
points.** The week's sole single-digit finish.

Bruce — remember the guilt? Passed on nine first-round quarterbacks and then
panic-bought Nix, Shough and Watson? **Tyler Shough, 30 points.** Sixth-best
performance in the league. Add **Bijan Robinson 20**, **Deshaun Watson 11**,
a pair of eights from **Jeremiyah Love** and **Aaron Jones Sr.**, and Bruce
scrapes home on **91** — tenth in scoring, and **1-0**.

Mitch had **Trevor Lawrence 26** and **Lamar Jackson 22** and **Breece Hall
17** and still only managed **83.5** from six names. Two enormous
quarterbacks, and the rest of the plate is **Kyren Williams 8**, **Tyler Bass
7.5**, **Tee Higgins 3**. Mitch, you built the sauce and forgot the dish.

(3rd), **Matt 130.5** (4th), **Mike 120** (5th, and losing), **Chris 111.5**
(6th), **Sean 109** (7th), **Pete 104.5** (8th and unbeaten), **Eli 91.5**
(9th), **Bruce 91** (10th and unbeaten), **Mitch 83.5** (11th), and **Cal 58**

Six of you leave on **W1**, six on **L1**. Dan, Aaron, Matt, Chris, Pete and

Head chef of the week goes to **Caleb Williams, 38**, with **Josh Allen, 38**
sharing the toque. Two perfect plates. The rest of you — WAKE UP.
