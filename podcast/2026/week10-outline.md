# The PFL Cast — Week 10 Episode Outline (Week 11 look-ahead)

**Hosts:** Peyton Manning & John Madden
**Runtime:** ~10 minutes
**Tone:** affectionate roast. League owners should laugh, not wince.

---

## Notes for the show writer

### Hosts

**Peyton Manning**
- Film-study obsessive. He "watched the tape" on everything, even fantasy box scores, and brings a binder.
- Yells "Omaha!" before changing the subject. That's how this show moves between segments.
- Self-deprecating, with dry timing.
- Turns any moment into a fake commercial read.
- League owner **Eli** shares his little brother's name, and Peyton can't leave it alone. He's proud of Eli, then immediately undercuts him.

**John Madden**
- "BOOM!" Pure enthusiasm.
- States the obvious as deep wisdom: "The team that scores more points usually wins."
- Describes telestrator drawings out loud, full of squiggles and arrows that make no sense.
- Loves tough guys and players who carry a load.
- Goes on tangents about the Madden Cruiser bus, refusing to fly, and turkey legs.

**Together:** Peyton over-prepares and Madden goes off-script. Every segment needs at least one real disagreement between them. They also have running bits that build across the episode:
- Madden keeps restating his "more points wins" rule. The league keeps proving him wrong. By the end he refuses to give it up.
- Peyton's binder keeps getting thicker.

### Style
- Lead with stories, not stats. Use no more than 2–3 numbers per segment. Don't run through every game.
- Talk about owners by first name, and avoid guessing pronouns for them.
- Roast lightly: nobody gets hit twice.
- Keep segments connected. When a story touches the next one, use that as the handoff.

### Never say
- "Should have started / benched / sat" anyone. In this league the lineup sets itself automatically from the best scorers.
- Waivers, FAAB, projections, or "favorite/underdog by X points."
- Any Week 11 results. Those games haven't finished.
- Any statistic that isn't in this outline. If a number isn't here, talk around it.

## 1. Cold open (~0:30)
- Open in the middle of an argument. Madden is laying down his law: "The team that scores the most points wins. That's football. That's LIFE."
- Peyton slides one name across the desk: Sean. Biggest score in the league this week, and still 4-6.
- Madden is stunned into silence, briefly.
- Peyton: "Omaha!" Welcome to The PFL Cast.

## 2. "Madden's Law is broken": Sean (~2:00)
- Sean is 2nd in the whole league in total points, put up a personal best for the season (157.5), and is still 4-6.
- Peyton has "studied the tape" and concludes the offense is fine and the schedule is cursed. He compares it to throwing for 400 yards and losing anyway, and admits he did that a lot.
- Madden won't accept it. Either the law holds, or football is broken.
- **Debate:** Is Sean a sleeping giant about to wreck somebody's playoff hopes, or just snakebit?
- **Look-ahead:** Sean plays Chris this week. Chris has McCaffrey, who just had the best game of anyone in the league. Madden: "Now THAT'S a tough guy."

## 3. "The Brother Bit": Eli, plus "Eli's guy" Aaron (~2:00)
- Eli has won two straight after a rough start. Prescott (39) and Pickens (33) both had their best games of the year.
- Peyton is the proud big brother: "That's my guy." Then he undercuts it right away: still 3-7.
- **The handler joke:** In this league, Aaron is allowed to send in Eli's roster moves. Peyton: "Every little brother needs somebody handling his paperwork. I know a thing or two about that."
- **The turn:** Meanwhile Aaron has lost three straight, and last week's score would have beaten half the league. Madden's theory: Aaron is spending too much time running Eli's team. "You can't drive two buses at once!"
- **Debate:** Peyton says Aaron is just unlucky. Madden says it's divided attention.
- **Look-ahead:** Eli plays Mike, and Eli's injury list keeps growing (Puka Nacua is out until mid-December, and TreVeyon Henderson just went on IR). Aaron plays a banged-up Cal (Jordan Addison, Kyler Murray and Jake Ferguson are all out). Aaron's slump-breaker may have arrived.

## 4. "David vs Goliath": Mitch vs Joe (~2:00)
- Mitch had a personal worst for the season: 45 points. Trevor Lawrence scored more than half of that by himself.
- Madden draws a telestrator picture of Lawrence carrying the entire team on his back, up a hill, in the snow, "and nobody's blocking for him!"
- This week Mitch faces Joe, who has the most points in the league and shares the best record (7-3).
- Peyton draws up a slingshot game plan for Mitch. Madden says Goliath also had the most points, which proves Madden's Law, until Peyton reminds him how the story ends.

## 5. Awards: The Turkey Leg (~1:30)
Thanksgiving is next week, so Madden hands out his famous turkey legs.

- **Turkey Leg (hero of the week):** Christian McCaffrey, 41 points for Chris. It's the best game of the week and nearly double his previous season high. Peyton makes a binder-backed pitch for Prescott. Madden overrules him on the grounds that "running backs eat turkey, quarterbacks eat salad."
- **Cold Gravy (affectionate roast):** Matt, after the roughest week of Matt's season (50.5). Give it one good joke and move on.
- **Winning Ugly (honorable mention):** Pete. Pete keeps winning without putting up big scores, sits 7-3 and leads the division. Peyton respects it ("a win's a win"). Madden calls it a second violation of Madden's Law in one episode.

## 6. Week 11 picks, with stakes (~2:00)
Each host makes **one lock and one bold call**. Peyton picks with his head, Madden with his gut. They argue at least once.
- **Mitch vs Joe:** the bottom team against the top team. Peyton flirts with the upset. Madden picks by Madden's Law.
- **Sean vs Chris:** do Sean's points finally turn into a win?
- **Dan vs Pete:** can Pete win ugly again, or does Dan cool him off?
- **The wager:** whoever goes worse on picks has to ride the Madden Cruiser to next week's Thanksgiving episode. If Peyton loses, he has to do the whole ride without his binder.
- Running bit: "We'll check the receipts next week."

## 7. Sign-off (~0:20)
- Peyton does a fake sponsor read, maybe for turkey legs.
- Madden climbs aboard the bus. "Happy Thanksgiving, everybody. More points wins. BOOM!"
- Peyton gets the last word, under his breath: "...usually."
