# The PFL Cast — Episode 1 (Week 10): Points Don't Pay Rent

**On the show:** Peyton Manning, John Madden · **Runtime:** ~11 minutes (the first episode: includes a launch intro) · **Tone:** affectionate roast

## Notes for the show writer

- **Peyton:** Has a binder for the debate itself, with tabs labeled "Wins" and "Also Wins." Dry and self-deprecating. Yells "Omaha!" whenever Madden is winning an exchange. Slips into fake commercial reads, for example for a "Win-Loss Column Premium Ledger." Can't stop teasing his "little brother" Eli about the league owner who shares the name.
- **Madden:** "BOOM!" for every big number. States the obvious like scripture ("The team with more points has more points"). Telestrator doodles of a scoreboard that keeps getting bigger. Loves McCaffrey as a tough guy. Tangents about the Madden Cruiser and turkey legs.
- **How they play off each other:** One argument runs the whole show. Madden says points are what count. Peyton says only wins count. Every segment gets dragged back to it.
- **Running bit, the Debate Scoreboard:** After each segment, Madden gives himself a "point" and Peyton gives himself a "win," and they fight over which one counts. By the end, Madden has piled up points and Peyton has more wins, and neither will concede. The scoreboard ends up as backwards as Sean's season.
- **Never say:**
  - "started," "benched," "sat," or "starting lineup." The scoring lineup fills itself from the best scorers.
  - Waivers, FAAB or projections. Roster moves are IR moves, supplemental moves and trades.
  - Any Week 11 result. Week 11 has not been played.
  - Any number not in the digest. Talk around it instead.
  - "Commissioner" for Joe. He is the league's scorekeeper.
  - Preseason or draft grades, or anything from a previous season.
  - Points against, in any wording.
  - Hypothetical or "all-play" records.
  - Anything about a defense scoring zero.
  - Busts or "nobody scored under 2."
  - Injury news. The designations reflect current status, not Week 10.
  - Made-up quotes from real owners. Describe them, never quote them.
  - Pronouns for owners. Use their names.
  - The "that's not an X, it's a Y" construction, in any wording.

## Launch intro: Welcome to The PFL Cast (~0:45)

This is the first episode ever, so open by announcing the show before the argument starts. Keep it quick and warm, and play it as a big occasion for these two.

- **Madden drives the welcome.** He makes it a grand opening: "This is the first-ever episode of The PFL Cast!" He calls in from the Madden Cruiser, or from the studio with the bus idling outside, and cuts a ribbon with a turkey leg. BOOM!
- **What the show is:** a weekly show about the PFL, the Peninsula Football League. Twelve owners, one league, every week of the season.
- **Who they are, in character:** Peyton has already prepared. He has a binder labeled "Episode 1" with tabs for every owner, and admits he's been studying for weeks. Madden admits he prepared by eating.
- **What listeners get every week:** the week's best stories (not a game-by-game recap), the awards, and each host's picks for next week. They'll come back and check the receipts on those picks.
- **The promise to the owners:** everyone gets roasted eventually, lovingly. Peyton: "If you're not mentioned this week, don't relax."
- **First disagreement before the show even starts:** they can't agree on the opening. Peyton wants a carefully rehearsed welcome. Madden has already started.
- **Handoff:** Peyton turns to the binder's first real tab, "The Argument," which leads straight into the cold open.

## 1. Cold open (~0:30)

- Peyton opens the binder to "The Argument" and announces the first episode's one topic: do points or wins matter?
- Madden: "Points! BOOM!" Peyton: wins.
- They agree to keep score of the argument itself. They immediately disagree about whether to score it in points or wins.
- **Handoff:** Peyton: "Exhibit A is Sean."

## 2. Storyline: Sean's 157.5 vs. Pete's 7-3 (~2:30)

- **Madden drives.** Sean put up 157.5, the week's high and Sean's best week of the season. Sean is still 4-6. Madden draws a giant telestrator circle around the number.
- Sean is second in the league in points, behind only Joe, and has the record of a team that loses its car keys every Sunday. Keep the roast gentle and affectionate: Sean gets this one roast and that's it.
- **Peyton counters with Pete.** Pete won with 105.5, a score that beat only 5 of the other 11 teams, and is 7-3. Peyton gives it a fake commercial read: "Pete's Team: winning without the mess of excess points."
- **The disagreement:** Madden says Pete is lucky and Sean is good. Peyton says the standings don't print "good," they print wins. Madden: "You can't eat a win." Peyton: "You can't eat a point either, John." That sets off a turkey leg tangent.
- **Scoreboard:** Madden gives himself a point and Peyton gives himself a win. Each says the other's doesn't count.
- **Handoff:** Madden: "Let me tell you about a tough guy."

## 3. Storyline: McCaffrey's 41 (~1:30)

- **Madden drives** with pure tough-guy worship. Christian McCaffrey put up 41, the week's top performance and his season high, in Chris's win over Bruce. Madden has a Cruiser story about a running back who "runs like he's late for the bus."
- **Peyton** pulls it back to the argument: Chris got a win out of it, so the win is the story.
- **The disagreement:** Madden says McCaffrey's points are the reason for the win, so points win again. Peyton: "Then it's a win. Thank you." Neither moves an inch.
- **Scoreboard:** Each claims the segment. Madden does a doodle showing that both of them won.
- **Handoff:** Peyton sighs: "Fine. Let's talk about Eli."

## 4. Storyline: Eli's best week (~1:45)

- **Peyton drives** and plays up the little-brother teasing. League owner Eli had a best-of-the-season 135.5 over Cal, with Dak Prescott at 39 and George Pickens at 33.
- Eli has now won two straight. Peyton is suspicious, flips through the binder, and treats it as a family emergency.
- **The twist:** Eli is still 3-7, so Peyton's own logic says the week barely matters. He hates admitting that.
- **The disagreement:** Madden happily takes Eli's side: "Big points, BOOM!" Peyton insists it's two wins that count, which accidentally makes his little brother look good. He yells "Omaha!" to escape.
- **Scoreboard:** Madden claims a point. Peyton reluctantly claims a win but files it under "Eli, unfortunately."
- **Handoff:** Madden: "Time for trophies!"

## 5. Awards & roast: "The Points vs. Wins Trophies" (~1:45)

Each owner gets roasted once, gently. Sean, Pete and Eli don't appear here.

- **The "Didn't Pay Rent" Award (Peyton's pick): Aaron.** Aaron lost with 114, a score that beat 6 of the other 11 teams. That's three straight losses. Peyton says it proves points don't pay rent. Madden says it proves the schedule is mean.
- **The Madden Cruiser Award for the longest trip (Madden's pick): Mike.** Mike beat Mitch by 72, the biggest margin of the week, behind Joe Burrow's 35. Madden insists Mike's points did it. Peyton notes, again, that it's one win.
- **The "One Man Band" Award: Mitch.** Mitch scored 45, a season low, and Trevor Lawrence carried most of it. Madden admires Lawrence as a tough guy hauling the whole bus up a hill. Keep it kind.
- **Honorable mention: Matt.** Matt had his worst week of the season and is still 6-3-1. Peyton loves it. Madden is offended by it.
- **Scoreboard:** They argue about whether the awards count as points or wins and settle on "both," which neither of them accepts.
- **Handoff:** Peyton: "Omaha! Picks."

## 6. Week 11 picks, with stakes (~1:30)

- **The bet that settles the show: Sean vs Chris.** Madden picks Sean, the second-highest scorer in the league. Peyton picks Chris on the record.
- **Stakes:** If Sean wins, Peyton has to read the whole binder as a turkey leg commercial. If Chris wins, Madden has to tear the scoreboard doodle off the telestrator himself.
- **Mitch vs Joe:** Mitch has the fewest points in the league and Joe has the most. Madden gives the obvious-wisdom pick. Peyton plays contrarian on principle and roots for Mitch "for the story." This is Joe's only real moment; don't force him into anything bigger.
- **Dan vs Pete:** Peyton takes Pete, who has won two straight and has the best record in the Even Division. Madden takes Dan, who has scored more points than Pete this season. This is the debate again, in miniature.
- **Mike vs Eli** as a quick-hit closer: Peyton can't bring himself to pick his "little brother," and Madden picks Eli just to annoy him.

## 7. Sign-off (~0:30)

- They read the final Debate Scoreboard. Madden leads in points, Peyton leads in wins, and both declare victory.
- Peyton realizes they've turned into Sean and Pete.
- Madden says the obvious one last time: "Whoever wins next week wins."
- Peyton closes the binder and ends on one last "Omaha!"
