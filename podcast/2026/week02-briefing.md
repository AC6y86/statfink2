- Opp avg faced (SOS): mean of the season scoring averages of the opponents played so far. Opp avg remaining: the same for the rest of the head-to-head schedule.
- Avg margin: mean of points for minus points against in head-to-head games.
- Weekly high scores: weeks in which the team had the league's top score (a tie for the top counts for each team). The weekly high score carries a prize.
- Manager lineup efficiency: NOT APPLICABLE. The lineup is automatic, so actual score = optimal score. Counted share is given instead.
- Last 3 avg and trend need at least 4 weeks; before that they would only repeat the season average.
